# Substack Strategy — Southern Legends (+ Venture Assessment)
*Documented June 5, 2026*

---

## The Short Answer

**Start a Substack for Southern Legends. Don't start one for anything else yet.**

---

## Why Southern Legends Is the Right Fit

Substack is purpose-built for exactly what Southern Legends is: intimate longform essays about real people, written for a loyal reader who wants more than a blog post. The platform has built-in discovery for narrative journalism, creative nonfiction, and regional storytelling. That's the audience.

What Substack gives Southern Legends that the blog alone doesn't:
- **Email subscriber list you own** — every subscriber is a direct relationship, not an algorithm
- **Substack Discovery** — readers already looking for this kind of writing can find it
- **Notes** — short fragments, quotes from interviews, behind-the-scenes moments. The blog can't do this. Notes is the connective tissue between essays.
- **Paid tier pathway** — at $5–7/month, even 200 paid readers = $1,000–$1,400/month. That's the writing income model that makes SL sustainable. Essays fund the book.
- **Community** — readers who comment and respond turn into the interview network. Someone who loves the Jean profile might know the next subject.

**The coexistence model:** southernlegends.blog stays for SEO and Google indexability. Substack is the subscriber home and distribution engine. Cross-post the full essay to both. Over time, Substack Notes carries the between-essay presence.

---

## How to Start

1. Create a free Substack at southernlegends.substack.com (or claim it — check if it's taken)
2. Import the existing 10 live profiles as archive posts
3. Start with free tier — gate nothing yet. Build the list first.
4. At 500 subscribers: introduce a paid tier with one paid-only piece per month (a fragment, an extended interview outtake, a behind-the-scenes note)
5. At 1,000 subscribers: paid is viable as a revenue line

## Venture-by-Venture Assessment

### Southern Legends — YES
The only venture where Substack is a direct fit. Essays + loyal readers + paid tier pathway + discovery = everything SL needs to grow without paid marketing.

### Plainspoken Blueprint — Wait
PB could eventually run a thought-leadership newsletter ("The Blueprint" — positioning/messaging insights for founders). But the content obligation is real, and the audience doesn't exist yet. Start it after the first paying coaching cohort exists and you have something to say that's earned from real client work. Not before.

### The Aisle — No
The Aisle is an event/platform business. Vendor and couple email sequences are better handled via Resend — transactional, event-driven, CRM-connected. A public newsletter doesn't fit the business model. What The Aisle needs is an email list, not a publication.

### Tend — Too Early
Not built yet. If Tend eventually has a pre-launch audience-building need, Substack could work (relationship/marriage content has strong Substack communities). File for later.

### Green Tomato — Maybe Later
Satire/commentary has a Substack audience. But the site is live and the content cadence isn't there yet. Don't start a second publication before SL is running.

## The One-Line Case

Substack turns the writing you're already doing into a publication with a subscriber list, a revenue model, and a discovery engine. For Southern Legends specifically, it's not an extra thing — it's the distribution layer the essays are missing.
