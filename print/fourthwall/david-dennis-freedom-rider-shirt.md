# David Dennis — Freedom Rider Shirt

## Product Concept

Unisex tee featuring the hedcut portrait of David Dennis from the Southern Legends Freedom Riders profile. Minimalist. Historically grounded.

## Design Assets

- Hedcut: `southern-legends/public/images/profiles/freedom-riders-national-monument/david-dennis-matt-headley-hedcut.webp`
- Style: hedcut portrait + "Freedom Rider" or "Anniston, 1961" text beneath
- Colors: white tee with black ink, or black tee with white/gold ink
