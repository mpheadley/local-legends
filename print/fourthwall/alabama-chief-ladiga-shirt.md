# Alabama / Chief Ladiga Trail Shirt

## Design Concept

Unisex tee. Three elements composed together:
1. **Outline of Alabama state** — clean line art
2. **Star on Anniston / Calhoun County** — marks the SL home base
3. **Chief Ladiga Trail illustration** — drawing of the trail or trail marker; Chief Ladiga's name as text element
4. **Southern Legends wordmark** — below or alongside

Visual feel: vintage map illustration meets trail marker. Earthy tones. Works on cream, forest green, or slate.

## Story Angle

Chief Ladiga Trail runs through the heart of Northeast Alabama — the same ground where Southern Legends profiles are set. Every vendor, pastor, farmer, and farmer's market regular on this site lives within a few miles of that trail. The shirt is a localism statement. Not "I'm from Alabama." More specific: "I'm from this particular corner of it."
