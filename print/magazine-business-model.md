# Southern Legends Magazine — Business Model
*Draft: 2026-06-05*

## Format
- **Print:** Short run, 24–32 pages, saddle-stitched, 8.5×11
- **Digital:** PDF download on southernlegends.blog + email to subscribers

## Issue 1 Target
- 3–4 profiles (Aquality, Jean Ellison, Interfaith, 1 new)
- 1–2 essays (Digital Gym, No Shade)
- 8–10 ad slots

---

## Print Cost Estimates (Issue 1)

| Print Run | Est. Cost | Cost/Copy |
|---|---|---|
| 250 copies | $350–500 | $1.40–2.00 |
| 500 copies | $600–900 | $1.20–1.80 |
| 1,000 copies | $900–1,400 | $0.90–1.40 |

*Use MagCloud (magcloud.com) or Mixam for short runs. Mixam quotes online instantly.*

**Recommendation:** Start at 500. Enough to distribute meaningfully, not so many you're stuck with boxes.
