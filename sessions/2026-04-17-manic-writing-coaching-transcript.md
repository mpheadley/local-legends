## Summary

**Session Focus:** Which Southern Legends piece to write next — the manic writing, the moment before the crash, or the month in the camper after discharge.

**Decision:** Matt will write about the manic writing (Patreon posts, texts, social media from the manic episode).

**Emotional Center Identified:** Confused and betrayed by yourself — the gap between feeling like you'd finally arrived at health and integration, while actually unraveling without knowing it.

**Key Scenes in Draft:** 
- Porch at sunset with family, fireflies, Soren explaining dad's happiness as "he was depressed, he took meds, now he's better"
- Heather watching and questioning his groundedness while he laughs and jokes
- The contradiction between beauty and wrongness happening simultaneously

**Critical Question for Therapist:** Is the impulse to write and share this publicly part of recovery, or is it itself a manic pattern worth watching?

**Next Step:** Discuss with therapist before continuing, then return to the artifacts (Patreon posts, social media) to ground the piece in direct quotes showing that voice of arrival and confidence.
