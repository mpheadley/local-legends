# Southern Legends — Marketing Plan
*Updated: 2026-07-03*

## Current Status
11 profiles published at southernlegends.blog. Podcast live on Spotify and Apple Podcasts. 0 paid subscribers. Writing pace: 1 profile every 2 weeks.

## 90-Day Goal
5 new profiles published + 100 email subscribers by Oct 1, 2026.

## Content Pillars
1. The profiles themselves — published at southernlegends.blog, announced everywhere
2. "How I found this story" — behind-the-scenes field notes on sourcing, reporting, the road
3. Alabama history and place-based storytelling — context posts that connect profiles to larger state history

## Weekly Content Cadence
| Day | Content type | Platform |
|-----|-------------|----------|
| Profile publish day (every 2 weeks) | Profile announcement — teaser quote card + link | Email list + Facebook + Instagram |
| Profile publish day | Substack note — one paragraph, the sentence that made me stop | Substack |
| Between profiles (weekly) | Field note — 200 words, one specific moment from reporting or research | southernlegends.blog + email |
| Between profiles | Quote card from any existing profile — pull one line, design a card | Instagram + Facebook |

## Email List Growth Tactic
Reader magnet: the unpolished first chapter of the book — honest, behind-the-scenes, not the polished version that will go to publishers. Offered on every profile page below the byline and in every email footer. Landing page: southernlegends.blog/chapter-one. No paywall, just email.

## Automation to Build This Month
New profile publish triggers an auto-email to the list via Resend. Webhook fires from the CMS (or a git push hook on the southernlegends repo). Email includes: profile headline, one teaser quote, one image, and a "Read the full profile" button. Keeps the list in the loop without Matt manually drafting a separate email after every publish.

## Key Metric
Email list size (target: 100 subscribers by Oct 1, 2026).
