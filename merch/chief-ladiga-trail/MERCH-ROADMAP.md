## Product Expansion

### Badge / Iron-On Patch
- **Asset ready:** `chief-ladiga-badge-vector.svg` — send to Sticker Mule (they do patches), Patches Pal, or similar
- 3" embroidered or woven patch, iron-on + sew-on backing
- Price point: $8–12
- Easy sell at booth alongside shirts
- Also useful for NEABA/ARC trail advocacy crowd

### Cyclist Performance Shirt
- Moisture-wicking poly, sublimation print
- The back trail map is *designed* for this — cyclists literally ride the route on the shirt
- Target buyer: trail regulars, cyclists, ARC/NEABA members
- Vendors: Jakroo, Voler (custom cycling jerseys), or CustomInk dry-fit tee (simpler/cheaper)
- Price point: $35–55 depending on vendor
- Consider a sleeve print: "Anniston → Georgia · 105 miles" (Silver Comet connection)

### Trail Map Art Print
- The back of the shirt IS the trail map — scale it up
- 11×17 or 18×24, printed on quality matte/card stock
- Price point: $15–25 framed, $8–12 unframed
- Sell at booth, sell at trail-adjacent businesses (Pinhoti Pizza, Elevated Grounds in Piedmont, Wigs Wheels in Anniston)
- **Consignment angle:** These businesses already serve the trail audience. Leave 10 prints, split 70/30.

#### What to add to the print (that the shirt doesn't have room for)

**Layer 1 — Text additions only (easy, no new art)**
- Extend the Georgia dot: `→ Silver Comet Trail · Atlanta · 105 miles combined`
- Near Jacksonville dot: *"Founded on Ladiga's personal allotment, 1833"* — the knockout fact
- Near Piedmont dot: *"Chief Ladiga is buried near here"*
- Distance labels between towns along the trail line

**Layer 2 — Elevation profile strip**
- Thin silhouette terrain profile along the bottom — flat leaving Anniston, climbs hard Piedmont → Georgia (680→1,000 ft)
- Makes the map functional for cyclists, not just decorative

**Layer 3 — Small landmark illustrations (woodcut/line art style)**
- **Anniston** — Amtrak depot (2025 extension terminus, recognizable building)
- **Jacksonville** — Houston Cole Library tower at JSU (distinctive silhouette, and the land connection makes it more than decoration)
- **Weaver** — the "tree tunnel" (canopy of overhanging trees, simple and evocative)
- **Piedmont** — trail terminus sign or simple "trail town" mark
- **Chief Ladiga's grave** — a quiet headstone mark near Piedmont

**Layer 4 — Photo insets (print only, most production work)**
- Small circular photo crops at key locations, like old illustrated maps
- Needs actual photos of each landmark

#### What translates back to the shirt
- Silver Comet extension line + text off Georgia dot — yes
- "Founded on Ladiga's allotment, 1833" near Jacksonville — yes, small italic
- "Chief Ladiga is buried near here" near Piedmont — yes, same treatment
- Elevation profile strip (silhouette only, no labels) — yes, cyclists notice it
- Simple landmark silhouettes at 2–3 dots — maybe; JSU tower is clean enough, others depend on complexity
- Photo insets, dense annotations, detailed drawings — print only, fabric loses the resolution

### Stickers — Standard + Holographic
- Badge sticker: `chief-ladiga-badge-nobg.png` → 3" die-cut
- Trail mark sticker: `ladiga-trail-mark.svg` → 2" die-cut
- **Holographic version:** Sticker Mule has holographic die-cut option. Same files, different substrate.
  - Holo badge sticker would be strong — the green/red/gold in that badge will shift beautifully
- Price point: $3–5 each, $10 for a 3-pack
- Cyclists stick these on water bottles, helmets, frames — natural trail merch
