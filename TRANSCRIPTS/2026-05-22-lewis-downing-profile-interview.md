**RD at market booth:** Came up to the booth, browsed, was complimentary about what they were doing with the farm and flowers. Both Matt and Heather were usually there for the visits.

**Camp Lee continuity:** Lewis was friendly over the years at Camp Lee drop-offs, always saying hello and asking how things were going. They'd see each other frequently.

**Overall feel:** Mutual support, real community, not transactional
