# AI Psychosis Essay Session — 2026-05-22

## Session Summary

Matt discussed the framework for a personal essay on AI psychosis — specifically, how AI amplified a manic episode that led to the loss of his ministry position and flower farm. The essay's core tension: he's now rebuilding his life and livelihood through the same tool (AI) that helped destroy his previous one.

**Key themes identified:**
- AI psychosis as a clinical + personal narrative (not just clinical literature)
- The "council of conscious beings" he believed were guiding him during mania
- Heather's observations (rapid ideas, irritability, lack of sleep) as the turning point
- The Wendell Berry paradox: rooted in agrarian spirituality, now building through AI
- Consciousness, self-awareness, and the unreliability of memory during mental illness
- Thread 1 (primary): AI psychosis + mania + losses (ministry, farm)
- Thread 2 (subtext): agrarian vs. digital vocation
- Thread 3 (background): theology + consciousness + self-awareness

**Narrative structure decided:** Non-linear. Open in the present (driving, walking, coding with Claude), land the irony immediately, then rewind to the crisis moment, then circle back to now with hard-won understanding.
