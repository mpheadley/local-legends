## Foothills Magazine — 16-Page Spread (TIME SENSITIVE)

**Deadline: July 9, 2026**
**Contact for pricing: Ginna** (Foothills/Anniston Star ad sales — get number from Lisa Davis)
**Distribution: Foothills delivery van** — already covers the region

This is a major opportunity. A 16-page spread in Foothills reaches:
- Calhoun County homes and businesses
- The exact demographic: 25–45 women, homeowners, community-oriented
- A regional publication with built-in trust

### How to Get a Discount

**Option A — Content trade**
Offer to write 2–4 story-quality pages of editorial content (SL profiles or Aisle profiles) in exchange for a rate reduction. Lisa already knows your writing. This is a trade they'll value — original local content is hard to get.

**Option B — Sponsorship quid pro quo**
If Foothills / Anniston Star runs a story on The Aisle expo, offer them a media partner listing + logo on all expo materials + tickets for staff. Reduces the cash ask in exchange for earned media value.

**Option C — Split the spread**
Half SL (editorial/profiles), half Aisle (expo promotion). Present it as a joint "Alabama stories + Alabama weddings" editorial. Foothills gets strong content, you get half the space at full rate for less total cash.

**Option D — Barter ad space**
The Aisle expo program is a printed piece distributed at the October expo. Offer Foothills a full-page ad in the expo program in exchange for a rate reduction on the 16-page spread. Direct exposure to 400+ couples = high-value ad placement for them.

**Recommended ask:** "I'd love to do a content trade — I'll write [X pages of editorial] in exchange for a rate discount. What would that look like?" That's the easiest yes for them. Then layer in the expo program trade if needed.

### What 16 Pages Could Look Like

1. **Cover / opener** — The Aisle + SL combined brand intro (1 pg)
2. **SL Profiles** — 3–4 profiles (6–8 pgs)
3. **Aisle expo preview** — Oct 18, venue, what to expect (2 pgs)
4. **Meet the vendors** — 6–8 Aisle vendors featured (3–4 pgs)
5. **Back page** — giving model, UWECA, Imagination Library CTA (1 pg)

## Print Specs

### Southern Legends Postcard (4×6)
**Front:** A strong profile photo + "Southern Legends — Stories of Alabama People" + southernlegends.blog
**Back:** One pull quote from a recent profile + "Free to read. Free to share." + URL
**Print run:** 250 cards
**Printer:** MOO (premium) or GotPrint (budget) — ~$60–$90 for 250

### The Aisle Postcard (4×6)
**Front:** Expo hero image + "Anniston Bridal Expo · October 18, 2026" + theaisle.app
**Back:** 3-line value prop ("Meet 30+ local vendors. One afternoon. Anniston Museums & Gardens.") + GA/VIP pricing + QR code
**Print run:** 250 cards
**Printer:** Same as above

### Aisle Brochure (trifold, 4×9 folded)
**Panel 1:** Cover — "Your wedding. Your way." + expo date + theaisle.app
**Panel 2:** What is The Aisle / vendor categories
**Panel 3:** Expo details — date, venue, tickets, vendor application
**Inside panels:** Vendor categories, couple benefits, giving model
**Print run:** 100–250 copies
**Printer:** GotPrint or Canva Print — ~$80–$140 for 250
**Leave-behind for:** Chamber of Commerce, Called Coffee, business owner tables
