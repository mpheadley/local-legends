What the Tornado Left

Otter Creek Farmstead · Frank & Jane Brown, Ohatchee

April 27, 2011: a tornado destroyed 2,200 hardwoods. They rebuilt from 2013. The clearcut accidentally became ideal quail habitat. Garden & Gun named it one of 25 Dream Trips of a Lifetime. Farm + distillery + agritourism + wedding venue.
