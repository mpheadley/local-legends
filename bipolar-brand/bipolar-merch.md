# Bipolar Merch — Ideas & Research
*April 16, 2026*

---

## The Gap

- r/bipolar: 300K members, active community
- Everything on Etsy/Redbubble/Amazon is cheap POD — "Bipolar Badass," clipart ribbons, stock fonts on Gildan tees
- One elevated brand exists: BPB Wear (Dublin) — charitable model, artist collabs, but still graphic-tee territory
- No one is doing editorial typography, restrained palette, quality blanks in this category
- "Bipolar pride" as a framing is contested in the community — worth knowing before naming anything
- ~7 million US adults have bipolar disorder (2.8%)

**The gap is real. Nothing exists at the intersection of elevated design and this diagnosis.**

---

## Market map

**Saturated — stay out:**
- "Bipolar Warrior" / "Bipolar Badass" — motivational poster energy
- Green ribbon awareness merch
- Lightning bolt imagery
- Loud colors, stock fonts, Gildan tees
- Redbubble/TeePublic commodity designs

**Demand with no good supply — this is the lane:**
- Elevated, understated design for people who are done performing their diagnosis
- Insider humor that doesn't announce itself
- Merch that looks like something you'd wear because *you* like it, not because you want people to ask about it
- The sine wave with no text — recognition object, not billboard

The people in r/bipolar who have taste are buying nothing right now because nothing good exists.
