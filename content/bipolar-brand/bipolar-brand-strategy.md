*April 16, 2026*

## The POD ceiling

POD margins are thin — $8–12 on a $35 tee. Scales passively but slowly. It's a starting point, not a destination.

---

## What broader looks like

### Short term — low risk, start here
- **POD** — test what resonates before investing in inventory
- **Substack or paid newsletter** — people pay for the essays, merch is secondary
- **Speaking** — mental health panels, NAMI events, church communities. A former pastor who went manic and rebuilt. That's a talk.

### Medium term — if an audience shows up
- **Print runs on quality blanks** (Bella+Canvas, AS Colour) — better margins, better product, requires inventory
- **Wholesale** — mental health orgs, therapy offices, NAMI chapters
- **A book** — the essays are already accumulating. Easter piece, the 30-minute errand, the farm. That's a manuscript.

### Longer term — if the story gets traction
- **Community** — paid membership, Discord, something for people who are rebuilding
- **Partnerships** — mental health brands, therapists, platforms like NAMI or DBSA
- **Courses or workshops** — writing as recovery

---

## The throughline

The story is the asset. Merch, speaking, writing, community — all downstream of people trusting the voice. POD is the easiest first test but it's not the ceiling.

## Neurodivergent market research (April 16, 2026)

**Key subreddits:**
- r/ADHD — 1.5M+ members, most active, strong identity pride culture
- r/autism — 500K–700K, identity-first, protective of community norms
- r/AutisticWithADHD — 82K, fast-growing, AuDHD identity is its own strong subculture
- r/neurodivergent — smaller, lower activity; the umbrella framing is more popular on TikTok/Instagram than Reddit

**Merch culture:** Active and growing, driven by ADHD/AuDHD TikTok. Enamel pins, stickers, text-based tees ("late diagnosed," "AuDHD"), sensory-safe clothing. Etsy is primary marketplace.

**Elevated brands:** Almost none. ND Renegade is the most polished — clean black-and-white, sensory-friendly, cause-driven — but still reads as a small cause brand, not a design-forward lifestyle label. Same white space as bipolar: nobody doing it with real typographic care and editorial aesthetic.

**Neurodivergent vs. bipolar:**
- Neurodivergent is larger and more saturated — broader umbrella, stronger TikTok identity-pride culture
- Bipolar merch is thinner — mostly one-off Redbubble, less identity-pride, more illness-acknowledgment
- Neurodivergent has a clearer cultural identity to sell against

**Strategic implication:** Your story is bipolar. Your audience is neurodivergent. Lead with the specific story, speak to the wider community.

## Wendell Berry angle

Berry writes about rootedness, place, slow living, land, community. Matt used Berry heavily in preaching and farm work — it's not a borrowed reference, it's a native language. That authenticity matters.

Doesn't belong in the bipolar/neurodivergent brand. Fits these better:
- **Heather Florals / Rooted Weekend Co.** — "the care of the earth" direction, farm-to-market aesthetic
- **Southern Legends** — tote, print, or pullquote treatment
- **Matt's preaching-to-writing piece** — Berry as through-line between the two vocations

Berry quotes worth pulling when ready:
- "It may be that when we no longer know what to do, we have come to our real work."
- "Do unto those downstream as you would have those upstream do unto you."
- "The care of the Earth is our most ancient and most worthy responsibility."
