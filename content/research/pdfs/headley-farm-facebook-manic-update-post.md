# Facebook Post — Manic-Era Farm Update

**Source:** Matt Headley, Facebook (public post)
**Date:** Approximate — post-resignation from ministry, pre-trail-grand-opening. Likely late 2024 or early 2025.
**Filed:** 2026-04-25
**Use in:** CLT essay (confirms sheep creep, trail shop concept, mania period) + Farm essay (primary source for manic scaling phase)

---

Friends, behold... an UPDATE: 🤪We've been in wide-open, balls-to-the-wall hustle mode over at Headley Flower Farm 👨🏻‍🌾👩🏻‍🌾 — pivoting out of full-time pastoring and scaling up the farm and flower biz just in time for the spring bloom explosion. 💐💥

Right now, it's all hands on deck — we're out here cutting privet, briars, Bradford pears, and old fallen cypress trees 😤 trying to clear a new perimeter fencerow around the farm.

We've had some amazing friends show up with chainsaws and work gloves, and it's been such a gift. If you're local and feel like sweating out some stress or trading a gym workout for a little community farm therapy, hit me up. 💪😅

The fence isn't just for looks — we've had a parade of trespassers lately: deer, stray dogs (bless 'em), and one persistent guy I've now dubbed "the sheep creep" 👀 — keeps showing up from the trail to "admire" our sheep… and lets his dog chase them all over the place 😱

So yeah — boundaries are a thing now. 😂

We're also working on a trailside gateway for the soon-to-open Chief Ladiga Trail south extension — dreaming up a connector "node" with a little trailside flower stand, a bike pump station, art installations, and some space for local business shoutouts. We're calling it "The Front Porch" (more name suggestions?) — tucked right behind Walmart curbside in Lenlock, near the new Starbucks + McClellan Summerall project. 🚴‍♂️🌻

Long-term, we're building toward something we're calling the Wholehearted Homestead — a working flower farm, event space, and regenerative agri-community rooted in beauty, healing, and connection.💚

And today is Sunday…

I'm not pastoring anymore, but that doesn't mean I don't feel the pull to slow down, sit in the back, and just receive.

If there's a local church you love, or a pastor who could use some encouragement, drop it in the comments. I might swing by with a few flowers and a full heart. 💒

Thanks for all the love, prayers, sweat, and kind words y'all have shared with us lately. As someone once told me when I was clawing my way out of depression:

"You have no idea how many people are in your corner."

Still hits.

Your friendly neighborhood spiritualAF, lip-syncing worm farmer,
— Matt
✌️💚🐑💐
