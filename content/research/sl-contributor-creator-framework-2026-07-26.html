SL Contributor & Creator Framework

2026-07-26 · Source: session research + profile drafts · For: Matt's use only
Every person and organization profiled on Southern Legends is a potential creator, not just a subject. All satire contributions must go through the satire platform's anonymity protocols; GT/TPT anonymity is not disclosed to contributors.
