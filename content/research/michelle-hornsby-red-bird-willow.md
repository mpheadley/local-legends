## Interview prep (scaffold — to be filled after research)

Categories of questions, not specific questions yet. Specific questions come after Matt has done the basic research and knows what's actually at Red Bird Willow.

### Origin
- How Red Bird Willow started. What was she doing before. The first year — what worked, what didn't.

### What it actually is
- A walk through the property / operation. Day in the life. What animals, what crops, what spaces. What's the work *actually* like.

### Why this
- The "what kept you going" question (per CLAUDE.md editorial lens). Not asked directly; built up to. Frame: what almost stopped you, what made you keep showing up.

### Community
- Customers, neighbors, partners. Who else is part of the world Red Bird Willow exists in. The Pleasant Valley / Calhoun County / NE Alabama context.

### The sheep (one or two questions only — this isn't the frame)
- What it was like taking them. How they've changed her operation. The quiet version, not the dramatic one.

### Future
- What she's building toward. Five-year horizon. What success would look like.

**Do not ask:** anything that flattens her into a representative of "homesteading" or "small farms" generally. The profile is about Michelle and Red Bird Willow specifically.
