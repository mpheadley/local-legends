📢 ANNOUNCEMENT: Transitioning into a New Season

Dear friends,

I wanted to take a moment to personally share some news about an important transition in my life. While an official announcement has already gone out at church, I want to express my own gratitude and reflections on this next season.

After much prayer and discernment, I have made the decision to step away from my pastoral role at Anniston First United Methodist Church. This has been a deeply meaningful journey, and I am incredibly grateful for the relationships, the support, and the memories we have shared.

Heather and I have felt the love and generosity of this community in profound ways. We are stepping forward into new opportunities, trusting that God is leading us into an exciting and faith-filled chapter. Our family will be focusing on the work ahead—our farm, deepening our faith, and exploring new ways to serve.

I want to thank each and every one of you who have reached out to express your support and encouragement. Your kindness means the world to us. While I will no longer be serving on staff, I remain deeply committed to nurturing the relationships that matter most. I would love to stay connected in the ways that make sense for each of us.

Thank you for your prayers, your love, and your ongoing support. We are excited for what's next, and we carry all of you with us into this new chapter.

With gratitude,
Matt Headley 💚🙏✌🏼

## Research notes

- **Tone gap:** The public post says "after much prayer and discernment." The backstory (manic-episode.md) says he resigned by text, on the day he posted to Instagram, while in a paranoid state. The gap between the public language and the private reality is the story.
- **"Our family will be focusing on the work ahead — our farm"** — this is the hinge. The farm becomes the full-time project immediately after resignation.
- **No mention of mania** — the post reads as a graceful, chosen transition. That's the version the congregation got.
- **"exploring new ways to serve"** — manic idealism, but also genuinely held at the time.
- Cross-reference: Shannon Jenkins profile — "I resigned while I was manic. I didn't know it yet."
