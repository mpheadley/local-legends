## The Short Version

Mayor Ciara (Sierra) Smith-Roston visited the Headley Flower Farm in May 2025 while Matt was manic. She stood in the field, heard the vision for the Chief Ladiga Trail Porch, said *"I can see it. Y'all's vision is beautiful and powerful."* Then the farm ended. She became mayor. Her husband is Jay Roston (former detective) — not Jay Jenkins. Jay Jenkins is a separate person: former Ward 1 councilman and architect, who also happened to be a longtime flower customer.

That is the profile.

## The Web of Connections

### Jay Jenkins → Heather → Sierra
**Note: Jay Jenkins ≠ Jay Roston. These are two different people.**

- **Jay Jenkins** (Ward 1 councilman 2010–2024, architect) was a weekly flower patron at the Headley Flower Farm farmers market booth for 4 years.
- He hired Heather for his daughter Sonny's wedding — Heather's **first wedding**. December 14, 2024. White anemones, ranunculus, eucalyptus. Professional photos on file: `public/images/profiles/jay-jenkins/sonny-jenkins-*.webp`.
- June 2025: Jay Jenkins bought anniversary florals from Heather when the farm was actively going downhill. Matt delivered them to city hall. Jay Jenkins didn't know the context.
- Jay Jenkins was Sierra's **council colleague**, not her husband. He resigned Ward 1 in October 2024; Sierra was in the room.

- **Jay Roston** (Sierra's actual husband) — former detective. The "Roston" in Smith-Roston is his. They have a son, Jackson. No known connection to the flower farm.

### Sierra → The Farm Visit (~May 2025)
Full scene — use verbatim:

When she arrived, she told them to call her Sierra. She crouched down and gave Matt's daughter a hug before she looked at the field. They walked the rows together. Matt and Heather shared their vision for the Chief Ladiga Trail Porch.

She said:
> *"This is so peaceful out here. I can see it. Y'all's vision is beautiful and powerful."*

They gave her flowers before she left.

This was during the manic phase — the farm at its most alive. The walk-in cooler was in. The workshop was renovated. Dahlias and peonies in third-year prime. The Ladiga kiosk was being built. She came when it was everything it was ever going to be.

**The arc:** She said "I can see it." Then the farm ended. Then she became mayor.

### Sierra → Jay's Resignation Room (October 15, 2024)
Anniston Star photo caption (Bill Wilson, Oct 16, 2024):
*"Councilwoman Ciara Smith in the background"* — Jay is being hugged by councilwoman Millie Harris. Sierra is visible in the background.

She was already on the council when Jay resigned effective immediately at the end of what the Star called "a somewhat routine City Council meeting." The council was stunned. Jay didn't put it on the agenda. He waited until all routine business was done, then stepped down at the end of comments.

She was in that room when his seat became available.

### Lewis Downing → Jay's Seat → Sierra
After Jay resigned, Lewis Downing filled Ward 1. Lewis had been buying flowers from Heather at the farmers market for years. Heather watched him without knowing who he was.

Her words (Recording 45–46, 2026-05-20, confirmed quotable):
> *"I felt like he always saw me, like he would stop to talk to me, and even before I really knew who he was, I just, I would see how he would interact with people."*
> *"People of all social standing — not just people of importance."*

Heather noticed Lewis because he noticed her. She didn't know he would one day hold Jay's seat. Sierra went on to become mayor. All three — Jay, Lewis, Sierra — are in the same civic thread. Heather witnessed it from the flower booth without knowing she was witnessing it.

### The Bloom Bar → AMAG → Sierra's City
- The Bloom Bar: bought for $7,000 during the manic phase, drove to Orlando with Matt's son to get it. When the farm ended, it sat as a grief object. Heather was turned away at Oak Meadow ("not yet"). They kept it.
- Heather is now Program Manager at the Anniston Museum of Natural History (AMAG). She got the job while Matt was in UAB.
- The Aisle expo is at AMAG's Longleaf Event Center, 800 Museum Dr — free to The Aisle through Heather's role.
- The Bloom Bar is the centerpiece of The Aisle expo on October 18, 2026 — at the museum, in the city Sierra leads.
- Sierra is mayor of the city that owns the building where Heather works, where The Aisle will be, where the Bloom Bar will finally be used.

The farm didn't survive. Everything from the farm did.

## What the Profile Is About

Not her résumé. Not "first Black female mayor, youngest ever." Those are true but they're the Wikipedia version.

The profile is about: what does it mean to lead a city like Anniston at this moment? She knows what's in the soil here — the Freedom Riders bus burned on the outskirts of this city. Fort McClellan poisoned veterans. The Model City mythology was a story told by the people who owned it. She showed up to a flower farm during peak mania and said "I can see it." Now she's running the city.

**The underneath question:** What kept her going toward Anniston when the honest answer to "why here?" is complicated?

## Craft Note — Interview as Article

Matt's form for the Sierra interview when it happens:

You are already in the article when you walk in. The interview is the scene. Track: where does she sit, what does she do with her hands, the first thing she says that isn't an answer to a question, when she pauses. The pause is the real answer starting.

Don't open with the résumé. Open with something small. "The farm visit — what do you remember about that morning?" One question earns the next.

The article doesn't start at the beginning. It starts at the moment that already has all the meaning in it, and earns backward and forward from there.
