# Anniston Star: "Called Coffee Company of Anniston stirs up a partnership with a Peruvian family" — Sep 13, 2020
By Danielle Stallworth

## Full text

The story of Called Coffee Company of Anniston begins in an unlikely place: Junín, Peru.

Owner Jerod Snider and his wife, Hannah, were living there as missionaries when they met the Torre family at their church. Gregorio and Isabel Torre are coffee farmers, and they brought the Sniders some to try.

"I'd never had coffee that good," Jerod Snider said.

The Torres asked if the Sniders could help bring their coffee to the U.S., and thus began their collaboration.

"We'd decided that we were going to move back here, and that if we were going to help them sell coffee, we might as well do it ourselves." Snider said. "We started out importing their coffee and selling just the bags of roasted beans, then eventually got around to making drinks."

The menu at Called Coffee consists of basic coffee drinks, espresso-based drinks like cappuccinos and lattes, and flavored seasonal beverages. The current bestseller is the coconut mocha latte.

"We also usually have one non-coffee drink per season," Snider said. "Right now it's a plum spritzer. We use local plums from the farmers market to make the syrup out of."

The Sniders got started with their coffee truck in November, just working local festivals and weekend events. They were booked through the summer, but once the pandemic hit in March and those events got canceled, they had to switch up their plans. They started finding places around town to park the truck in the morning and posted their schedule and locations on their social media platforms.

"At first it started really slow, but it's starting to grow into something sustainable," Snider said. "Even though it was bad and hurt us at first, I think in the long run we'll look back on it as a good thing because it helped us expand our business."

"It's definitely not the business side of things. My favorite part is interacting with the customers and getting to know them. And hopefully being able to improve their day, whether it's through a coffee drink or a kind word."
