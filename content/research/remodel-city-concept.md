# Remodel City — Concept Notes

*Domains: remodel.city (expires May 5, 2026 — RENEW OR DROP), remodelcity.org*
*Name play on Anniston's "Model City" nickname*

---

## The Name

Anniston was planned and built in the 1870s as a "Model City" — a company town designed from scratch to demonstrate what an industrial city could be. It was literally named as a model for others to follow.

"Remodel City" does conceptual work the name alone can't:
- It implies the original model failed or aged out
- It implies active rebuilding, not nostalgia
- It's a critique and a proposal at the same time
- It's the reckoning-not-celebration frame built into the URL

---

## What It Could Be

### Option 1 — Living companion to the CLT book
The CLT bicentennial book (2032) needs a digital home before the book is finished. Remodel City could be where the research lives publicly — profiles, history, maps, oral histories — as a long-form web project that eventually becomes the book's online presence.

### Option 2 — Civic commentary platform
A NE Alabama publication with a specific editorial frame: what does it mean to rebuild a city that called itself a model? Covers downtown development, civic institutions, economic history, neighborhood change. Tone: serious but accessible. Not a news site — more like a long-form civic journal.

### Option 3 — Anniston narrative project
Profiles of people, places, and institutions doing the actual work of rebuilding — separate from Southern Legends (which is regional) and more specifically Anniston-focused. Could fold into SL or run alongside it.

### Option 4 — Satirical / Green Tomato crossover
"Remodel City" has a satirical register too. A city that bills itself as a model while dealing with PCB contamination, economic decline, and political dysfunction is inherently tragicomic. The name could support a darker-humor civic satire angle.

## What It Needs Before Building

- Clarity on whether it's a book companion, a publication, or a civic commentary site
- At least 5 pieces of original content ready before launch
- A clear editorial voice distinct from SL (more civic, less personal)
- Time — this is a 2027+ project at earliest

## Domain Decision

**Renew if:** The civic commentary / CLT book companion idea feels real and you'd build it in 2–3 years.
**Let drop if:** It's a neat idea but you have no bandwidth and four other parked domains already.

The .city TLD is the clever one. remodelcity.org is the backup — less elegant but serviceable.
