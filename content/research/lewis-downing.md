## Primary Sources

### Anniston Star — Spotlight, June 21, 2018
*By Faith Dorn, Special to The Star*
PDF: `~/Downloads/Spotlight on_ Lewis Downing, Downing & Sons _ Spotlight _ annistonstar.com.pdf`
URL: https://www.annistonstar.com/features/community/spotlight/spotlight-on-lewis-downing-downing-sons/article_dd821358-73ef-11e8-a8d9-b3d16bb119dd.html

Key sourced material (Lewis's direct words):
- Born in Birmingham, grew up in Anniston
- 10th Street Elementary, first graduating class at Sacred Heart High School
- UA then Jacksonville State, never finished degree
- Mother worked at The Anniston Star; loved the ink smell from the print shop
- "I grew up on Gurnee Avenue at the store. I did not know I would end up back there, but looking back, I think I am on the right course."
- Gets there at quarter of 8, puts out plants, does whatever needs doing
- Fabric buying: "We go out on the road and find fabrics. We find high quality fabrics at great values."
- Store history: grandmother wanted fabric/sewing notions, started alongside True Value after Mary Carter bankruptcy
- Dog Greta: found on a friend's farm at 3–4 weeks old, has been at the store every day since
- Advice: "Live easily and simply, if you can. Treat everyone fairly. Try to be a good person."
- Favorite film: The Last of the Mohicans. Favorite music: rock and roll, bluegrass, folk. Wanted to be a chef.

### Anniston Star — Downing Sworn In, December 12, 2024
*By Bill Wilson, Star Staff Writer*
PDF: `~/Downloads/Downing sworn in to Anniston City Council _ Anniston _ annistonstar.com.pdf`
URL: https://www.annistonstar.com/news/anniston/downing-sworn-in-to-anniston-city-council/article_47234122-b8fb-11ef-a67e-dffb0c862a12.html

Key sourced material:
- Sworn in by Calhoun County Probate Judge Alice Martin
- Accompanied by wife Katie and three children: Hannah, Luke, and Lillie
- First official act: motion to adjourn
- Lewis: "I've got a ton of confidence right now. I'm eager to get to work and see what we can do for Anniston. I'm excited more than anything."
- R.D. on the Bible: **"This is my grandmother Downing's Bible. Don't know exactly how old it is. I think it dates to the early '20s, and it's been passed through the family for a long time. It's the Bible that I was sworn in on when I was elected to the Calhoun County Commission."**
  → NOTE: R.D.'s grandmother's Bible = Lewis's **great-grandmother's** Bible. Profile originally said "grandmother" — corrected.
- R.D. on Lewis: "He loves this city and I'm proud for him, and I'm hopeful for the future of the city."
- Karla Eden (Main Street Anniston Executive Director) was present: "It's exciting to see one of our downtown business owners, whose roots are deeply planted in the Anniston community, stepping up to serve on the City Council."
- R.D. served as Calhoun County Commissioner 16 years, 1994–2010 (confirmed)

### Anniston Star — Full Term Candidacy, June 11, 2025
*From Staff Reports*
PDF: `~/Downloads/Lewis Downing to seek full term on Anniston Ward 1 councilman _ News _ annistonstar.com.pdf`
URL: https://www.annistonstar.com/news/lewis-downing-to-seek-full-term-on-anniston-ward-1-councilman/article_3f64df39-ba0b-46f8-9f27-d8ca34d38de4.html

Key sourced material (Lewis's direct words, written email to the Star):
- "I grew up in Anniston. I was born in Birmingham but we moved into a small house on 10th Street when I was just two. As it would turn out years later, that is the house that my wife and I lived in when we got married and had our first two children."
- Decision to run tied to "a personal commitment to service as he approached a milestone birthday" — the Star's paraphrase
- **"I was always raised to serve, to do your part, to be an asset and not a liability. That always stuck with me."** ← source for "milestone birthday / asset and not a liability" in profile
- Age confirmed: 40 at time of article (June 2025)
- School history: 10th Street Elementary, White Plains High School, Anniston High School, Sacred Heart Catholic School (graduated 2003), UA then JSU
- Third-generation owner confirmed
- McClellan: "Development of McClellan is going to take honest, responsible leadership."
- Opponents: Benjamin New, John Squires
