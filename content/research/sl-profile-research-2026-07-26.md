## James Spann

- Born June 6, 1955, Huntsville, AL. Raised in Greenville (Butler County) and Tuscaloosa.
- Father abandoned him at age 7. Found faith and purpose through storms.
- 47-year broadcast meteorology career. Chief Meteorologist, ABC 33/40 Birmingham.
- August 2025: launched **Alabama Weather Network** (streaming, statewide severe weather coverage).
- Credentials: Certified Broadcast Meteorologist (AMS). Nine-time AP Award for best weather anchor. Multiple Emmys. National Broadcaster of the Year. FLASH National Weather Person of the Year.
- Faith: Came to faith at Calvary Baptist Church, Tuscaloosa. Member, **Double Oak Community Church** (Birmingham/Shelby County). Leads children's worship every week. Preaches to kids. VBS.
- Book: *Weathering Life* (faith + career memoir).
- Motto: "Every job is a ministry."
- NE Alabama tie: Covered March 25, 2021 Calhoun County tornado outbreak (5 killed). Broadcast wall-to-wall while his own home was being damaged mid-broadcast.

## Kelly Clem

- Age 34 at time of tornado. Pastor, Goshen United Methodist Church, Goshen, AL.
- Palm Sunday, March 27, 1994, 11:39 a.m.: F4 tornado struck during worship service. 146 in building. Roof collapsed. 20 killed, 92 injured. 6 children among dead.
- Daughter **Hannah** (age 4) was killed. Hannah was waiting for the Easter program.
- No tornado sirens in the county at the time.
- Day after, when asked if disaster shattered her faith: *"It has not shattered my faith. I'm holding on to my faith. It's holding me."* And: *"Easter is coming."*
- Easter Sunday: returned to preach. Read from Romans. Handed out Easter baskets to surviving children. "There's no place I'd rather be today."
- Defining quote: **"You don't need faith for the things you understand."**
- Post-Goshen ministry: Pastor, Holmes Street UMC Huntsville (~2006–2014). Now District Superintendent, UMC (North Alabama).

## Bishop Rueben P. Job (historical — formation context)

- Born February 7, 1928, Jamestown, ND. Raised on a farm. Died January 3, 2015, Brentwood, TN. Age 86.
- Called to ministry early. B.A. Westmar College (1954). B.Div. Garrett-Evangelical Theological Seminary (1957).
- Air Force chaplain in Europe (1961–1962). Pastorates in North Dakota.
- **World Editor, The Upper Room** (Nashville) — made him the center of the UMC spiritual formation movement.
- Elected bishop 1984 (North Central Jurisdictional Conference). Iowa Episcopal Area until retirement 1992.
- **Key works:**
  - *A Guide to Prayer for Ministers and Other Servants* (Upper Room Books, 1983, with Norman Shawchuck) — lectionary-based daily office for ministers. Includes Merton, Underhill, Nouwen, Bonhoeffer, CS Lewis, Teresa of Avila, etc.
  - *A Guide to Prayer for All God's People*
  - *Three Simple Rules: A Wesleyan Way of Living* (Abingdon, 2007) — 300,000+ copies
  - Chaired hymnal revision committee → **1989 United Methodist Hymnal**
- Legacy: Rueben P. Job Endowed Chair in Spiritual Formation at Garrett-Evangelical Theological Seminary.
- **Sources:**
  - [Dakotas UMC — Job dies at 86](https://www.dakotasumc.org/news/bishop-rueben-p-job-dies-at-86)

## The Thomas Merton Prayer (full text)

*From "Thoughts in Solitude" (1958), Thomas Merton, Trappist monk, Gethsemani Abbey, KY*

> My Lord God, I have no idea where I am going.
> I do not see the road ahead of me.
> I cannot know for certain where it will end.
> Nor do I really know myself,
> and the fact that I think I am following your will
> does not mean that I am actually doing so.
> But I believe that the desire to please you
> does in fact please you.
> And I hope that I have that desire in all that I am doing.
> I hope that I will never do anything apart from that desire.
> And I know that if I do this you will lead me by the right road,
> though I may know nothing about it.
> Therefore will I trust you always though
> I may seem to be lost and in the shadow of death.
> I will not fear, for you are ever with me,
> and you will never leave me to face my perils alone.

*Appears in: A Guide to Prayer for Ministers and Other Servants (Job/Shawchuck, Upper Room, 1983)*
*Also widely cited by: Yale Divinity School vocation curriculum, U.S. Senate chaplain, On Being Project*
- **Sources:**
