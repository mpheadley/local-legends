# Ciara Smith-Roston — Seed

Research and scene notes for a potential Southern Legends profile.

---

## Who She Is

Mayor of Anniston, Alabama. The city's first Black female mayor and its youngest ever. Serving alongside Lewis Downing, who is Vice Mayor.

---

## Personal Connection

Visited Headley Flower Farm during its peak scaling period (manic episode era — walk-in cooler, renovated workshop, dahlias and peonies in third-year prime, Ladiga Trail kiosk). Matt's words from `manic-episode.md`: "Mayor Ciara Smith visited our farm. She saw the vision."

**The scene (verbatim — use this):**
This was approximately May 2025, during Matt's manic episode — peak scaling phase of the farm.

When she arrived, she told them to call her Sierra.

She bent down and gave Matt's daughter a hug, then invited her in for one. She came as a person, not an official.

They gave Sierra flowers.

She stood in the field while Matt and Heather were sharing their vision for the Chief Ladiga Trail Porch. She looked out and said:
> "This is so peaceful out here. I can see it. Y'all's vision is beautiful and powerful."

That is the line. That is the entry point for this profile.

**What makes the scene work:** She didn't say the correct mayor thing. She said the human thing. She crouched to a child's level before she said a word about the land. Then she looked out at the field and saw the vision. That sequence — child, then vision — is the whole profile in 30 seconds.

Also referenced in `the-hospital.mdx`: listed among the things being inventoried and grieved — "The Anniston Star articles. Mayor Ciara Smith's visit. Our equipment and my tools."

The farm visit is the opening. She came when it was at its most alive. She showed up. That matters.

**Jay Jenkins connection (same arc):**
Jay Jenkins — Sierra's husband — bought anniversary florals from Heather in June 2025, when the flower farm was going downhill. Matt delivered them to city hall. Jay had been a weekly flower customer for years; Sierra had visited the farm. Their patronage of Heather's flowers is the connective tissue between the Jenkins family and the Headley Flower Farm story. See `content/research/jay-jenkins.md`.

## Confirmed from Anniston Star resignation article (Oct 16, 2024)

**She was in the room when Jay resigned.** Photo caption from Bill Wilson's article: *"Councilwoman Ciara Smith in the background"* — Jay is being hugged by councilwoman Millie Harris, and Ciara is visible in the background. She was a councilwoman at that point, not yet mayor.

Jay resigned effective immediately at the end of what the Star called "a somewhat routine City Council meeting." The council was stunned. Ciara was there.

The sequence: Jay resigned → Lewis Downing filled Ward 1 → Ciara Smith eventually became mayor. All three in the same civic thread. She went from council colleague to mayor. She was already in that room.
