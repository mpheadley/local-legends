> *"The rawness, frankly, maybe business owners that aren't comfortable with my background, maybe aren't really good clients anyway."*

Matt still asking: 
- Why do I need to publish this?

- Isn't a private journal good enough?

The tension "lives there" — decision not yet resolved about how much vulnerability to surface on the public site vs. keeping some in private journaling (ChatGPT dumps, morning journals).

## Implications for the Site
