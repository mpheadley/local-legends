## What This Project Is

A multi-year public history campaign building toward the 200th anniversary of the Treaty of Cusseta (1832) — when Chief Ladiga of the Muscogee Creek Nation signed away the land that became a railroad, then a corridor, then the Chief Ladiga Trail in Northeast Alabama.

**Frame:** Reckoning, not celebration. The Creek Nation leads. Matt supports as journalist, narrator, and editorial thread.

**The tension at the center:** A trail named after a man whose people were removed to build the railroad that became the trail. Most people who walk it don't know any of this.

**Matt's role:** Narrator with proximity — not Creek cultural authority. He walked this trail through every season of his adult life. He met Toby Bennington in his office with manic visions of a pedestrian bridge. He lived in a farmhouse built in 1832, the same year Ladiga signed the treaty. He figured out the connection while walking the trail during a manic episode. He owns the editorial thread.
