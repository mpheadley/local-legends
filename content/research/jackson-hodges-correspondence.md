## Key Facts

- **Active Transportation Infrastructure Investment Program grant**: City had already applied for a very similar connector concept (coming out near the fire training center). Toby knew Matt's idea was close to what they'd already planned.
- **Federal grants in flux**: Spring 2025. Trump administration activity. This is why the grant path wasn't certain.
- **Five-year plan**: The mechanism for big city projects when grants fail. Toby's suggestion that Matt's idea could go in the five-year plan was the city's way of expressing interest without committing.
- **Jackson's role**: The human connection point — a city employee who saw Matt's Facebook post about Summerall Gate development, which prompted Matt to reach out. By 8:20 AM the same morning he'd already gotten a response from Toby.
- **Shea Miller**: Already in conversation with Matt by this date. Suggested public art, wayfinding signage, wellness programming at the trail node.
- **Jared Carter** = North Alabama Trails & Recreation. Matt was following up with him.
- **"Audrey from tourism"** = City of Anniston tourism contact, name only.
- **The one-pager**: Stored as a Google Drive PDF. This may be the same as or precursor to the `Wholehearted_Trail_Connector_Development_Report.pdf` referenced in the research doc. Worth locating.
