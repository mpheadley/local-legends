# Summary

Matt is developing a foundational essay for the Southern Legends journal about his faith journey over twenty years of pastoral vocation, the disruptions of 2016–2020 (Trump, COVID, George Floyd), his encounter with Marcus Borg's *Meeting Jesus Again for the First Time*, and his current position in faith deconstruction/uncertainty. The essay sits in honest interrogation rather than resolution—he's living in what Borg calls "postcritical naivete" and what he recognizes as liminality.

**Key themes identified:**
- **Vocational disruption (2016–2020):** Political upheaval, pandemic, racial reckoning that burnt him out and made institutional pastoral ministry untenable
- **Marcus Borg as permission structure:** Borg's idea that faith can exist without certainty, that doubt is legitimate, gave language to what Matt was experiencing
- **Postcritical naivete:** Not naive innocence, but faith that's been deconstructed and now chooses to believe despite uncertainty—describes where Matt is
- **Liminality:** Living in the in-between, margins, not resolved. Not even in reconstruction yet—just hanging out in the uncertainty
- **Audience shift:** The secular/spiritual-but-curious audience will meet him with questions, not the impulse to fix. This is his true congregation now
- **Psychedelic medicine as part of faith journey:** He participated in the psychedelic community during his first depression (healing work). This feels honest to include, though it's risky professionally. Less at stake now, and it actually strengthens credibility with people in deconstruction
- **New calling emerging:** He's still a pastor/prophet, just to a different flock—people actually living in liminal space, asking real questions
- **Real communities already exist:** Mosaic (Andy Yarnell, Gadsden), Romair Net Corvo (spirituality shop on Noble Street in Anniston), possibly a community in Jacksonville, Theology at Five (Jacksonville Presbyterian, progressive)

**What the essay isn't:**
- Resolved or landed
- A defense of faith
- Trying to convince anyone
- Performance of certainty or even performance of authenticity

**What the essay is:**
- Honest reckoning with where faith actually stands after twenty years, burnout, mental illness diagnosis, and major cultural disruption
- Permission for others living in the same liminal space to know they're not alone
- The real intellectual and spiritual work he always wanted to do—speaking to people actually seeking, not preaching to people seeking comfort

**Structure emerging:**
1. **Entry point:** Borg and the disruptions (Trump 2016, COVID, George Floyd, institutional church burnout)
2. **Core movement:** What broke down + what postcritical naivete means + what liminality feels like + the psychedelic healing work as part of the faith journey
3. **Current state:** Not resolved. Hanging in the margins. Drawn to communities like Mosaic and Romair, but also not ready to force participation
4. **Implicit ending:** This essay, this honest speaking, IS the calling now. The new pulpit. The audience that actually wants the questions

**Publication plan:**
- Republish the 2024 Anniston Star article as foundation/context
- This new essay builds from there to show continuity and deepening
- Archive other Patreon videos/content in the journal for centralization
- May also spawn: separate piece(s) on psychedelic medicine and faith, the Mosaic community, etc.
