Down on Wilmer Avenue in Anniston, Aquality Farms grows vegetables and sells them straight to folks who know good produce when they see it. The family behind the operation takes pride in what comes out of their soil, showing up at the farmers market with tomatoes, greens, and whatever else grows right that season. Anniston's been built on hard work and community, and this farm fits right into that tradition.
