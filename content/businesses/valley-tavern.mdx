Out on Pleasant Hill Road, Valley Tavern draws folks who want live music with their drinks. The band takes the stage most nights, and regulars know to show up early if they want a decent seat. You'll find the kind of crowd that appreciates good playing and cold beer in equal measure. Ardmore sits tucked into that corner of Tennessee where Alabama meets Georgia, a place where small towns still know how to gather.
