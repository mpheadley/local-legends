If you're hungry and rolling down Edwards Lake Road in Birmingham, El Compa Taco Truck serves up honest Mexican food from a bright yellow kitchen on wheels. Their tacos and fresh-made salsas draw regulars who know a good thing when they taste it. The folks running it treat customers like neighbors, not transactions. That's the kind of place that keeps Birmingham's neighborhoods fed and connected.

*El Compa Taco Truck is based in [Birmingham, Alabama](/places/birmingham). Edwards Lake Rd, Birmingham, AL 35235, USA · [elcompa.com](http://www.elcompa.com) · (205) 352-5282*
