On Capshaw Road outside Athens sits Bethel Church of Christ, a congregation rooted in the simple faith of the Churches of Christ tradition. They gather for worship, Bible study, and community fellowship, keeping to the straightforward ways their faith has always emphasized. Members here believe in living out their beliefs through service and devotion. In this corner of Limestone County, rural life and steadfast religious conviction still shape how neighbors come together.
