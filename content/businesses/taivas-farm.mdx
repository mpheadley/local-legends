Taivas Farm sits on rolling acreage in Glencoe, offering couples a genuine place to marry and celebrate. The venue handles weddings and events with the kind of personal attention that comes from owners who understand what matters most on a big day. They've built something real here—a working farm that opens its land to folks looking for an authentic Northeast Alabama setting, away from the cookie-cutter options elsewhere.
