Riddle's Chapel Global Methodist Church has been a steady presence on Ripley Road since 1887, serving the Athens community with quiet faithfulness across generations. The congregation gathers for worship and fellowship, rooted in Methodist tradition while part of the newer Global Methodist denomination. You'll find them welcoming visitors and neighbors alike. Out here in rural Madison County, churches like this one still anchor the rhythms of small-town life.
