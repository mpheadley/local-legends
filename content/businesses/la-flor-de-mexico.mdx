La Flor De Mexico is a mobile food operation serving Oxford with authentic Mexican cuisine. Operating from a food truck, the business brings fresh, made-to-order meals to the community, filling a gap for folks craving genuine flavors beyond chain restaurants. Owner-operated and focused on quality ingredients, it's become a reliable spot for families and working people looking for honest food at fair prices. That kind of consistency matters in a small town.
