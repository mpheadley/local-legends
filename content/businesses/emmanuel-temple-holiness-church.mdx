Emmanuel Temple Holiness Church on Simmsville Road has served Alabaster's faithful for generations, offering a place where the community gathers to worship and find fellowship. The church welcomes visitors seeking spiritual connection through traditional services and heartfelt prayer. Located at 680 Simmsville Road, Emmanuel Temple stands as a quiet anchor in this growing suburban town just south of Birmingham, where old-time faith still matters to the people who call it home.
