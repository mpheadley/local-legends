At Lucy Rucy's Sweets and Treats in Bay Minette, custom wedding cakes become edible art. Owner Lucy and her team craft cakes that taste as good as they look, working closely with couples to bring their visions to life. Each cake reflects the couple's personality and style. Located on North Hoyle Avenue, Lucy Rucy's sits in a town where community celebrations matter and where folks still value handmade goodness over shortcuts.

*Lucy Rucy’s Sweets and Treats is based in [Bay Minette, Alabama](/places/bay-minette). 103 N Hoyle Ave, Bay Minette, AL 36507, USA · [lucyrucy.com](https://www.lucyrucy.com) · (251) 580-9367*
