Stop by Youngs Farm and Statuary Stand on Highway 59 in Bay Minette for fresh produce and handcrafted garden statues. This roadside market stall offers what locals have relied on for years: quality vegetables straight from the farm and distinctive stone pieces to decorate your yard. The owners know their customers by name and take pride in what they sell. Bay Minette sits in the heart of Baldwin County, where farming traditions run deep.
