Baja California Grill brings authentic Mexican street food to Jacksonville from a food truck that's become a fixture in the community. They serve hand-rolled burritos, fresh ceviche, and traditional tacos made with recipes passed down through the owner's family. In a town where good Mexican food matters, they've filled that gap with honest cooking and fair prices. People keep coming back because the food tastes real.

*Baja California Grill is based in [Jacksonville, Alabama](/places/jacksonville). 1555 Pelham Rd S # A, Jacksonville, AL 36265, USA · (256) 435-0075 · [bajacaliforniagrill.shop](https://bajacaliforniagrill.shop)*
