On Gay Street in downtown Auburn, Auburn United Methodist Church has served the community since 1856. The congregation gathers for worship, fellowship, and service rooted in Methodist tradition. Their doors remain open to seekers, believers, and neighbors looking for spiritual connection. Auburn itself has grown around this historic church, becoming a vibrant college town where faith and learning intersect.
