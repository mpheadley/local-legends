Curtiston Methodist Church has anchored faith and community in Attalla since its doors opened on Perman Street. Generations of families have gathered in these pews for Sunday services, weddings, and the kind of fellowship that sustains a town. The church remains a quiet witness to the lives lived around it, marking time through seasons of joy and sorrow. Attalla's neighborhoods still rely on institutions like this one to hold things together.
