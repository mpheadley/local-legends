# Blurb

You'll find Momma Goldberg's Deli parked on Glenn Avenue in Auburn, slinging sandwiches and sides that taste like someone's grandmother spent all day cooking. Their mobile setup keeps things simple: good bread, quality meat, and the kind of straightforward food that doesn't need fancy talk. It's the sort of place that feeds students, families, and anyone hungry enough to know what matters. Auburn's got a way of attracting folks who do honest work, and this deli fits right in.
