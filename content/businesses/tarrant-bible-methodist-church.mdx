Tarrant Bible Methodist Church has been serving Birmingham's Linthicum neighborhood since way back, anchoring the community with straightforward faith and steady fellowship. They welcome folks through the doors at 1229 Linthicum Street with the kind of genuine hospitality that defines small churches across the South. Sunday services and Bible study keep the congregation grounded in Scripture and each other. Birmingham's neighborhoods like this one were built on churches just like Tarrant, places where people still show up for one another.
