At Brito's Produce on Finley Avenue, you'll find what grows well in Alabama soil and what travels from across the border. The market stall stocks fresh vegetables, fruits, and the ingredients folks need for real cooking. Owner Joe Brito knows his regulars by name and what they're looking for. Birmingham's Finley Avenue corridor has long served as a gathering place where neighbors still shop local.

*Brito’s Produce is based in [Birmingham, Alabama](/places/birmingham). 455 Finley Ave W, Birmingham, AL 35204, USA · (205) 251-8016*
