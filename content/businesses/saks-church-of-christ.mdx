Saks Church of Christ has served its Anniston community for generations, welcoming folks through its doors on Saks Road with straightforward faith and neighborly spirit. The congregation gathers to worship, study scripture, and support one another through life's seasons. They're rooted in the simple conviction that faith matters in everyday living. Anniston itself has long been a place where people of faith have built strong communities together.
