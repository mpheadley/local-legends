When a Birmingham bride needs a dress that fits just right, K & J's Elegant Pastries on 9th Avenue has been the go-to spot for years. The shop carries gowns for weddings, proms, and other special occasions, with a team that takes time to help each customer find what works for her. They handle alterations too, making sure every detail is perfect. In a neighborhood that's seen plenty of changes, this bridal destination keeps doing what it does best.
