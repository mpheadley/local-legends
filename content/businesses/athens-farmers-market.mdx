Every Saturday, folks gather at the Athens Farmers Market on West Green Street to buy fresh vegetables, local goods, and whatever else area farmers brought to town that morning. It's the kind of place where you know the person selling your tomatoes and can ask them how to cook it right. The market keeps Athens's downtown alive and connected, giving neighbors a reason to show up and see each other.

*Athens Farmers Market is based in [Athens, Alabama](/places/athens). 409 W Green St, Athens, AL 35611, USA · [athensmainstreet.org/athens-farmers-market](https://athensmainstreet.org/athens-farmers-market)*
