For over a century, Village View United Methodist Church has anchored the Hine Street neighborhood in Athens, opening its doors to folks seeking community and faith. The church gathers people for Sunday worship, fellowship, and service rooted in Methodist tradition. Located at 331 Hine Street South, it remains a steady presence in a town where neighbors still know each other's names.
