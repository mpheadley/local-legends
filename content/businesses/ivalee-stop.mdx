Ivalee Stop brings honest food to Attalla from a food truck parked on Highway 77. The menu keeps things simple: sandwiches, sides, and the kind of meals that stick with you. Owner and cook alike, the folks here know their regulars by name and remember their orders. Attalla's a working town where people value a fair price and real food made with care.
