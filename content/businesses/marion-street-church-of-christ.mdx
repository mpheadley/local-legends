For over a century, Marion Street Church of Christ has served Athens with quiet faithfulness. Located on North Marion Street, this congregation focuses on biblical teaching and community care, welcoming neighbors through worship and service. Members gather to study Scripture and support one another through life's seasons. Athens residents know this church as a steady presence in their downtown neighborhood.
