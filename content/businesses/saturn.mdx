Live music fills the brick walls at Saturn, where Birmingham's musicians and out-of-town bands take the stage on 41st Street South. The venue draws crowds hungry for everything from indie rock to hip-hop, served up with good sound and better company. You'll find the kind of place where strangers become friends over a shared song. In a neighborhood that's quietly becoming a cultural hub, Saturn sits right where people gather to remember why live music matters.
