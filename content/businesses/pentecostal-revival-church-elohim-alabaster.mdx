Pentecostal Revival Church Elohim welcomes seekers to worship on US-31 in Alabaster, where Pastor Raul Polanco leads a congregation rooted in Pentecostal faith and community. The church gathers folks for prayer, fellowship, and spiritual growth through a traditional approach that honors the movement's roots. You'll find them ready to talk faith any Sunday. Alabaster itself sits between Birmingham and the lakes, a growing town where working families build their lives.
