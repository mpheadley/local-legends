When a couple walks down the aisle, photographer Courtney at Photos By Heart knows the real story isn't just about the dress or the rings. She captures the laugh shared during vows, the squeeze of a hand, the tears that say everything words can't. Working from her loft studio in Birmingham's vibrant arts district, she photographs weddings with an eye for the moments that actually matter. Second Avenue North keeps growing with artists and makers who understand that authenticity beats polish every time.
