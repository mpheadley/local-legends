Pop In and Out brings a different kind of shopping to Birmingham's Bessemer corridor. This pop-up shop keeps things fresh by changing what they offer, so regulars never know what they'll find when they stop by. It's the kind of place that rewards curiosity and spontaneity. The neighborhood itself has long been a working heart of Birmingham, where practical meets resourceful.

*POP IN AND OUT is based in [Birmingham, Alabama](/places/birmingham). 5301 Bessemer Super Hwy, Birmingham, AL 35228, USA · [citgo.com](http://www.citgo.com)*
