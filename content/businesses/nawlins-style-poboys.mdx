If you're driving through Alabaster hungry, Nawlins Style Po'Boys will set you straight with sandwiches that taste like New Orleans showed up on wheels. They load their bread with roast beef, shrimp, and all the fixings that make a po'boy worth eating. Pull up to 1207 1st St N and order from the window. Alabaster's been growing, but it still knows how to welcome a good meal passing through town.
