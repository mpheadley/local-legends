Hannah Miller captures weddings with an eye for the moments that matter most: the quiet laugh before the ceremony, the way light falls across a first dance. Based in Auburn, she photographs couples throughout Alabama with a straightforward style that lets genuine emotion shine through. Her work honors the people in front of her camera, not trends. Auburn's tree-lined streets and historic charm provide a fitting backdrop for a photographer who understands that the best pictures come from paying attention.
