Families homeschooling across Alabama gather each year at the Anniston Community Fair to connect, share resources, and find support for their educational journey. Local vendors, workshops, and fellow parents create a space where kids can learn alongside others following a similar path. It's practical, friendly, and rooted in the idea that education thrives in community. Anniston sits in the foothills of East Alabama, where tight-knit neighborhoods still mean something.
