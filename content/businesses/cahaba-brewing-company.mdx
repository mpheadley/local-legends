Head to Cahaba Brewing Company's food truck in Birmingham and you'll find craft beer made right here in Alabama, poured fresh and honest. Located on 5th Avenue South, they're brewing the kind of beers that taste like home, with a menu that matches the quality. You can call (205) 578-2616 to find out where they're parked that day.
