On Mell Street in Auburn, Panera Bread serves up soups, salads, and sandwiches to students and townspeople alike. You can grab lunch between classes or settle in with coffee and a pastry for an afternoon. The menu's straightforward, the service is quick, and it's become a reliable spot for folks who want something fresh without the fuss. Auburn's downtown keeps growing, and places like this help make it a destination worth visiting.
