Roll Cajun Boudin brings authentic Louisiana cooking to Birmingham's streets from their food truck on Sixth Avenue South. The menu centers on boudin sausage, prepared the way it's done down in Cajun country, along with other regional favorites that taste like they came straight from someone's grandmother's kitchen. You'll find the truck parked and ready most days, serving folks who know good food when they taste it. Birmingham's Southside neighborhood has long been a place where people gather around real cooking and real stories.

*Roll Cajun Boudin is based in [Birmingham, Alabama](/places/birmingham). 2208 6th Ave S, Birmingham, AL 35233, USA · [rollcajun.com](https://www.rollcajun.com) · (205) 908-4722*
