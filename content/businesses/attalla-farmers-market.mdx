Every Saturday morning, folks from around Attalla gather at the farmers market on US-278 to pick up fresh vegetables, plants, and goods straight from local growers. It's the kind of place where you know who grew your tomatoes and can ask them how to cook it. The market keeps the community connected and supports the people who work the land. Attalla sits in the heart of Etowah County, where agriculture still matters.
