Ampersandwich Avondale keeps things simple: good sandwiches, made fresh, served hot. This pop-up spot on Third Avenue puts care into what lands between two slices of bread, letting quality ingredients and honest cooking speak for themselves. You'll find them ready to feed hungry folks looking for real food without the fuss. Avondale's been drawing people back with places like this that get the fundamentals right.
