For over fifty years, Alabaster Church of God has anchored the First Avenue corridor with straightforward preaching and steady community presence. The congregation welcomes visitors looking for traditional worship and genuine fellowship, meeting weekly at their modest sanctuary just west of downtown. They lean into service work and neighborly care rather than flash. Alabaster itself grew from a small mining town into a thriving suburban community where churches like this one remain gathering places.

*Alabaster Church of God is based in [Alabaster, Alabama](/places/alabaster). 530 1st Ave W, Alabaster, AL 35007, USA · [alabasterchurchofgod.org](http://alabasterchurchofgod.org) · (205) 663-9295*
