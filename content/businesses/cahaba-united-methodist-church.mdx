Cahaba United Methodist Church has been a steady presence in Birmingham's Cahaba Valley neighborhood for generations. Located on Cahaba Valley Road, the church welcomes folks looking for a traditional Methodist community rooted in faith and service. Sunday services bring neighbors together, and the church stays active in local outreach throughout the year. In this corner of Birmingham, places like this one anchor the everyday rhythms of family and faith.
