On Church Street in downtown Birmingham, Ivory & White Bridal Boutique helps brides find the dress that feels like theirs. The shop carries gowns in various styles and sizes, working with each woman to make sure she walks out feeling confident. Whether you're planning an intimate ceremony or a big celebration, the team takes time to listen to what you're imagining. Church Street itself remains a vital gathering place in the heart of the city.
