Every Saturday morning, Anniston's farmers and vendors gather at the City Market Building on West 11th Street to sell what they've grown and made. You'll find fresh produce, baked goods, and local crafts in a place that's been feeding the community for generations. The Downtown Market brings neighbors together around real food and honest work. It's the kind of gathering that keeps Anniston's heart beating strong.

*THE DOWNTOWN MARKET is based in [Anniston, Alabama](/places/anniston). City Market Building, 126 W 11th St, Anniston, AL 36201, USA · [annistonmarket.com](http://annistonmarket.com) · (256) 343-6560*
