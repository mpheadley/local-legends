Jeremy Berkson photographs Birmingham weddings with an eye for the quiet moments that matter most. His work captures real emotion without pretense, the kind of images families actually want to live with. You'll find him at 2045 Delene Drive, and you can reach him at (954) 496-4194 or through his website. Birmingham's neighborhoods hold stories worth telling, and Berkson knows how to find them in the spaces between the posed shots.
