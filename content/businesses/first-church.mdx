First Church in Athens has been a gathering place for the community since its founding, offering worship and fellowship to generations of North Alabama families. Located on Lucas Ferry Road, the church welcomes visitors through its doors and invites them to be part of something larger than themselves. You can learn more at firstchurchathens.org or call (256) 232-7710. Athens remains a quiet corner of Alabama where faith and community still run deep.
