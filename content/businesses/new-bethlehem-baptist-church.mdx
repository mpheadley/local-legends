For nearly a century, New Bethlehem Baptist Church has anchored the faith life of Bessemer's North End. Located on 11th Avenue, this congregation gathers for worship, service, and community support rooted in Baptist tradition. The church welcomes visitors to Sunday services and midweek programs that strengthen spiritual bonds. Bessemer itself carries the industrial heritage of Birmingham's steel valley, a working town where churches like New Bethlehem remain gathering places for neighbors and families.
