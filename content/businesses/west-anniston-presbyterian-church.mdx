West Anniston Presbyterian Church has welcomed neighbors through its doors since the 1800s, offering a quiet place for worship and community in the heart of town. The congregation gathers for Sunday services, fellowship, and the kind of steady faith that's anchored generations of families. Anniston itself grew around these institutions, becoming a place where history and everyday life intertwine along tree-lined streets.
