In Birmingham's Southside, The Avondale transforms a historic loft into a wedding space where couples can marry the way they want. Exposed brick and tall windows give the place character without pretense. The team handles logistics so you can focus on your people. It's the kind of venue that feels like a genuine Birmingham gathering spot, the sort of place that's always mattered to the neighborhood.

*The Avondale is based in [Birmingham, Alabama](/places/birmingham). 4322 3rd Ave S, Birmingham, AL 35222, USA · [theavondaleloft.com](http://www.theavondaleloft.com) · (205) 240-1682*
