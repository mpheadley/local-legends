Awaken Church of Jacksonville gathers folks on Sunday mornings for worship and teaching grounded in Scripture. They've built a community where newcomers and lifelong residents alike can explore faith questions in a straightforward way. Beyond Sunday services, they're involved in local outreach and helping neighbors navigate life's harder seasons. It's the kind of place where people show up as they are.

*Awaken Church of Jacksonville is based in [Jacksonville, Alabama](/places/jacksonville). 302 Mountain St NW, Jacksonville, AL 36265, USA · [awakenjacksonville.org/about-us](https://awakenjacksonville.org/about-us)*
