Eugene's Hot Chicken serves up crispy, fiery bird from a food truck parked on 9th Avenue North in Birmingham. The menu keeps things simple: chicken sandwiches and tenders seasoned with real heat, the kind that makes you reach for a cold drink. Since opening, Eugene's has drawn hungry folks looking for straightforward food done right. Uptown Birmingham keeps growing with spots like this, where good food and genuine hospitality matter more than fancy frills.
