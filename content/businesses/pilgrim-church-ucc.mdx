Pilgrim Church, a United Church of Christ congregation on Birmingham's south side, has long opened its doors to folks seeking spiritual community and social justice work. They worship together, support their neighbors, and stand up for what's right in the world. Located on 6th Avenue South, the church anchors a neighborhood that's seen hard times and held onto hope through decades of change.
