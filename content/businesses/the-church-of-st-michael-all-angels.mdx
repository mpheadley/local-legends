The Church of St. Michael & All Angels has anchored Anniston's West End since 1888, its Gothic Revival walls holding generations of faith and community. The sanctuary's stained glass and carved woodwork reflect a commitment to beauty in worship that feels timeless. Whether you're drawn by architecture, history, or spiritual seeking, this church welcomes you through its doors. Anniston itself remains a town where old buildings still tell stories worth hearing.
