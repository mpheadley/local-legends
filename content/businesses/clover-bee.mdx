Clover + Bee in Birmingham helps brides find dresses that feel like themselves. The shop carries gowns from independent designers alongside established names, giving you options beyond the usual chain stores. Their team takes time to listen to what you actually want instead of pushing you toward what's trendy. In a city where tradition runs deep, this bridal boutique proves Birmingham's still got room for something refreshingly different.
