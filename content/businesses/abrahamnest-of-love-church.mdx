On Hickory Avenue in Birmingham, Abraham/Nest of Love Church has been opening its doors to folks seeking community and spiritual grounding. The church focuses on genuine connection and faith-centered living, welcoming neighbors from all walks of life through worship and service. Their ministry reflects the kind of steadfast presence that's meant a lot to southwest Birmingham for years. In this part of the city, churches like this one remain anchors for families building lives together.
