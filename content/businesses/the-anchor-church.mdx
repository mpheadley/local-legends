Tucked along Wintzell Avenue in Bayou La Batre, the Anchor Church serves as a spiritual home for the working folks of this tight-knit coastal community. The church welcomes neighbors with straightforward faith and genuine fellowship, staying rooted in what matters most to people here. Bayou La Batre itself has long depended on the water, with generations of shrimpers and fishermen building their lives around the bay's rhythms and seasons.

*The Anchor Church is based in [Bayou La Batre, Alabama](/places/bayou-la-batre). 13855 S Wintzell Ave, Bayou La Batre, AL 36509, USA · (251) 607-8486*
