On any given night, Plum Bar pulls in folks looking for live music and good company. Located on 17th Street North in Birmingham, it's the kind of place where the band might play anything from blues to rock, and nobody's worried about being anywhere else. You'll find regulars nursing drinks and newcomers surprised by what they stumbled into. This stretch of Birmingham keeps evolving, with old warehouses turning into spots where people actually want to be.
