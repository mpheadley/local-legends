Every Sunday, Bay Assembly opens its doors on US-31 to folks seeking faith and community. The church welcomes all who walk through, offering a place to worship and connect with neighbors. Pastor and congregation alike believe in serving their community with genuine care and open hearts. Bay Minette itself sits nestled in Baldwin County, a town where people still know their neighbors' names.
