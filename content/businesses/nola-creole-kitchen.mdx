Nola Creole Kitchen rolls up to Anniston with the real deal: gumbo, jambalaya, and po'boys that taste like they came straight from the bayou. Owner brings authentic New Orleans cooking to a mobile kitchen on Hillyer High Road, serving folks who crave that deep Louisiana flavor. The food hits different when it's made with care and tradition. Anniston's always had a taste for good eating, and this kitchen fits right in.

*Nola Creole Kitchen is based in [Anniston, Alabama](/places/anniston). 920 Hillyer High Rd, Anniston, AL 36207, USA*
