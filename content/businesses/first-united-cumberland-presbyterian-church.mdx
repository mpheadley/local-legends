First United Cumberland Presbyterian Church has been a spiritual home for Birmingham folks since its doors opened on Bessemer Road. The congregation gathers for worship, fellowship, and service rooted in their Presbyterian traditions. They welcome neighbors seeking community and faith in an honest, straightforward way. In this corner of Birmingham, churches like this one still anchor neighborhoods and bring people together across generations.
