Asbury United Methodist Church has been a steady presence in Birmingham for generations, welcoming folks through its doors on Cahaba Valley Road. The congregation gathers to worship, serve their community, and support one another through life's seasons. You'll find honest faith practiced here, grounded in Methodist tradition and genuine care for neighbors. In the Cahaba Valley area, Asbury stands as a gathering place where Birmingham's people come together.
