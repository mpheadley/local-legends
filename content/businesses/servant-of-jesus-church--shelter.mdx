Servant of Jesus Church & Shelter in Anniston provides a safe place for people experiencing homelessness while offering spiritual guidance rooted in Christian faith. The organization meets immediate needs—shelter, meals, clothing—alongside longer-term support aimed at helping folks rebuild their lives. Run by dedicated volunteers and staff, it's become a steady presence for vulnerable neighbors who might otherwise have nowhere to turn.

*Servant of Jesus Church & Shelter is based in [Anniston, Alabama](/places/anniston). 3113 McCoy Ave, Anniston, AL 36201, USA · (256) 486-6005 · [www.servantsofjesus.net](http://www.servantsofjesus.net)*
