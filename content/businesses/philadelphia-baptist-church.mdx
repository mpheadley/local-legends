Philadelphia Baptist Church has served Birmingham's Pump House Road community for generations, offering worship and fellowship to families who've made it their spiritual home. Sunday services bring neighbors together in a place rooted in faith and tradition. The church remains a steady presence in a neighborhood where people know each other and look out for one another. Birmingham's south side holds stories like these in every corner.
