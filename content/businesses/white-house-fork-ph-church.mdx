White House Fork PH Church has been a gathering place for Bay Minette families since way back, offering worship and community to generations of folks in this corner of Baldwin County. The church sits quiet on Whitehouse Fork Road, a reminder of how faith takes root in small towns across Alabama. Bay Minette itself carries the spirit of rural life, where neighbors know each other and Sunday mornings still matter.
