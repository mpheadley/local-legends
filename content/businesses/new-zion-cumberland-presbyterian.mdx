New Zion Cumberland Presbyterian has stood on Fifth Street North in Birmingham since the church's founding, serving generations of worshippers through faith and community. The congregation gathers to pray, sing, and support one another through life's changes. You'll find them welcoming visitors who walk through those doors looking for spiritual connection and fellowship. In a neighborhood that's seen plenty of change, this church remains a steady presence.
