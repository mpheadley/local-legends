Pull up to Tacos Don Andy on Mini-Warehouse Road and you'll find authentic Mexican street food done right. The food truck serves up tacos, quesadillas, and other classics that taste like they came straight from someone's kitchen rather than a chain operation. Owner Andy knows his craft and keeps regulars coming back for honest flavors at fair prices. Birmingham's industrial neighborhoods have long been home to hardworking folks who appreciate good food without the fuss.

*Tacos Don Andy is based in [Birmingham, Alabama](/places/birmingham). 5474 Mini-Warehouse Rd, Birmingham, AL 35244, USA · (205) 757-6682*
