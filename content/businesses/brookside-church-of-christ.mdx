Brookside Church of Christ has been a steady gathering place for Birmingham folks seeking worship and community since its founding. Located in the heart of the city, the congregation welcomes people through its doors each week for service and fellowship. They focus on scripture, prayer, and living out Christian values in everyday life. Birmingham's Brookside neighborhood has long been a place where neighbors know each other and look after one another.
