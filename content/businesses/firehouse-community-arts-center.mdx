In a converted firehouse on Birmingham's south side, live music brings neighbors together night after night. Firehouse Community Arts Center books local and touring bands while hosting open mics and jam sessions that keep the stage warm year-round. It's the kind of place where you might discover your next favorite musician or reconnect with old friends over good sound and better company. This stretch of 41st Street keeps proving that community matters most.

*Firehouse Community Arts Center is based in [Birmingham, Alabama](/places/birmingham). 412 41st St S, Birmingham, AL 35222, USA · [firehousecac.org](https://www.firehousecac.org) · (205) 516-8547*
