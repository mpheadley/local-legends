For over a century, Pleasant Hill United Methodist Church has anchored the Bessemer community with straightforward faith and genuine neighborly care. Located on Bell Hill Road, this congregation welcomes folks looking for traditional worship and real connection. Their doors open to anyone seeking a church home built on service and Scripture. In Bessemer, where working people have long valued honest roots and steady values, Pleasant Hill stands as a quiet reminder of those enduring principles.
