If you're hungry in Auburn, Taqueria y Carniceria Durango pulls up with authentic Mexican food that hits different. From fresh carnitas to made-to-order tacos, they do the work right at their food truck on North Dean Road. The meat comes from their butcher counter, so you know what you're getting. Auburn's got plenty of chain restaurants, but this is where folks go when they want something real.
