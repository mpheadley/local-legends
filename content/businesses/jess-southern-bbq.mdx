Jess Southern BBQ rolls into Anniston from a food truck on McClellan Boulevard, serving up slow-smoked meats that taste like someone's been tending the pit all day. The kind of place where you order at the window and eat standing up, sauce on your fingers, without apologizing. Anniston's got grit and history running through it, and this truck fits right in with that honest way of doing things.
