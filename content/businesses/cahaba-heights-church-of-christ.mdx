Cahaba Heights Church of Christ has served Birmingham's Caldwell Mill neighborhood for generations, offering a welcoming place where folks gather for worship and community. The congregation focuses on Bible study, fellowship, and supporting neighbors through life's ups and downs. You'll find them on Facebook staying connected with their flock. In this part of Birmingham, churches like this one remain the backbone of neighborhood life.

*Cahaba Heights Church of Christ is based in [Birmingham, Alabama](/places/birmingham). 4602 Caldwell Mill Rd, Birmingham, AL 35243, USA · [facebook.com/CahabaHeightsChurch](https://www.facebook.com/CahabaHeightsChurch)*
