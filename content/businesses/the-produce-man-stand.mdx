At The Produce Man Stand in Bessemer, you'll find fresh vegetables and fruits stacked high and prices kept honest. The folks running this market stall know their regular customers by name and remember what you bought last week. They source local when they can and aren't afraid to tell you straight about what's ripe and what to skip. Bessemer's working neighborhoods depend on places like this to feed their families right.
