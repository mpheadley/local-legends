Tucked on Highland Avenue in Birmingham sits a 1920s Tudor Revival home where couples exchange vows surrounded by original hardwood and vintage charm. The Donnelly House offers intimate spaces for weddings and events, with flexibility for your vision and attentive service. Stone fireplaces and period details create naturally beautiful backdrops without feeling fussy. Highland is one of Birmingham's most walkable neighborhoods, where restored historic homes tell the city's evolving story.
