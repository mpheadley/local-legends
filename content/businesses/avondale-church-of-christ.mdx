For over a century, Avondale Church of Christ has anchored its Birmingham neighborhood with straightforward faith and genuine community care. Located on Second Avenue South, this congregation welcomes neighbors seeking honest worship and fellowship without pretense. They gather weekly to study scripture, support one another, and live out their beliefs in everyday life. In a city known for its resilience and spiritual depth, Avondale remains a steady presence where people still know each other's names.
