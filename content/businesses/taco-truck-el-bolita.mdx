From a truck parked on Fourth Avenue North, El Bolita serves up honest tacos that draw folks from all over Birmingham. The menu keeps it simple: fresh meat, warm tortillas, and toppings that taste like they matter. You'll find a steady line of regulars who know good food when they taste it. This stretch of downtown has become a gathering spot where the city's neighborhoods meet.

*Taco truck El Bolita is based in [Birmingham, Alabama](/places/birmingham). 1100 4th Ave N, Birmingham, AL 35203, USA · (205) 542-0739*
