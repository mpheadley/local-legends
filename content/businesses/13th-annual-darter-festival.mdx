Birmingham's Darter Festival draws folks who know their fish. Now in its thirteenth year, this gathering celebrates the small, colorful darters that call Alabama's waters home. Whether you're a serious collector, a curious angler, or just someone who appreciates what swims in our creeks, you'll find talks, vendors, and fellow enthusiasts. The festival keeps Birmingham connected to the natural world that built this city.
