Bluff Park Baptist Church has served Birmingham's McGwier Drive neighborhood since 1957, offering Sunday worship and community programs rooted in faith and fellowship. Members gather for Bible study, youth groups, and outreach that reflect their commitment to living out Christian values in everyday life. The church welcomes visitors looking for a place to worship and connect with neighbors. In Birmingham's diverse Bluff Park area, this congregation remains a steady anchor for the community it calls home.

*Bluff Park Baptist Church is based in [Birmingham, Alabama](/places/birmingham). 2211 McGwier Dr, Birmingham, AL 35226, USA · [bluffparkbaptist.org](http://bluffparkbaptist.org) · (205) 822-3240*
