For over a century, Auburn Church of Christ has anchored the heart of this college town, welcoming students and families through its doors on South College Street. The congregation gathers to study Scripture, share meals, and support one another through life's seasons. Their straightforward approach to faith reflects the practical values that run deep in Auburn's community. The church sits at the crossroads where university life and hometown traditions meet.
