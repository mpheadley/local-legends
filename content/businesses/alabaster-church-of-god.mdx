Alabaster Church of God has been serving its community since 1964, offering Sunday services and fellowship for folks seeking spiritual connection. Located on First Avenue, the church welcomes visitors of all backgrounds to worship together and participate in their weekly programs. The congregation believes in the power of prayer and community support to guide people through life's challenges. Alabaster itself has grown from a quiet town into a thriving suburb where neighbors still know each other's names.

*Alabaster Church of God is based in [Alabaster, Alabama](/places/alabaster). 530 1st Ave W, Alabaster, AL 35007, USA · [alabasterchurchofgod.org](http://alabasterchurchofgod.org) · (205) 663-9295*
