Iron City Baptist Church has served the Anniston community since 1902, offering a steady spiritual home for generations of believers. Located on Mandy Lane, the congregation gathers for worship, fellowship, and service rooted in Baptist tradition. Their doors remain open to anyone seeking connection and faith. Anniston itself carries the rich industrial heritage that gave this church its name, a city shaped by iron mills and the resilience of its people.

*Iron City Baptist Church is based in [Anniston, Alabama](/places/anniston). 41 Mandy Ln, Anniston, AL 36207, USA · [ironcity.org](http://ironcity.org) · (256) 238-1293*
