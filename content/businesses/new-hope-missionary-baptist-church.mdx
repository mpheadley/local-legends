New Hope Missionary Baptist Church has served Jacksonville since 1872, making it one of the oldest continuously operating churches in the area. The congregation gathers to worship, study scripture, and support one another through life's transitions. For over 150 years, the church has been a steady presence in the community, offering spiritual guidance and fellowship to generations of families. Their website at nhmbc1872.org provides information about service times and upcoming events.
