For over a century, Morgan United Methodist Church has anchored its Bessemer community with steady faith and neighborly care. Located on Morgan Road, the congregation welcomes folks through its doors seeking spiritual fellowship and genuine connection. Whether it's Sunday service or a weeknight gathering, Morgan stays true to Methodist traditions of service and devotion. Bessemer's working people have long called this corner of town home.
