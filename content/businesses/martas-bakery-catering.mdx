When Birmingham couples need a wedding cake that tastes as good as it looks, they call Marta's Bakery & Catering on Crosshaven Drive. Marta and her team bake fresh cakes for the big day, handling everything from custom designs to full catering services. They understand that a wedding cake needs to feed your guests and reflect who you are. Birmingham's South Side has long been a neighborhood where people know their baker by name.
