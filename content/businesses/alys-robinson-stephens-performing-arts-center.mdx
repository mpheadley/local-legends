Down on 10th Avenue South in Birmingham, the Alys Robinson Stephens Performing Arts Center keeps live music alive. This venue hosts everything from bluegrass bands to classical performances, giving folks a real place to gather and hear quality entertainment. Whether you're after traditional string music or contemporary shows, they've got something worth your time. The center sits right in the heart of a neighborhood that's been quietly becoming one of Birmingham's cultural anchors.

*Alys Robinson Stephens Performing Arts Center is based in [Birmingham, Alabama](/places/birmingham). 1200 10th Ave S, Birmingham, AL 35294, USA · [alysstephens.org](https://www.alysstephens.org) · (205) 975-2787*
