For nearly a century, Eastern Valley Baptist Church has anchored Potter Avenue in Bessemer, welcoming neighbors through its doors on Sunday mornings and throughout the week. The congregation gathers for worship, Bible study, and service rooted in faith and community. Members know each other by name here, and newcomers are treated like family from their first visit. Bessemer itself has always been a place where churches like this one form the backbone of neighborhood life.
