Rob Angus keeps bluegrass alive in Birmingham, spinning out the kind of picking and singing that echoes through honky-tonks and living rooms alike. His band grounds their sound in the traditions that matter, letting the fiddle and banjo do the talking. You'll find them playing steady around town, drawing folks who still believe in real music played real well. Birmingham's music scene runs deep, from its jazz roots to the country sounds still echoing off Lakeview.
