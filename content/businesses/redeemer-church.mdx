Redeemer Church in Oxford gathers folks on Sunday mornings to worship together and figure out what faith means in their daily lives. They focus on scripture, community service, and showing up for neighbors in need. It's the kind of place where people find solid ground, whether they've been church-going their whole lives or are stepping through the doors for the first time. More information at redeemeroxford.org.
