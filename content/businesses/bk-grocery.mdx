Out on Brownsferry Road in Athens, B&K Grocery shows up when folks need it most. This pop-up shop brings fresh groceries and essentials to a stretch of land where options run thin. Owner knows the community and stocks what neighbors actually want, not what some distant warehouse decides. Athens has always been a place where people look out for each other, and B&K fits right into that spirit.
