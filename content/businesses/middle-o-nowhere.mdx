On the Tennessee side of the state line in Ardmore, Middle O' Nowhere brings live music to a stretch of backroad where not much else happens. The band takes the stage regularly, drawing folks who don't mind driving out past where the pavement gets thin. It's the kind of place where neighbors become friends over cold drinks and good tunes. Ardmore itself sits quiet and rural, the sort of town that moves at its own pace.
