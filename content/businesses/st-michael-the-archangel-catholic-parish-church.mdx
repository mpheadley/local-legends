St. Michael the Archangel Catholic Parish Church has served Auburn's faithful since 1886, offering Mass and sacraments to students and longtime parishioners alike. Located on North College Street, the parish welcomes all who seek spiritual community and guidance. Whether you're looking for a welcoming congregation or seeking connection to Auburn's religious heritage, St. Michael's doors stay open. Auburn itself thrives as a college town that values both tradition and forward thinking.
