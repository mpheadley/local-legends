In a converted warehouse on Birmingham's Southside, Mojo Pub brings live music seven nights a week. The space fills with blues, rock, and soul from regional and touring bands while locals pack the bar for cold drinks and good company. It's the kind of place where strangers become friends over shared songs. This stretch of 7th Avenue South has become a genuine music district, drawing folks who want the real thing.

*Mojo Pub is based in [Birmingham, Alabama](/places/birmingham). 2801 7th Ave S, Birmingham, AL 35233, USA · (205) 703-0933*
