Coop DeVille brings wood-fired chicken and traditional sides to Jacksonville from a food truck that's become a reliable fixture around town. They focus on doing one thing well—rotisserie chicken cooked slow over hardwood—and pairing it with straightforward, honest food. For folks looking for a meal that tastes like it was actually cooked instead of assembled, Coop DeVille shows up when you need it.

*Coop DeVille is based in [Jacksonville, Alabama](/places/jacksonville). 401 Pelham Rd S, Jacksonville, AL 36265, USA · (256) 435-1818 · [www.facebook.com/coopdevilleofjacksonville](https://www.facebook.com/coopdevilleofjacksonville)*
