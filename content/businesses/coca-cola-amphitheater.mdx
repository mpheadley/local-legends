Birmingham's Coca-Cola Amphitheater brings live music to the city's North Avenue, hosting everything from country to rock to blues. The outdoor venue seats thousands under the sky, drawing folks from across Alabama for concerts that matter. You'll find everything from touring acts to local talent taking the stage. The neighborhood around 15th Avenue North has grown into a real gathering spot for music lovers and the folks who feed them.

*Coca-Cola Amphitheater is based in [Birmingham, Alabama](/places/birmingham). 2350 15th Ave N, Birmingham, AL 35234, USA · [coca-colaamphitheater.com](https://www.coca-colaamphitheater.com)*
