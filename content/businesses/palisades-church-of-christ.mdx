For over fifty years, Palisades Church of Christ has anchored the Palisades community in Birmingham with steady faith and genuine fellowship. Located on Palisades Boulevard, this congregation welcomes folks seeking a straightforward approach to Scripture and Christian living. Their doors stay open for worship, study, and service to neighbors near and far. In a neighborhood known for strong community ties, Palisades remains a gathering place where roots run deep.
