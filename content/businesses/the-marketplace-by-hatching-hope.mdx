At The Marketplace by Hatching Hope in Alabaster, local artisans gather to share their handmade goods with neighbors who appreciate honest craftsmanship. You'll find everything from jewelry to home décor, each piece carrying the mark of someone's careful hands. It's the kind of place where you discover something you didn't know you needed. Alabaster's growing community of makers and shoppers keeps these small spaces thriving along Shady Acres Road.

*The Marketplace by Hatching Hope is based in [Alabaster, Alabama](/places/alabaster). 376 Shady Acres Rd, Alabaster, AL 35007, USA · [facebook.com/themarketplacebyhatchinghope](https://www.facebook.com/themarketplacebyhatchinghope) · (205) 624-2135*
