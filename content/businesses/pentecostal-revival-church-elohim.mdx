In Alabaster, folks gather at Pentecostal Revival Church Elohim to worship and find community along US-31. The church opens its doors to believers seeking spiritual renewal through traditional Pentecostal faith and fellowship. Pastor Raul Polanco leads the congregation in prayer, praise, and biblical teaching that speaks to the heart. Alabaster itself has grown into a welcoming suburb where neighbors still know each other's names.
