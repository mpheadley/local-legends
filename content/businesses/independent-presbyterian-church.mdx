Independent Presbyterian Church has anchored Highland Avenue in Birmingham since 1874, welcoming folks through its doors with straightforward faith and genuine community. The congregation gathers for worship that honors tradition while staying connected to the neighborhood around them. You'll find them invested in local service and conversation that matters. Highland Avenue itself tells the story of Birmingham's evolution, lined with historic buildings that reflect generations of change.
