You'll find bold Indian cooking from a food truck parked on Birmingham's southside. Sahani Kitchen slings curries, biryanis, and street food that'll surprise you if you're used to playing it safe at dinner. The team knows their spices and doesn't apologize for heat or flavor. Fourth Avenue South keeps growing with spots like this, drawing folks who want something real and different from what they've eaten before.

*Sahani Kitchen is based in [Birmingham, Alabama](/places/birmingham). 2520 4th Ave S, Birmingham, AL 35233, USA · (205) 203-4250*
