First Memorial Baptist Church has served Birmingham's northside for generations, rooted in faith and community care. Located on 15th Avenue North, this congregation gathers to worship, support one another, and reach out to neighbors in need. Their doors stay open for those seeking spiritual fellowship and practical help. In a neighborhood that's seen its share of changes, First Memorial remains a steady anchor for folks looking to belong.

*First Memorial Baptist Church is based in [Birmingham, Alabama](/places/birmingham). 3012 15th Ave N, Birmingham, AL 35234, USA · [firstmemorialbc.org](http://www.firstmemorialbc.org) · (205) 254-8006*
