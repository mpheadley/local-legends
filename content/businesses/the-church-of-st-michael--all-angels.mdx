The Church of St. Michael & All Angels has anchored downtown Anniston since 1888, serving as both a spiritual home and a gathering place for the community. This Episcopal congregation welcomes visitors through its historic doors, offering traditional worship and fellowship. The church remains committed to service work and charitable giving that extends well beyond Sunday mornings, making it a steady presence in Anniston's civic and spiritual life.

*The Church of St. Michael & All Angels is based in [Anniston, Alabama](/places/anniston). 1000 W 18th St, Anniston, AL 36201, USA · (256) 237-4011 · [www.stmichaelsanniston.org](http://www.stmichaelsanniston.org)*
