Anniston First United Methodist Church has served Noble Street for generations, opening its doors to folks seeking spiritual community and meaningful worship. You'll find them at 1400 Noble Street, ready to make newcomers feel at home. Anniston itself carries a rich industrial heritage that shaped families and neighborhoods throughout the region.
