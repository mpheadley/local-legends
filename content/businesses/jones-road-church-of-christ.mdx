Sitting on Jones Road outside Athens, this church of Christ congregation has been gathering folks for worship and fellowship for generations. They keep to the fundamentals: scripture, prayer, and community rooted in faith. If you're passing through the area or looking for a straightforward place of worship, they welcome visitors most any Sunday. Athens itself sits in the heart of North Alabama's rolling farmland, where church life still anchors the rhythm of small-town living.
