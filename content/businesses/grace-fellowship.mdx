Grace Fellowship welcomes folks from all walks of life to gather on Summerall Gate Road in Anniston. The church centers its ministry on Scripture and community, offering a place where neighbors connect over shared faith. You'll find honest preaching and genuine fellowship on Sunday mornings. Anniston itself sits nestled in the foothills of East Alabama, a town with deep roots and a strong sense of place.
