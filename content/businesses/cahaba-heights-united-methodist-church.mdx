Cahaba Heights United Methodist Church has been a gathering place for Birmingham families since 1963. Located on Cahaba Heights Road, the church offers Sunday worship, youth programs, and community service opportunities rooted in Methodist tradition. Whether you're seeking spiritual community or looking to get involved locally, they welcome newcomers year-round. The Cahaba Heights neighborhood itself reflects Birmingham's steady growth as families continue choosing these tree-lined streets for home.
