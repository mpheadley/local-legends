Connie Lee's Soul Food Truck parks on Teresa Drive serving up the kind of cooking that sticks with you. The menu carries straight-ahead favorites: fried chicken, collards, mac and cheese, cornbread. You order at the window, grab your plate hot, and eat like somebody who knows what matters. Birmingham's neighborhoods run deep with stories, and this food truck holds its own piece of that history.
