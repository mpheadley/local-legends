Fresh vegetables hit the table at Evelyn's Produce, a farmers market vendor on Birmingham's west side where customers know what they're getting: quality produce picked with care. Owner Evelyn stocks what grows well here, keeping prices honest and selections seasonal. You'll find her at 344 Finley Ave W, connecting neighbors with real food. In this part of Birmingham, local businesses like hers keep the community fed and rooted.
