Elliottsville Church of Christ in Alabaster has been welcoming neighbors through its doors for generations, offering a place where faith and community intertwine. Located on Highway 119, this congregation gathers to study Scripture and support one another through life's joys and struggles. Their straightforward approach to worship reflects the values that have sustained rural Alabama churches for decades. Alabaster itself sits in the heart of Shelby County, a growing area that honors its roots while looking forward.
