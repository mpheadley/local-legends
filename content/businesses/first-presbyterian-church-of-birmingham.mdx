Since 1874, First Presbyterian Church has anchored Birmingham's spiritual life on Fourth Avenue North. The congregation gathers in a historic sanctuary where generations have worshipped, found community, and worked toward justice. Their doors stay open to anyone seeking connection and meaning in faith. In a city built on steel and determination, this church stands as a quiet constant.

*First Presbyterian Church Of Birmingham is based in [Birmingham, Alabama](/places/birmingham). 2100 4th Ave N, Birmingham, AL 35203, USA · [fpcbham.org](http://www.fpcbham.org) · (205) 322-5469*
