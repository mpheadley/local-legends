Rolling through Bessemer with a menu that honors real Southern cooking, Granny's Fish 'N Grits serves up catfish and shrimp alongside their namesake grits, just the way folks around here have always wanted them. You'll find this food truck at 5031 Ford Parkway, where the portions are honest and the flavors don't need explaining. Bessemer's working neighborhoods have long relied on good, straightforward food that sticks to your ribs.
