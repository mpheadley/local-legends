You'll find genuine Mexican food parked on 5th Avenue in Attalla, where Tienda y Taqueria MAYA III serves up tacos, tamales, and fresh-made specialties from a food truck that's become a fixture in the community. Owner-operated and family-focused, they keep things simple and honest, letting quality ingredients do the talking. Their website and phone line make ordering easy for folks who know what they're after. Attalla's been hungry for this kind of authentic neighborhood cooking.
