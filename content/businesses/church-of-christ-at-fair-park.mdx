The congregation welcomes visitors through its doors with genuine hospitality and straightforward biblical teaching. Whether you're seeking a welcoming faith community or reconnecting with your roots, you'll find thoughtful people gathered around shared values. In a neighborhood shaped by working people and deep-rooted faith, this church remains a quiet cornerstone of community life.
