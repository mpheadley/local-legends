On Ferron Avenue in Anniston, West Anniston Church of Christ opens its doors to neighbors seeking faith and fellowship. The congregation gathers to study scripture, worship together, and support one another through life's seasons. Their commitment to community runs deep, rooted in Christian values that have guided their members for generations. Anniston itself holds a rich history, from its industrial past to its transformation into a cultural hub for east Alabama.
