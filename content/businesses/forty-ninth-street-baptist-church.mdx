Forty Ninth Street Baptist Church has served Anniston's West End since 1906, standing as a spiritual anchor through generations of change. The congregation gathers in fellowship and faith, rooted in the traditions that have sustained Black communities across Alabama. Their doors open wide to neighbors seeking community and purpose. West Anniston itself carries the stories of working families who built their lives around these very streets and churches.

*Forty Ninth Street Baptist Church is based in [Anniston, Alabama](/places/anniston). 1104 W 49th St, Anniston, AL 36206, USA · [facebook.com/49thstreetbaptist](https://www.facebook.com/49thstreetbaptist) · (256) 237-0941*
