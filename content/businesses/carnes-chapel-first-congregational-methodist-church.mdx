Carnes Chapel First Congregational Methodist Church has served the Attalla community since 1887, offering a quiet place for worship and fellowship along a country road. The congregation gathers to study scripture, pray together, and support their neighbors with the kind of steady faith that's marked their pews for generations. You'll find them on Facebook sharing updates and welcoming visitors who want to join their Sunday services. Attalla sits in Etowah County, where the foothills meet industrial heritage and small-town resilience.

*Carnes Chapel First Congregational Methodist Church is based in [Attalla, Alabama](/places/attalla). 41 Carnes Chapel Rd, Attalla, AL 35954, USA · [facebook.com/CARNESFCM](https://www.facebook.com/CARNESFCM)*
