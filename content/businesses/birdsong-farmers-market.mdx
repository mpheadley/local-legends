Every Saturday at Birdsong Farmers Market in Birmingham, you'll find local growers selling what they've pulled from the earth that week. The vendors know their soil, their seeds, and their neighbors. You get vegetables picked fresh, not shipped in from somewhere else, and you get to talk to the people who grew them. In this part of Birmingham, that kind of connection still matters.
