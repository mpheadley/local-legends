Fat Daddy's brings live music to Auburn every night, drawing folks who want real songs from real bands instead of recorded tracks. You'll find cold beer, friendly faces, and the kind of atmosphere where strangers become friends by the second round. The stage might be small, but the sound fills the whole room. Auburn's Lee Road corridor has become a gathering spot where students and locals mix without pretense.
