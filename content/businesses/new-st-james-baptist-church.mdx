New St James Baptist Church has anchored Birmingham's Fourth Avenue since the early twentieth century, serving as a spiritual home and gathering place for generations of worshippers. Located at 600 4th Ave N, the congregation continues its tradition of faith and community support in the heart of the city. Birmingham's Fourth Avenue remains a vital corridor of African American history and culture.
