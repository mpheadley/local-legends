Ono Grill sushi Hibachi & Chinese Food operates out of Anniston as a food truck and market, bringing fresh takes on Japanese and Chinese cuisine to folks around town. They handle sushi rolls, hibachi-cooked meals, and Chinese standards with care. It's the kind of place that fills a real gap in what Anniston can eat on any given day, offering something different from the usual restaurant circuit without pretension.
