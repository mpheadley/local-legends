For over a century, Camp Branch United Methodist Church has anchored the Alabaster community with steady worship and genuine fellowship. Members gather in this modest sanctuary to pray, sing, and support one another through life's seasons. The church opens its doors to anyone seeking spiritual connection and a place to belong. Alabaster itself has grown into a vibrant suburb south of Birmingham, where longtime residents and newcomers alike call home.
