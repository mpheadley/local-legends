Edgewood Presbyterian Church has served Birmingham's Oxmoor Road community since 1949, offering worship, fellowship, and service rooted in faith and neighbor care. The church welcomes all who seek spiritual growth and connection, gathering regularly for Sunday services and community outreach. Whether you're looking for a church home or curious about Presbyterian traditions in Alabama, Edgewood invites you in. Birmingham's diverse neighborhoods each tell stories of faith and resilience.
