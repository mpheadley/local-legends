Live bands shake the walls most nights at SAW's Juke Joint on Dunston Avenue, where barbecue smoke and good music go hand in hand. You'll find folks from all corners crowded around tables, cold drinks in hand, watching performers bring soul to the stage. The joint keeps Birmingham's juke tradition alive, proving that some neighborhoods still know how to gather and celebrate together.

*SAW’s Juke Joint is based in [Birmingham, Alabama](/places/birmingham). 1115 Dunston Ave, Birmingham, AL 35213, USA · [sawsbbq.com](http://www.sawsbbq.com) · (205) 745-3920*
