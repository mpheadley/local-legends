# Bloom and Petal

In a strip mall off Highway 280, Bloom and Petal turns wedding dreams into arrangements that actually last. Owner knows flowers like a good baker knows bread, which means she listens more than she talks and lets your vision guide the design. They'll work with your budget without making you feel cheap about it. Birmingham's South Huffman neighborhood keeps growing, and this florist has quietly become the place couples trust with their big day.
