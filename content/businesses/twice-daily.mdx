You can stop by Twice Daily in Athens for thoughtfully curated goods that change with the seasons. This pop-up shop on Highway 72 keeps things fresh by rotating what's available, so regulars know to check back often. The selection spans home goods, gifts, and finds you won't see everywhere else. Athens itself has quietly become a destination for folks seeking something different from the usual chain stores.
