On Highway 119 in Alabaster, Champy's Famous Fried Chicken serves up golden, crispy chicken while live bands fill the dining room with real music. It's the kind of place where families gather on weekends, where the food is straightforward and good, and where somebody's always got a guitar in hand. The chicken's been drawing folks for years, and the live entertainment keeps them coming back. Alabaster's got a way of holding onto spots like this.
