On a Thursday night at J & R Bar & Grill, you'll find folks shoulder to shoulder, nursing drinks while a live band fills the room with everything from country to rock. Located on Graymont Avenue, this Birmingham spot keeps things simple: good food, cold beer, and music that makes you forget what day it is. The neighborhood around it has seen better days, but places like this remind you why people stick around.
