Since 1927, Anniston Country Club has hosted golfers and gatherings on Highland Avenue. The eighteen-hole course sits pretty in this historic mining town, offering members a place to play and socialize across generations. It's where locals know your name and the fairways roll through Alabama's Piedmont landscape. Anniston itself carries the character of an industrial past transformed into a community that values its green spaces and neighborly traditions.
