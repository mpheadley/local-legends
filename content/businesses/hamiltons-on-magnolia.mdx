On Magnolia Avenue in Auburn, Hamilton's food truck serves up honest cooking that draws folks back again and again. The menu changes with the seasons, keeping regulars guessing what's coming next. You'll find the truck parked at 174 E Magnolia, where the owners treat every customer like family. Auburn's tree-lined streets and college-town energy make this stretch a natural gathering spot for hungry neighbors.
