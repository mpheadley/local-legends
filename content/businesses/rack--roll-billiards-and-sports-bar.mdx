Rack & Roll Billiards and Sports Bar in Anniston gives folks a straightforward place to shoot pool, watch the game, and grab a meal. They run a food truck operation alongside their brick-and-mortar spot, bringing their menu around town. It's the kind of casual gathering spot where neighbors run into each other, where pool tournaments happen on weekends, and where the burgers are honest and filling. Simple as that.

*Rack & Roll Billiards and Sports Bar is based in [Anniston, Alabama](/places/anniston). 917 Noble St, Anniston, AL 36201, USA · (256) 624-6434 · [www.rackandrollbilliards.com](https://www.rackandrollbilliards.com)*
