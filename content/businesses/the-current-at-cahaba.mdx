The Current at Cahaba serves up wood-fired food from a Birmingham food truck that knows how to do things right. Their menu changes with the seasons and what's fresh, keeping things honest and straightforward. You'll find them parked at 4500 5th Ave S, turning out meals that taste like someone actually cares about the ingredients. This corner of Birmingham has quietly become a destination for folks who want real food without the fuss.

*The Current at Cahaba is based in [Birmingham, Alabama](/places/birmingham). 4500 5th Ave S Building C, Birmingham, AL 35222, USA · [thecurrentatcahaba.com](https://thecurrentatcahaba.com) · (205) 578-2616*
