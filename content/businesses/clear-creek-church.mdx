Clear Creek Church has been a gathering place for Attalla families for generations, offering worship and community to those seeking spiritual connection. Located on Gallant Road, the church welcomes neighbors through its doors with genuine warmth and steady faith. The congregation shares meals, celebrates milestones, and supports one another through life's ups and downs. Attalla itself remains a tight-knit town where neighbors know each other's names and show up when it matters most.
