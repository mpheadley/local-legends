For over a century, First United Methodist Church of Alabaster has welcomed neighbors through its doors on Highway 119, offering Sunday worship, youth programs, and community service rooted in Methodist tradition. The church grounds provide space for prayer, fellowship, and spiritual growth across generations. Whether you're seeking a church home or curious about local faith communities, you'll find genuine hospitality here. Alabaster itself has grown from a quiet crossroads into a vibrant suburban town while keeping its small-town character.
