Golden Springs Baptist Church has served Anniston's Robertson Road community for generations, offering a place where neighbors gather for worship and fellowship. Whether you're looking for Sunday service or a welcoming church home, Golden Springs remains rooted in the values that have held this Calhoun County town together for decades.
