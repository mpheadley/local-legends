Grant Street Baptist Church has been a steady presence in Bessemer since 1892, offering worship and community to generations of families in this industrial town. The congregation gathers at their Grant Street location to study scripture, support one another through life's challenges, and serve their neighbors. Whether you're longtime Bessemer or just passing through, you'll find a welcoming faith community here. Bessemer's working-class roots run deep, and Grant Street Baptist reflects the resilience that defines the city.
