For nearly half a century, Auburn United Methodist Church has been teaching little ones their ABCs and how to get along with others. Located on East Magnolia Avenue, the preschool gives families a faith-centered place where children learn through play and exploration. Teachers here know their students' names and their parents by heart. Auburn itself has grown up around the church, a college town where traditions run deep and neighbors still know each other.
