Live music fills the nights at Skybar Café on Auburn's downtown strip, where locals gather to hear everything from country to rock. The intimate rooftop venue keeps the focus where it belongs: on the band and the people around you. Whether you're catching a Friday night show or stopping by for a drink, you'll find the kind of place that makes Auburn's college town feel like home.

*Skybar Café is based in [Auburn, Alabama](/places/auburn). 136 W Magnolia Ave, Auburn, AL 36830, USA · [auburnskybar.com](http://www.auburnskybar.com) · (334) 734-5155*
