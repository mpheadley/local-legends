Word Alive in Oxford helps people grow stronger readers and writers through classes and community programs. They work with kids, adults, and families who want to improve their skills or discover they actually enjoy learning to read. In a region where literacy matters for everything from jobs to citizenship, Word Alive fills a real gap, meeting folks where they are without judgment.
