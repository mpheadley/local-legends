El Puentecito is a mobile food operation in Oxford serving authentic Mexican cuisine from a truck. Owner Maria Gutierrez prepares traditional recipes passed down through her family, offering everything from fresh tamales to carne asada tacos. The operation has become a gathering spot for folks craving genuine flavors and honest cooking. In a town where options matter, El Puentecito fills a real need with food that tastes like it comes from somewhere true.
