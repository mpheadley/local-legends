Pull up to Niffer's Place on Opelika Road and you'll find honest food cooked fresh in a food truck that's become a gathering spot for Auburn folks. The menu keeps things simple and satisfying, with the kind of meals that stick with you. Owner Niffer treats customers like neighbors, remembering regulars and their orders. Auburn's got a way of making these kinds of places feel like home.
