In Alabaster, Cultivate Church gathers folks seeking honest conversation about faith and life. Located on First Street, they focus on building community rather than performing religion, meeting people where they actually are. The church welcomes questions and doubt alongside belief, creating space for real growth. Alabaster itself has grown into a thriving bedroom community just south of Birmingham, where neighbors still know each other's names.
