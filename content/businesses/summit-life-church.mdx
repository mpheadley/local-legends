On Long 12th Street in Bessemer, Summit Life Church opens its doors to folks looking for genuine connection and spiritual grounding. They focus on honest worship and real community, meeting people where they are rather than where they think they should be. The church holds services throughout the week, inviting neighbors to explore faith together. Bessemer's working neighborhoods have long been places where people pull together through hard times and celebration alike.
