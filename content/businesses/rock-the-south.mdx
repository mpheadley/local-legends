Rock The South brings live music to Jacksonville with a straightforward mission: good bands, good beer, good times. Since opening, they've become a gathering spot where neighbors catch local acts and touring musicians who might otherwise skip Northeast Alabama. The venue matters because live music tends to follow money toward bigger cities, but Rock The South insisted on staying put, giving the community something worth staying home for.

*Rock The South is based in [Jacksonville, Alabama](/places/jacksonville). 610 Church Ave SE, Jacksonville, AL 36265, USA · [rockthesouth.com](http://rockthesouth.com)*
