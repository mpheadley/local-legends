For over forty years, Just For You | Martie's Formalwear has dressed Bessemer brides in their special-day finest. Located on Fourth Avenue, this family-run shop stocks wedding gowns, bridesmaids' dresses, and formal wear while offering alterations to get the fit just right. The team knows that finding the perfect dress matters, and they take time with every customer who walks through the door. Bessemer's historic downtown continues drawing folks who value that kind of personal attention.
