Hot House Design creates wedding flowers that feel genuine, grown from the designers' real conversations with couples about what matters to them. Located on Robert Jemison Road, they build arrangements that tell your story rather than follow a template. Their work shows up in Birmingham celebrations where folks want something that feels distinctly theirs. The shop sits in a neighborhood where local makers and artists have been quietly shaping the city's character for years.

*Hot House Design Llc is based in [Birmingham, Alabama](/places/birmingham). 89 Robert Jemison Rd, Birmingham, AL 35209, USA · [hothousedesignstudio.com](https://hothousedesignstudio.com) · (205) 324-2663*
