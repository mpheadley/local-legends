Roll up to Coldwater Mountain Brewpub on Walnut Avenue in Anniston, where a food truck serves up good eating and cold beer without the fuss. The folks here keep things straightforward: quality brews, solid food, and a genuine welcome. You'll find locals and newcomers alike grabbing a bite and a drink in the shade. Anniston's got that small-town character where a place like this becomes part of the neighborhood's backbone.

*Coldwater Mountain Brewpub is based in [Anniston, Alabama](/places/anniston). 1208 Walnut Ave, Anniston, AL 36201, USA · [drinkcoldwater.com](http://drinkcoldwater.com) · (256) 403-2334*
