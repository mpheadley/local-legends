Red Mountain Church welcomes folks from all walks of life to worship together in Birmingham's diverse Southside neighborhood. Located on 7th Avenue South, this congregation focuses on genuine community and honest faith without pretense. They gather Sundays for service and stay connected through the week via their website and phone line. Birmingham's Red Mountain area has long been a crossroads where working people of different backgrounds have built lives side by side.

*Red Mountain Church is based in [Birmingham, Alabama](/places/birmingham). 2829 7th Ave S, Birmingham, AL 35233, USA · [redmountainchurch.org](http://www.redmountainchurch.org) · (205) 202-5682*
