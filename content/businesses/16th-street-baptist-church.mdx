A sacred place where faith endured through Birmingham's darkest hour, the 16th Street Baptist Church still stands on 6th Avenue North as a testament to resilience. The 1963 bombing that killed four young girls shook the nation, yet this congregation rose to keep their doors open and their spirits whole. Visitors come to remember, to pray, and to understand the cost of the civil rights movement. Downtown Birmingham's streets tell stories of struggle and survival.

*16th Street Baptist Church is based in [Birmingham, Alabama](/places/birmingham). 1530 6th Ave N, Birmingham, AL 35203, USA · [16thstreetbaptist.org](http://16thstreetbaptist.org) · (205) 251-9402*
