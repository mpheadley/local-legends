For over forty years, Riverchase Church of Christ has been a gathering place for folks seeking community and faith along Montgomery Highway in Birmingham. The church welcomes visitors to worship services, Bible studies, and fellowship events throughout the week. Whether you're new to the area or looking for a church home, they're focused on honest conversation about Scripture and what it means to live it out. Birmingham's Riverchase neighborhood draws families looking for roots and connection.
