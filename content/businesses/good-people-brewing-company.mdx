Down on 14th Street in Birmingham, Good People Brewing Company slings beer and food from their food truck with the kind of friendly service that makes strangers feel like regulars. They've built something honest in a neighborhood that's always been about real people doing real work. The beer tastes like it matters, and the vibe says you belong there. This stretch of Birmingham keeps proving it knows how to gather folks around something worth sharing.

*Good People Brewing Company is based in [Birmingham, Alabama](/places/birmingham). 114 14th St S, Birmingham, AL 35233, USA · [goodpeoplebrewing.com](http://www.goodpeoplebrewing.com)*
