Oxford Commons is a pop-up shop bringing locally made goods and regional crafts to Oxford. Run by folks who believe their small town deserves access to quality products from nearby makers, the shop appears seasonally with rotating inventory. It's become a gathering spot where neighbors reconnect and discover what their own region produces. For a community often overlooked, Oxford Commons reminds people that good things are being made right here.

*Oxford Commons is based in [Oxford, Alabama](/places/oxford). Leon Smith Pkwy, Oxford, AL 36203, USA*
