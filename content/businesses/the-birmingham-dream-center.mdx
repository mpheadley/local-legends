On Birmingham's north side, the Dream Center opens its doors to folks who need a hand up. This church gathers neighbors around food, job training, and community support that actually makes a difference in people's lives. They work quietly, showing up for their community day after day. The Highlands neighborhood has seen hard times, but places like this remind you what real faith looks like in action.
