Bush Hills Church of Christ has served Birmingham's Fourth Avenue West neighborhood since its founding, offering a place where folks gather for worship and community. Located at 1400 4th Ave W, the church welcomes visitors through its doors every week. The congregation focuses on scripture and fellowship, staying rooted in traditional Christian values. Fourth Avenue West remains one of Birmingham's most historically significant neighborhoods, where faith and family have always mattered most.
