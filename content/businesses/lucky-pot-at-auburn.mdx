Lucky Pot rolls up to Auburn with steaming pots of Asian comfort food that'll stick to your ribs. You'll find them parked on Opelika Road, serving noodles, rice bowls, and hot pots that taste like someone's grandmother knew what she was doing. Stop by for lunch between classes or grab dinner after a long day. Auburn's got that college-town energy where good food trucks become gathering spots for students and locals alike.

*Lucky Pot at Auburn is based in [Auburn, Alabama](/places/auburn). 1780 Opelika Rd Suite103, Auburn, AL 36830, USA · [luckypotauburn.com](https://luckypotauburn.com) · (334) 246-3100*
