Live music and cocktails meet in a Birmingham basement where the band plays loud and the drinks pour honest. Pogo Cocktail Bar doesn't fuss with fancy presentations, just good spirits and better company. You'll find locals shoulder to shoulder, dancing when the mood strikes. The Lakeview neighborhood has transformed into a genuine gathering place, where people still show up for real entertainment and each other.
