In the Ensley neighborhood of Birmingham, Greater New Beginning Apostolic Church has served its community for decades with straightforward faith and action. Located on Avenue L, the church opens its doors to folks seeking spiritual fellowship and practical support. Pastor and congregation members work together to strengthen the neighborhood through worship and service. Ensley, like much of Birmingham, reflects the resilience and hope that define working people across Alabama.
