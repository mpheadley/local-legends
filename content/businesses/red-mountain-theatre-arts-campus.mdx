You'll find genuine bluegrass music and theater arts thriving together at Red Mountain Theatre Arts Campus in Birmingham. This outfit brings the raw energy of traditional string music to life while offering classes and performances that keep old-time traditions alive. Whether you're catching a show or learning to pick a banjo, you're supporting folks who believe in passing down what matters. Red Mountain sits right in the heart of Birmingham, where the city's arts scene keeps growing stronger every year.

*Red Mountain Theatre Arts Campus is based in [Birmingham, Alabama](/places/birmingham). 1600 3rd Ave S, Birmingham, AL 35233, USA · [redmountaintheatre.org](https://www.redmountaintheatre.org) · (205) 324-2424*
