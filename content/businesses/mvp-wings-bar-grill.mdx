Out of a food truck on West 10th Street, MVP Wings Bar & Grill serves up wings and grill fare that draws folks from across Anniston. The operation keeps things simple: good food, fair prices, and the kind of service that makes regulars feel welcome. Owner and crew know their neighbors by name and remember how you like your order. Anniston's working neighborhoods depend on spots like this, where real people gather over real food.

*MVP Wings Bar & Grill is based in [Anniston, Alabama](/places/anniston). 916 W 10th St, Anniston, AL 36201, USA · (256) 770-3919*
