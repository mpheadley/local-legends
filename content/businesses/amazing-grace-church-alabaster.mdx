Tucked along Highway 3 in Alabaster, Amazing Grace Church opens its doors to folks seeking genuine fellowship and straightforward faith. The congregation gathers to worship together, drawing from Christian tradition while staying rooted in their community. Whether you're a longtime member or just passing through, you'll find a welcoming place to sit a spell. Alabaster itself has grown from a quiet crossroads into a thriving suburban community where families put down roots.
