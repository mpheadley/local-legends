Hemley Road Church of Christ in Bayou La Batre opens its doors to neighbors in need, offering food pantry services that help folks put meals on the table. Staffed by church volunteers who know their community well, the pantry hands out groceries without fuss or judgment. Located on Hemley Street, it's a quiet place where people take care of people. Bayou La Batre sits along the Alabama coast, where shrimping boats still work the water and tight-knit communities look after their own.
