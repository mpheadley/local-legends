For over fifty years, North Shelby Church of Christ has gathered folks in the Cahaba Valley community to worship and serve. The congregation focuses on Bible study, fellowship, and helping neighbors through practical ministry. Members emphasize spiritual growth rooted in Scripture and genuine connection. Their website offers sermon archives and service information for those looking to join. Birmingham's North Shelby area reflects the steady growth of suburban faith communities across Alabama's heartland.
