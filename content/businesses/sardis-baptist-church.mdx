Sardis Missionary Baptist Church has been a spiritual home for Birmingham's West Side community for generations. Located on 4th Court West, this congregation gathers to worship, support one another, and root themselves in faith and fellowship. Their doors open for those seeking connection and purpose through the traditions of the Black church. Birmingham's neighborhoods tell stories of resilience and belonging, especially in the spaces where people gather to lift each other up.
