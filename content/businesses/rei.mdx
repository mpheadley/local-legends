You can gear up for Alabama's great outdoors at this REI pop-up shop on Summit Boulevard. Whether you're planning a hiking trip in the Talladega range or a weekend paddle down the Cahaba River, you'll find quality equipment and folks who know the local trails. Stop by and talk with staff who understand what gear actually matters for our climate and terrain. Birmingham's Summit area keeps growing as a neighborhood where locals shop and gather.
