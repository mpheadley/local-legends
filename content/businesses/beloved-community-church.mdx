On 41st Street in Birmingham, Beloved Community Church gathers folks around faith and justice work. They're rooted in the belief that church should show up for neighbors, whether that's through community meals, advocacy, or just being present where people hurt. You'll find them wrestling with hard questions about what it means to live out your beliefs. In a city still learning its way toward healing, congregations like this one keep trying.
