Saint Francis Xavier Catholic Church has served Birmingham's faithful since 1892, anchoring the city's Southside with steady spiritual guidance. The brick sanctuary holds generations of worship, from quiet morning prayers to celebrations that fill the pews. Their doors stay open to anyone seeking community and connection. In a neighborhood that's always changing, this church remains a reliable landmark where folks know they're welcome.
