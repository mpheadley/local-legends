In Alabaster, you'll find The Marketplace by Hatching Hope, where local artisans gather to sell their handmade goods. It's a straightforward spot for craft vendors at 376 Shady Acres Road, a place where makers can share what they create. Whether you're hunting for unique gifts or supporting neighbors' work, this is where to look. Alabaster itself sits just south of Birmingham, a growing community that values the kinds of personal connections these craft spaces build.
