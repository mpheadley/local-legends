M & J Home Cooking Oxford serves up real Southern food from their mobile kitchen, the kind that tastes like somebody's grandmother made it. They focus on honest meals—fried chicken, collard greens, cornbread—prepared fresh and sold at a price that doesn't empty your wallet. In a time when fast food dominates, they're keeping traditional home cooking alive in Oxford, one plate at a time.
