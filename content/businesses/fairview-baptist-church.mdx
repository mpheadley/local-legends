Out on Nick Davis Road near Athens, Fairview Baptist Church has been a steady place for worship and community for generations. The congregation gathers to study Scripture, pray together, and support their neighbors in the way rural churches have always done. You'll find them genuine and welcoming, the kind of folks who remember your name and ask how your family's doing. In this corner of Madison County, small churches like Fairview remain the heartbeat of their communities.
