Since 1974, St James United Methodist Church has been a steady presence on Grants Mill Road, offering worship and community to Birmingham's families. The church welcomes folks looking for traditional Methodist fellowship and service rooted in faith. Sunday services bring neighbors together for prayer, music, and reflection. In a city constantly growing and changing, St James remains a familiar landmark where people have gathered for generations.
