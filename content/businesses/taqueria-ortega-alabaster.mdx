From a food truck parked on Industrial Road in Alabaster, Taqueria Ortega serves authentic Mexican fare that draws folks from across the county. The kitchen turns out fresh tacos, tamales, and other traditional dishes made right there, day after day. Owner and cook pride themselves on quality ingredients and recipes passed down through family. Alabaster's industrial corridor has found an unexpected gathering spot where good food brings neighbors together.

*Taqueria Ortega is based in [Alabaster, Alabama](/places/alabaster). 151 Industrial Rd, Alabaster, AL 35007, USA*
