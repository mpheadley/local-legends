Autaugaville Methodist Church has anchored the spiritual life of this small Alabama town since its founding. Located on North Autauga Street, the church continues serving its congregation and community with the steady faithfulness characteristic of rural Methodist traditions. Worship here reflects generations of Autaugaville families gathering to mark life's passages and seasons. The church stands as a quiet witness to the enduring values that have sustained this close-knit town through changing times.

*Autaugaville Methodist Church is based in [Autaugaville, Alabama](/places/autaugaville). 208 N Autauga St, Autaugaville, AL 36003, USA*
