On Randolph Street in Attalla, the Church of Love opens its doors to folks seeking spiritual community and fellowship. This congregation gathers to worship, pray, and support one another through life's ups and downs, the way churches have done in small Alabama towns for generations. Attalla itself sits nestled in St. Clair County, a working community where neighbors still know each other's names.

*Church Of Love is based in [Attalla, Alabama](/places/attalla). 416 Randolph St SE, Attalla, AL 35954, USA*
