Rocky Creek United Methodist Church has anchored the Ashford community since its founding, offering a gathering place where neighbors worship and share life's important moments. The church sits quietly on Creek Church Road, a testament to the enduring faith that runs deep through rural Alabama. Its white walls and simple steeple reflect the humble spirituality of the people who call this place home. Ashford itself remains a small, tight-knit community where such churches serve as the heartbeat of local life.

*Rocky Creek United Methodist Church is based in [Ashford, Alabama](/places/ashford). Creek Church Rd, Ashford, AL 36312, USA*
