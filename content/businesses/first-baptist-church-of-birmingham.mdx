Sitting on the shores of Lake Lakeshore Drive, First Baptist Church of Birmingham has welcomed worshippers since its founding. The church serves its congregation through traditional services, community outreach, and spiritual guidance rooted in faith and fellowship. Visitors can learn more by calling (205) 870-3888 or visiting fbcbirmingham.org. Birmingham's lakeside neighborhoods offer peaceful retreats from the bustling city center.
