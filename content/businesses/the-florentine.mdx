If you're getting married in Birmingham, The Florentine gives you a real building with character, not another cookie-cutter event space. Housed in a 1927 structure on Second Avenue North, it's got the bones and bones-deep charm that make for good photos and better memories. The exposed brick, high ceilings, and natural light do most of the heavy lifting. Second Avenue's got the heart of Birmingham beating through it these days.
