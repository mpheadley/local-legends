Oxford Performing Arts Center brings live music to the heart of Calhoun County, hosting regional and touring bands in an intimate setting that feels like home. The venue matters because it gives music lovers a place to gather and gives working musicians a stage. Whether you're after country, rock, or blues, you'll find real performances by real artists in a room where you can actually hear what's happening.

*Oxford Performing Arts Center is based in [Oxford, Alabama](/places/oxford). 100 Choccolocco St #200, Oxford, AL 36203, USA · (256) 241-3322 · [www.oxfordpac.org](https://www.oxfordpac.org)*
