If you're looking for real country music played by folks who mean it, head out Lucas Ferry Road to Johnny's Place. They've got the kind of band that plays what they know, and people show up on weekends ready to dance and carry on. It's the sort of spot where everybody knows your name and the music sounds like home. Athens keeps plenty of surprises tucked along its backroads.
