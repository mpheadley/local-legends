When a Birmingham couple decides to marry, they often find their way to Delights from Delore Bakery on Court I. The shop crafts custom wedding cakes that taste as good as they look, working closely with each couple to bring their vision to life. Owner Delore takes pride in quality ingredients and attention to detail. In a city where traditions matter, this bakery has become part of how families celebrate their biggest moments.
