Table Matters on Montevallo Road helps Birmingham brides find dresses that feel like them, not like somebody else's dream. The shop takes time with each woman who walks through the door, listening to what matters and what doesn't. You'll find gowns in all styles and sizes, handled by folks who actually know their way around a wedding dress. Montevallo Road itself has long been where Birmingham families shop for what they need.
