Mt Hebron Baptist Church has been rooted in Birmingham's North End since 1911, serving as a spiritual home and gathering place for generations of families. The church opens its doors for worship, fellowship, and community events that bring neighbors together around shared faith and values. You'll find their calendar full of programs that speak to the rhythms of church life in the Deep South. North Birmingham has always relied on its churches as anchors of hope and connection.

*Mt Hebron Baptist Church is based in [Birmingham, Alabama](/places/birmingham). 1620 24th Ave N, Birmingham, AL 35204, USA · [mthebron.org/events](https://www.mthebron.org/events) · (205) 324-0944*
