Out on Buck Island Road near Athens, the Limestone Sheriff's Rodeo Arena hosts country bands and keeps the spirit of rodeo alive in North Alabama. It's where folks gather for genuine honky-tonk nights and old-fashioned good times under the stars. The bands play loud, the crowd gets rowdy, and nobody's pretending to be anything they're not. Athens remains a place where rural traditions still hold their ground.
