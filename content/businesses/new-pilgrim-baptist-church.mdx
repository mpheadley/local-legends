New Pilgrim Baptist Church has been serving Birmingham's southside for generations, offering a place where neighbors gather to worship and support one another. Located on Goldwire Place, the church remains rooted in faith and community, welcoming folks through its doors every week. The congregation carries on the Baptist tradition that shaped so much of Birmingham's spiritual life. In this part of the city, churches like New Pilgrim anchor neighborhoods and keep folks connected.
