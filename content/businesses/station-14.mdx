When game day rolls around in Auburn, Station 14 fires up their mobile grill and feeds the hungry crowds. You'll find them slinging barbecue and comfort food that sticks to your ribs, the kind of cooking that tastes like somebody actually cares. They're parked at 3050 Alabama Highway 14 West, ready to handle whatever appetite you bring. Auburn's got plenty of places to eat, but this is where folks go when they want food that feels like home.
