St. Charles Catholic Church in Jacksonville has served the faithful since 1890, offering Mass and sacraments to Catholics across Northeast Alabama. The parish operates a school educating children kindergarten through eighth grade and hosts community events throughout the year. Whether you're a longtime member or new to the area, St. Charles welcomes visitors to worship, learn, and connect with neighbors who share their faith.
