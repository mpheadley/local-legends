Effina's food truck on Noble Street serves up honest cooking that folks in Anniston have come to depend on. You'll find real flavors and straightforward meals that don't pretend to be anything fancier than they are. The truck keeps regular hours and prices that won't leave your wallet hurting. Noble Street has long been where Anniston's working people gather and eat.

*Effina's is based in [Anniston, Alabama](/places/anniston). 919 Noble St, Anniston, AL 36201, USA · [effinas.com](http://effinas.com) · (256) 770-4830*
