Briarwood Presbyterian Church has been a steady presence in Birmingham since 1973, grounded in Reformed theology and a commitment to Scripture. Located on Briarwood Way, the church gathers folks for worship, teaching, and community rooted in faith. They're part of the Presbyterian Church in America, a denomination focused on biblical preaching and discipleship. In the heart of Birmingham's south side, Briarwood reflects the steady faith that anchors neighborhoods across Alabama.
