Live music happens nightly at Oasis on Birmingham's south side, where the stage welcomes local bands and touring acts into a room that's seen forty years of sweat and sound. The bar keeps things simple: cold drinks, good company, and the kind of noise that reminds you why people gather in dark rooms after dark. You'll find Oasis tucked into a stretch of 7th Avenue where music venues have always mattered to this city's soul.
