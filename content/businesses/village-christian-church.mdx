Village Christian Church has been a steady presence on Auburn's University Drive since 1972, serving students, families, and longtime neighbors with straightforward faith and genuine community. Sunday services welcome folks looking for honest preaching and the kind of fellowship that feels like coming home. Located right in the heart of Auburn, the church reflects the values that have long defined this college town and the people who call it home.
