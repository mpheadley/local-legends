On Center Point Parkway, Taquería Sazón Casero serves up authentic Mexican food from a food truck that's become a neighborhood fixture. The menu focuses on what the owners know how to make well: tacos, tamales, and traditional dishes that taste like someone's home kitchen. Folks line up for real flavors made fresh, not shortcuts. Birmingham's neighborhoods keep growing as more family-run spots like this one stake their claim in the city.
