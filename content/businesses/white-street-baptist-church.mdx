White Street Baptist Church has been a gathering place for Auburn's faithful since 1874. Located on Carlisle Drive, the congregation welcomes visitors through its doors with genuine warmth and solid Christian teaching. Whether you're new to town or searching for a church home, you'll find people here who mean what they say about faith and community. Auburn's tree-lined streets hold plenty of history, and this church stands as one of its steadier anchors.
