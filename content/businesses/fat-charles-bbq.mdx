Rolling out from Vulcan Road in Birmingham, Fat Charles BBQ serves up honest barbecue from a food truck that knows what it's doing. The pit work speaks for itself, turning out the kind of smoke-kissed meat that draws folks back week after week. You'll find them parked and ready, phone number handy for those who want to call ahead. Birmingham's neighborhoods have always had room for real food cooked right.
