For over a century, Parker Memorial Baptist Church has anchored faith and community life on Anniston's Quintard Avenue. The congregation gathers in a historic space where generations have worshipped, married, and buried their dead. They host events, Bible studies, and outreach programs that weave into the neighborhood's social fabric. Anniston's tree-lined streets hold dozens of stories like this one, places where regular people work out their lives together.
