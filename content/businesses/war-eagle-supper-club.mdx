Where Auburn folks go to hear live music and catch up with neighbors over a cold drink, War Eagle Supper Club keeps the good times rolling on South College Street. The band plays, the crowd moves, and time seems to slow down the way it should on a Friday night. This is the kind of place that makes Auburn's downtown feel like home, no matter how long you've been away.
