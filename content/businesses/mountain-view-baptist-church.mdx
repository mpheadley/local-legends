Mountain View Baptist Church has served Birmingham's Chalkville community since 1957, offering worship, fellowship, and outreach to families seeking spiritual grounding. Rooted in Baptist tradition, the church welcomes visitors to Sunday services and weekly gatherings that build connections among neighbors. You'll find them at 5568 Chalkville Road, where faith and community run deep. Birmingham's northeast corridor continues to grow while these congregations anchor their neighborhoods with steady purpose.
