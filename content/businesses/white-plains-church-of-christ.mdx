White Plains Church of Christ has served the Anniston community along Highway 9 for generations, offering a quiet place for worship and fellowship. The congregation gathers to study Scripture and support one another through life's seasons, keeping faith at the center of their shared life together. Located just outside town, the church reflects the rural character that still defines much of Calhoun County's landscape.

*White Plains Church Of Christ is based in [Anniston, Alabama](/places/anniston). 9127 AL-9, Anniston, AL 36207, USA*
