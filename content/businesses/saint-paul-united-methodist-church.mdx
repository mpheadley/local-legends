Saint Paul United Methodist Church has anchored Bay Minette's spiritual life for generations, welcoming neighbors along US-31 with open doors and genuine fellowship. The congregation gathers for worship, service, and the kind of community support that shows up when folks need it most. Bay Minette itself remains a working town where churches like Saint Paul still serve as gathering places where people actually know each other's names.
