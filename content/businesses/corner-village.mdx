Corner Village brings a rotating selection of local makers and vendors to a convenient Auburn spot, giving neighbors a reason to stop by 300 North Dean Road. You'll find handcrafted goods and regional products that change with the seasons, supporting the folks who make them. It's the kind of place that reminds you shopping can still feel personal. Auburn keeps finding new ways to celebrate the people who call it home.
