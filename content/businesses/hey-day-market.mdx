Pull up to Hey Day Market on South College Street in Auburn and you'll find a food truck serving real food to hungry folks. They keep things straightforward, offering good meals that stick with you. The operation runs with the kind of no-fuss attitude that makes sense in a college town. Auburn's downtown thrives on spots like this, where students and locals alike know they can count on honest cooking.
