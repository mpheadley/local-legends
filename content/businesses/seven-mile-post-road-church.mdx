Nestled along Seven Mile Post Road outside Athens, this church has served its community for generations with straightforward faith and genuine neighborliness. The congregation gathers to worship, support one another through life's seasons, and stay rooted in their rural Alabama traditions. You'll find unpretentious worship and people who know your name. Athens remains a tight-knit corner of North Alabama where families still know their neighbors and Sunday mornings matter.
