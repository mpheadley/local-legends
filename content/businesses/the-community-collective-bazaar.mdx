Every few months, Birmingham folks gather at The Community Collective Bazaar to shop local makers, eat good food, and spend time together. The marketplace brings together artists, craftspeople, and small vendors who pour their hearts into their work. You'll find everything from handmade goods to fresh baked items while supporting neighbors building something real. It's the kind of gathering that keeps Birmingham's creative spirit alive and thriving.
