Sacred Heart of Jesus Catholic Church has served Anniston's faithful for generations, offering Mass, sacraments, and community from its Golden Springs Road location. The congregation gathers to worship together, finding spiritual grounding in tradition and faith. Whether you're seeking a church home or curious about Catholic practice in East Alabama, they welcome visitors to join them. Anniston itself has long been a place where people of different backgrounds and beliefs build lives side by side.
