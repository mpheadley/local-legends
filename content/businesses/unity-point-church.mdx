Unity Point Church on Amerson Street brings folks together around faith and community service. The congregation focuses on practical help for neighbors, whether that's food assistance, job support, or just showing up when things get hard. They believe church means doing more than talking about faith. Anniston's Amerson Street corridor has long been a gathering place for people seeking connection and purpose.

*Unity Point Church is based in [Anniston, Alabama](/places/anniston). 124 Amerson St, Anniston, AL 36201, USA*
