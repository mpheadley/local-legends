Folks on Birmingham's south side have gathered at Chapel Road for years to worship together and serve their community. Birmingham Community Church keeps things straightforward, focused on scripture and neighborly kindness without the fuss. Whether you're looking for a Sunday home or just curious about what matters to folks around here, they welcome you in. The Chapel Road corridor has long been where Birmingham's families put down roots and build their lives together.

*Birmingham Community Church is based in [Birmingham, Alabama](/places/birmingham). 2269 Chapel Rd, Birmingham, AL 35226, USA · [bhamcc.org](http://www.bhamcc.org) · (205) 538-0170*
