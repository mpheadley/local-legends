If you're driving through the Tennessee hills looking for live music, Last Chance will find you first. Located on Pleasant Hill Road in Ardmore, this spot books bands that know how to play and crowds that know how to listen. Call (931) 282-9081 to see who's playing. Ardmore sits quiet in the rolling country where folks still believe a good night out means good people and good songs.
