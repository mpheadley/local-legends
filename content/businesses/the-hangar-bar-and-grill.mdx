Live music fills the Hangar Bar and Grill most nights, drawing folks from around Bessemer who want cold drinks and good company. Located out on Mitchell Field Road, it's the kind of place where strangers become friends over a set or two. The stage stays busy with local bands playing everything from country to rock. Bessemer's been a working town for generations, and the Hangar keeps that spirit alive.
