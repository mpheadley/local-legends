Live music keeps Iron City Bham packed most nights, drawing folks who want real bands playing real songs in a no-frills room on 22nd Street South. The sound system is honest, the crowd is mixed, and the calendar fills up fast with regional acts and touring bands. Birmingham's Southside has grown up around places like this, turning old brick buildings into gathering spots where the city's music scene actually breathes.
