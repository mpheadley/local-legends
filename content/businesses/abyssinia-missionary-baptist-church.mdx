Abyssinia Missionary Baptist Church has anchored Birmingham's Ensley neighborhood since 1908, serving generations with worship, community outreach, and spiritual guidance. The congregation gathers at their Avenue E sanctuary to practice faith rooted in service to others and commitment to their neighbors. You'll find them open to visitors year-round. In a city shaped by resilience and faith, Ensley remains a neighborhood where churches like this one keep community bonds strong.
