On any given night at LIVE Lounge in downtown Anniston, you'll find folks packed shoulder to shoulder watching local bands tear into their sets. The stage sits right there in the room with you, close enough to see the sweat fly. They keep the drinks flowing and the music loud, which is exactly how live music should be. Anniston's Quintard Avenue has been making a quiet comeback, one venue at a time.

*LIVE Lounge is based in [Anniston, Alabama](/places/anniston). 320 S Quintard Ave, Anniston, AL 36201, USA · [facebook.com/2LIVELounge](http://www.facebook.com/2LIVELounge) · (256) 403-0250*
