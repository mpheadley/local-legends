You'll find hand-rolled bao and bold Asian flavors parked on Magnolia Avenue in Auburn, where the Irritable Bao serves up steamed buns stuffed with everything from crispy pork to vegetarian options. The food truck keeps things casual and craveable, drawing hungry students and locals who want something different from the usual campus fare. Auburn's dining scene keeps growing beyond traditional Southern fare, with spots like this proving the town's appetite for good food done right.
