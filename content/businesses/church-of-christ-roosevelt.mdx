Church of Christ Roosevelt has served the Ensley-Roosevelt neighborhood of Birmingham for generations, offering a steady spiritual home to those seeking worship rooted in Scripture. The congregation gathers at their Cairo Avenue location to study the Bible and build community together. Located on Birmingham's west side, the church reflects the neighborhood's deep religious heritage and the everyday faith that has sustained families through decades of change.
