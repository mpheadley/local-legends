For over sixty years, Edgar's Bakery has been crafting wedding cakes that taste as good as they look. Located in Homewood, this Birmingham institution handles everything from classic white cake to custom designs, working closely with couples to bring their visions to life. Their bakers understand that a wedding cake matters because it's part of your story. In this neighborhood just south of downtown, traditions like these still mean something.

*Edgar's Bakery Homewood is based in [Birmingham, Alabama](/places/birmingham). 2713 18th St S, Birmingham, AL 35209, USA · [edgarsbakery.com](http://www.edgarsbakery.com) · (205) 987-0790*
