When the Church at Southside takes the stage, they're doing more than playing music. This Birmingham worship band connects people to faith through honest, heartfelt performance, Sunday after Sunday at 400 22nd Street South. They believe good music opens doors that sermons alone can't reach. In a neighborhood where community matters, they're helping folks find their way back to something real.
