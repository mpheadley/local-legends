New Hope Baptist Church has been a spiritual anchor on Birmingham's Cleburn Avenue for generations, offering a place where folks gather to worship, serve their community, and support one another through life's seasons. The church welcomes visitors to join their Sunday services and midweek gatherings. You can reach them at (205) 925-9393 or visit their website for service times. In a neighborhood where churches run deep in the social fabric, New Hope stands as a cornerstone of faith and fellowship.
