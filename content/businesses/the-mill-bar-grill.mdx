Every night at The Mill Bar & Grill in Bessemer, live bands take the stage and folks gather around tables to hear real music played by real musicians. It's the kind of place where you can count on cold drinks, good food, and neighbors you might've gone to high school with. Located on 19th Street, it's become part of what keeps Bessemer's downtown alive and worth visiting.
