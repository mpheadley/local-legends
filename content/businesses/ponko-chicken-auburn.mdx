PONKO Chicken operates out of Auburn, serving up Korean-style fried chicken from their food truck on South College Street. Their crispy bird comes seasoned bold and fresh, the kind of thing that keeps folks coming back between classes and on game days. The menu stays simple so they can focus on doing one thing right. Auburn's got that college-town energy where good food trucks become real gathering spots.

*PONKO Chicken Auburn is based in [Auburn, Alabama](/places/auburn). 2111 S College St Suite C, Auburn, AL 36830, USA · [ponkochicken.com](http://www.ponkochicken.com) · (334) 209-6688*
