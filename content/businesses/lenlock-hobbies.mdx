If you're hunting for model trains, slot cars, or die-cast collectibles, Lenlock Hobbies in Anniston has what you need. This pop-up shop on Highway 431 stocks gear for serious hobbyists and curious newcomers alike. The folks running it know their stuff and won't waste your time with fluff. Anniston's been a railroad town since the 1800s, so it's fitting that hobby enthusiasts still gather here to celebrate those iron rails.

*Lenlock Hobbies is based in [Anniston, Alabama](/places/anniston). 1021 US-431 Suite 10, Anniston, AL 36206, USA · [lenlockhobbies.com](https://lenlockhobbies.com) · (256) 820-7304*
