Magic City Art Connection brings together regional craft vendors for a spring market at historic Sloss Furnaces. You'll find handmade jewelry, pottery, paintings, and goods from local artists working across Alabama and the Southeast. The three-day event runs April 26-28, 2024, at the old ironworks that once powered Birmingham's industrial boom. It's a chance to support makers while exploring one of the city's most distinctive landmarks.

*Magic City Art Connection (April 26-28, 2024 @ Sloss Furnaces) is based in [Birmingham, Alabama](/places/birmingham). 20 32nd St N, Birmingham, AL 35222, USA · [magiccityart.com](http://www.magiccityart.com) · (205) 595-6306*
