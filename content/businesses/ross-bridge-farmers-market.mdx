Every Saturday morning, folks gather at Ross Bridge Farmers Market in Birmingham to find what matters: fresh vegetables, local honey, and the kind of neighborly conversation you can't get anywhere else. The vendors here grow it or make it themselves, which means you know exactly where your food comes from. It's the sort of place that keeps a community connected to the land and to each other. Birmingham's neighborhoods thrive when people show up like this.

*Ross Bridge Farmers Market is based in [Birmingham, Alabama](/places/birmingham). 2101 Grand Ave, Birmingham, AL 35226, USA · [rossbridgefarmersmarket.squarespace.com](https://rossbridgefarmersmarket.squarespace.com) · (205) 910-7111*
