If you want to hear real country music played by folks who know how to play it, head out to Otey's Tavern in Birmingham. The band takes the stage regularly, delivering the kind of honest country that gets people dancing and keeps them coming back. You'll find cold drinks, good company, and musicians who care about their craft. Out in the Country Club Park area, Otey's fits right into Birmingham's working music scene.

*Otey's Tavern is based in [Birmingham, Alabama](/places/birmingham). 224 Country Clb Pk, Birmingham, AL 35213, USA · [oteystavern.com](http://www.oteystavern.com) · (205) 871-8435*
