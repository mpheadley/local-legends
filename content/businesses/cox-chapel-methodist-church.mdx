For over a century, Cox Chapel Methodist Church has anchored the Attalla community at 60 Madison Way, offering worship and fellowship to generations of families. The humble brick building reflects the quiet faith that runs deep in this corner of Calhoun County, where Sunday mornings bring neighbors together across the pews. Attalla itself grew from iron foundries and textile mills, and Cox Chapel remains a steady presence through all the changes that have come.

*Cox Chapel Methodist Church is based in [Attalla, Alabama](/places/attalla). 60 Madison Way, Attalla, AL 35954, USA*
