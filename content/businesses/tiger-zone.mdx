Tiger Zone feeds Auburn students from a mobile setup in Village View Dining Facility, serving up quick meals between classes and late-night study sessions. The operation keeps pace with the fast rhythm of campus life, offering straightforward food when hungry Tigers need it most. You'll find them at 230 Beard-Eaves Court, where Auburn's student community gathers to fuel up and catch their breath.
