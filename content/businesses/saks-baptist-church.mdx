Saks Baptist Church has been a steady presence in Anniston for generations, offering worship and community to folks along Highway 431. The congregation gathers to study Scripture, support one another, and live out their faith in practical ways. They welcome newcomers with genuine warmth and a commitment to serving their neighbors. Anniston itself carries deep roots, a place where history and everyday life intertwine in the red clay foothills of East Alabama.
