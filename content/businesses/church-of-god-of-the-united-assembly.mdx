On Mulberry Lane in Attalla sits the Church of God of the United Assembly, where folks gather to worship and build community ties. This congregation carries on deep Pentecostal traditions that run through the heart of Alabama, meeting together in fellowship and faith. Attalla itself grew up around cotton mills and railroads, a working town where church life remains central to how neighbors know one another.
