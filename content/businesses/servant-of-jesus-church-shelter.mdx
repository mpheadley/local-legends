On McCoy Avenue in Anniston, Servant of Jesus Church & Shelter opens its doors to folks who need a meal, a bed, and a kind word. This faith-rooted community works quietly to meet real needs without fanfare. They serve their neighbors the way they understand Jesus would have. Anniston has long been a place where people show up for one another.
