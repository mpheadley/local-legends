Restore, the community outreach ministry of First United Methodist Church in Alabaster, meets folks where they are. Whether through food assistance, job training, or a listening ear, the church tackles real needs with quiet persistence. Located at 128 Market Center Drive, they've built something that matters to neighbors across town. Alabaster itself keeps growing, but Restore reminds us that community still means showing up for one another.

*First United Methodist Church of Alabaster - Restore is based in [Alabaster, Alabama](/places/alabaster). 128 Market Center Dr, Alabaster, AL 35007, USA · [fumcalabaster.org](http://www.fumcalabaster.org) · (205) 663-0509*
