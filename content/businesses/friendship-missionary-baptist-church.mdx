Friendship Missionary Baptist Church has served Anniston's west side since its founding, drawing neighbors together for worship, fellowship, and community care. Located on West 14th Street, the church remains a steady spiritual anchor where families gather each week to strengthen their faith and support one another through life's seasons. Anniston itself carries deep roots in Alabama's industrial past, with neighborhoods that tell stories of resilience and neighborly bonds.
