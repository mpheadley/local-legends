Where Birmingham folks have shopped for generations, Publix Super Market at Montclair Place keeps the neighborhood fed and connected. You'll find what you need, from fresh produce to pantry staples, along with neighbors catching up in the aisles. It's the kind of place where people still know the staff and the staff knows them back. Montclair has always been where South Birmingham comes together.
