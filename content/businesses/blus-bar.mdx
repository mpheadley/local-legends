Live music happens most nights at Blu's Bar on Highway 431 in Anniston, where locals gather to hear everything from country to rock. The bartenders know their regulars by name, and the stage stays busy with bands that actually know how to play. It's the kind of place where strangers become friends over a few drinks and good songs. Anniston's still got plenty of history worth exploring beyond the neon glow.

*Blu's Bar is based in [Anniston, Alabama](/places/anniston). 1013 US-431, Anniston, AL 36206, USA*
