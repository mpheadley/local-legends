On 20th Street South in Birmingham, Dave's Pub brings live music to folks looking for a real night out. The bands come steady, the crowd knows how to have a time, and the drinks pour honest. It's the kind of place where you might catch anything from rock to country, where regulars sit shoulder to shoulder with newcomers. This stretch of Birmingham keeps its grit and character, welcoming anyone willing to walk through the door.
