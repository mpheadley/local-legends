Oxford Civic Center brings live music to this small town through regular performances and community events hosted by the Parks and Recreation Department. The venue gives local and regional bands a place to play while offering Oxford residents entertainment without having to drive far. It's the kind of gathering spot that keeps a town connected and gives folks reasons to get out on weekend nights.

*Oxford Civic Center is based in [Oxford, Alabama](/places/oxford). 401 Mc Cullars Ln, Oxford, AL 36203, USA · (256) 831-2660 · [www.oxfordal.gov/departments/parks-and-recreation](https://www.oxfordal.gov/departments/parks-and-recreation)*
