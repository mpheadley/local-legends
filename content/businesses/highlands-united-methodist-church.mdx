Highlands United Methodist Church has anchored Birmingham's southside since 1902, serving folks with straightforward Methodist values and a welcoming door. They gather for worship, community outreach, and the kind of fellowship that keeps neighborhoods connected. Whether you're seeking spiritual roots or just a place to belong, you'll find neighbors here. This stretch of Birmingham still remembers its working-class past and holds tight to what matters.
