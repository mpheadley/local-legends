Greater Thankful Baptist Church has anchored West Anniston for generations, opening its doors to folks seeking faith and community. The congregation gathers at their W. 14th Street home to worship, learn, and support one another through life's seasons. Their website and phone line keep members connected throughout the week. In Anniston, churches like this one form the backbone of neighborhoods, where people still know their neighbors by name.
