Ashville Church of God has been a spiritual anchor for this rural community along Pine Dale Road since its founding. Rooted in the traditions of the Church of God denomination, the congregation gathers to worship, study scripture, and support one another through life's seasons. You'll find genuine welcomes here, not just on Sundays but throughout the week. Ashville itself remains a quiet corner of St. Clair County where neighbors still know each other's names.
