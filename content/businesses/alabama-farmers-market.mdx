Every Saturday morning, Birmingham's farmers gather at Alabama Farmers Market on Finley Avenue to sell what they've grown. You'll find vegetables, fruits, and goods straight from local farms, with vendors who know their crops and aren't shy about talking them over. It's the kind of place where you shake hands with the person who grew your tomatoes. In a city working to reconnect with its roots, this market keeps that connection alive.

*Alabama Farmers Market is based in [Birmingham, Alabama](/places/birmingham). 344 Finley Ave W, Birmingham, AL 35204, USA · [alabamafarmersmarket.org](http://alabamafarmersmarket.org) · (205) 251-8737*
