Nestled on Alford Avenue, Shades Mountain Church of Christ welcomes folks seeking solid biblical teaching and genuine fellowship. The congregation gathers for worship, study, and service rooted in New Testament practices. Whether you're a longtime member or just passing through Birmingham, you'll find a church that takes its faith seriously without pretension. The Shades Mountain neighborhood reflects the real, working-class spirit that defines so much of Birmingham's character.

*Shades Mountain Church of Christ is based in [Birmingham, Alabama](/places/birmingham). 959 Alford Ave, Birmingham, AL 35226, USA · [shadesmountaincoc.org](http://www.shadesmountaincoc.org) · (205) 440-3206*
