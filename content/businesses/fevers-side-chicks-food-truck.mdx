# Fevers Side Chicks Food Truck

Pull up to Fevers Side Chicks on 3rd Avenue West and you'll find honest food made right there in front of you. They sling sides and comfort fare that stick to your ribs without pretense. The crew knows what folks want and serves it with a no-nonsense approach. Birmingham's West side has always been a place where real food meets real people.

*Fevers Side Chicks Food Truck is based in [Birmingham, Alabama](/places/birmingham). 1001 3rd Ave W, Birmingham, AL 35204, USA · (205) 381-6951*
