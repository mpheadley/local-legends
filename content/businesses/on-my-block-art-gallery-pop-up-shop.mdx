Birmingham's On My Block Art Gallery brings local artists and their work straight to the community through pop-up events. You'll find paintings, sculptures, and crafts from the people who call these neighborhoods home, displayed where folks naturally gather. It's art without the pretense, created by your neighbors for your neighbors. These pop-ups keep Birmingham's creative energy alive in the places that matter most.

*On My Block Art Gallery/ Pop Up Shop is based in [Birmingham, Alabama](/places/birmingham).
