Every Saturday and Wednesday, Pepper Place brings together farmers and neighbors in Birmingham's vibrant Southside. You'll find fresh produce, local goods, and folks who know where their food comes from. It's the kind of place where conversations matter as much as the tomatoes. The market has become the heartbeat of a neighborhood that keeps growing and changing while holding onto what makes it real.

*Pepper Place is based in [Birmingham, Alabama](/places/birmingham). 2829 2nd Ave S, Birmingham, AL 35205, USA · [pepperplace.com](http://pepperplace.com) · (205) 802-2100*
