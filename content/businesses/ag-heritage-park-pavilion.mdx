Couples looking to marry outdoors without fuss can say their vows at Ag Heritage Park Pavilion in Auburn. The venue sits on Auburn University's agricultural grounds, offering a simple shelter and open space for ceremonies and receptions. You'll find genuine Southern charm here, with trees and grounds that feel lived-in and real. Auburn's a college town where agriculture still matters and history runs deep.
