Hoover Randle Home & Gardens sits on Tyler Road where couples say their vows among blooming gardens and historic architecture. The venue offers indoor and outdoor spaces that feel like celebrating at someone's gracious home rather than a rented hall. With the couple's vision in mind, the team works to make each wedding personal and unhurried. Birmingham's tree-lined neighborhoods like this one give the city its graceful character.
