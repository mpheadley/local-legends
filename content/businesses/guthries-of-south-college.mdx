You'll find a mobile food stand on South College Street in Auburn that's been serving up fried chicken the straightforward way since 2012. Guthrie's keeps it simple: quality chicken, hand-breaded and fried fresh, with a handful of sides that let the main event shine. They've built a loyal following among students and locals who know good food when they taste it. Auburn's college atmosphere thrives on spots like this that do one thing right.

*Guthrie’s of South College is based in [Auburn, Alabama](/places/auburn). 1684 S College St, Auburn, AL 36832, USA · [guthrieschicken.com](https://guthrieschicken.com) · (334) 209-1140*
