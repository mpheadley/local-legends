Amsterdam Cafe rolls up to Auburn with proper Dutch stroopwafels, poffertjes, and savory crepes that taste like they came straight from the streets of Amsterdam. You'll find this food truck parked on South Gay Street, serving folks who want something different from the usual college-town fare. The owners know their craft and it shows in every bite. Auburn's always been a place where students and locals mix, and this spot fits right in.

*Amsterdam Cafe is based in [Auburn, Alabama](/places/auburn). 410 S Gay St, Auburn, AL 36830, USA · [amsterdamcafe.com](https://amsterdamcafe.com) · (334) 826-8181*
