BridgeStreet Gallery and Loft gives couples a raw, honest space to get married in downtown Birmingham. The converted warehouse on Richard Arrington Jr Boulevard holds your celebration in exposed brick and natural light, no fussy frills required. They'll work with you to make it yours, whether that's intimate or expansive. It's the kind of place where Birmingham's creative soul shows up to witness two people commit to each other.

*BridgeStreet Gallery and Loft is based in [Birmingham, Alabama](/places/birmingham). 213 Richard Arrington Jr Blvd S, Birmingham, AL 35233, USA · [bridgestreetvenue.com](http://www.bridgestreetvenue.com) · (205) 521-7716*
