Presbytery Shpprds and Lapsley Resource Service help churches across Alabama tend to their communities and grow their faith. Based in Birmingham, they offer guidance, training, and support to congregations navigating everything from pastoral leadership to mission work. Their staff works quietly behind the scenes, connecting church leaders with resources they need to serve their people well. Birmingham's Lorna Ridge neighborhood has long been home to institutions that strengthen the spiritual life of the city.
