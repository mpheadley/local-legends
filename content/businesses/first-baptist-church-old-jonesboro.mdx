First Baptist Church Old Jonesboro has been anchoring faith and community in Bessemer since 1887. Located on Deola Road, this congregation gathers weekly to worship, study Scripture, and support one another through life's seasons. Their doors stay open to anyone seeking spiritual fellowship and genuine Christian community. In Bessemer, a city with deep industrial roots and strong neighborhood ties, churches like this one remain gathering places where people still know each other's names.
