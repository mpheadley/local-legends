In Birmingham, when folks are planning something special, they head to Pastry Art Bake Shoppe on 29th Avenue South. Owner and baker craft custom wedding cakes that taste as good as they look, handling everything from design to delivery. Each cake tells a story about the couple getting married. The shop sits in a neighborhood where small businesses still thrive and neighbors know each other's names.

*Pastry Art Bake Shoppe is based in [Birmingham, Alabama](/places/birmingham). 1917 29th Ave S, Birmingham, AL 35209, USA · [pastryartcakes.com](http://www.pastryartcakes.com) · (205) 877-3852*
