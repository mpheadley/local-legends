Holy Trinity Episcopal Church has anchored Auburn's Church Drive since 1856, offering steady worship and community service across generations. The congregation gathers for traditional liturgy and welcomes visitors seeking spiritual grounding in a historic setting. Their doors remain open to anyone looking for faith and fellowship. Auburn's tree-lined streets and college town character create a welcoming home for seekers and longtime believers alike.
