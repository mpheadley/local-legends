United Fellowship Baptist Church has been a gathering place for Alexandria's faithful since its founding, offering worship and community to generations of families in Calhoun County. Located on Becky Turner Road, the church welcomes visitors to Sunday services and serves as a spiritual anchor for the area. The congregation remains committed to its mission of fellowship and faith. Alexandria itself is a quiet rural community where neighbors know each other and church life remains central to daily living.
