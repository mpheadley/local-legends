When you're hungry for real Mexican food in Birmingham, Mas Sabor's food truck on Valley Avenue delivers. The menu hits right with authentic tacos, tamales, and fresh salsas that taste like they belong south of the border. Owner-operated and family-focused, this spot serves folks who know good food when they taste it. Valley Avenue's got that working neighborhood feel where people still support their neighbors.

*Mas Sabor is based in [Birmingham, Alabama](/places/birmingham). 401 Valley Ave, Birmingham, AL 35209, USA · [massaboral.com](https://massaboral.com) · (205) 261-8102*
