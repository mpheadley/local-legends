Nestled on Gilbert Ferry Road in Attalla, Life Assurance Apostolic Worship Center welcomes folks seeking spiritual community grounded in faith and fellowship. The church draws people together for worship, teaching, and the kind of genuine connection that matters in small-town life. You'll find them ready to talk about what they believe and how it shapes their days. Attalla itself sits in the foothills where hardworking people have built lives rooted in family and faith.
