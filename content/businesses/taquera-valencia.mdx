From a food truck on Springville Road, Taquería Valencia serves up genuine Mexican fare that draws folks from across Birmingham. The menu features hand-rolled burritos, fresh carnitas, and aguas frescas made the right way, not shortcuts. Owner-operated and family-focused, they've built steady crowds who know where to find real food cooked with care. This stretch of Birmingham keeps growing with the kind of neighborhood spots that stick around because people actually need them.
