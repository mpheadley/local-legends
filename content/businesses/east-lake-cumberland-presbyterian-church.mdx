East Lake Cumberland Presbyterian Church has served Birmingham's East Lake neighborhood for generations, offering worship and community support to folks who call this part of town home. The church stands as a gathering place where neighbors know each other and faith runs deep through the seasons. In a neighborhood that's seen plenty of change, this congregation keeps showing up for one another, the way churches have always done in Alabama towns.
