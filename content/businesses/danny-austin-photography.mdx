Danny Austin captures weddings the way they actually happen in Birmingham: honest, unrushed, and full of real feeling. Working from his Second Avenue studio, he photographs couples getting ready, walking down the aisle, and dancing like nobody's watching. His style favors natural light and genuine moments over posed perfection. Birmingham's vibrant downtown keeps drawing young couples who want their wedding story told true.
