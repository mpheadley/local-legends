Glen Iris Baptist Church has been a steady presence in Birmingham since 1910, rooted in the heart of a neighborhood that's seen plenty of change. The congregation gathers to worship, serve their community, and tend to the spiritual needs of their neighbors with quiet faithfulness. That kind of staying power matters in a city where churches have often been the backbone holding neighborhoods together through hard times and good ones alike.
