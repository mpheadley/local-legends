Mountaintop Church on Birmingham's Centerview Drive opens its doors to folks seeking honest faith and real community. Pastor and congregation alike believe in straightforward gospel teaching and neighbors helping neighbors through life's rough patches. They gather weekly to worship, pray, and work through what it means to follow Jesus in modern times. Birmingham's south side has long been a place where churches serve as anchors for families building better lives.
