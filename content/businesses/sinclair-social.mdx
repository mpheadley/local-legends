Sinclair Social pulls up to Anniston with food that tastes like somebody actually cares. Operating from a food truck on West 10th Street, they turn out honest cooking that draws folks back again and again. The menu changes with what's good, and nothing feels rushed or phoned in. Anniston's downtown has been finding its footing, and places like this are exactly what a neighborhood needs to feel alive again.

*Sinclair Social is based in [Anniston, Alabama](/places/anniston). 33 W 10th St, Anniston, AL 36201, USA · [thesinclairsocial.com](http://thesinclairsocial.com)*
