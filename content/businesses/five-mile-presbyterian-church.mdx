Five Mile Presbyterian Church has anchored its Birmingham neighborhood since 1891, offering worship and fellowship to generations of families. The congregation gathers to study scripture, support one another through life's seasons, and serve their community with quiet faithfulness. Their doors remain open to anyone seeking a place to pray and belong. In a city rich with spiritual tradition, Five Mile stands as a steady witness to faith that endures.
