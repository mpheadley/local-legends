On Noble Street in Anniston, Dark Horse Saloon keeps the live music scene breathing with local bands playing regularly. It's a straightforward spot where folks gather for cold drinks and honest entertainment, the kind of place that makes a night out feel right. The bar's been steady about bringing musicians through its doors, giving the community somewhere to hear real music played live. Anniston's got grit and history, and Dark Horse fits right into that character.
