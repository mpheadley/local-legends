Mezon Food Truck serves up honest meals from a mobile kitchen on Birmingham's south side. Owner and chef handle everything from prep to service, keeping the operation personal and the food fresh. You'll find them parked at 2902 4th Avenue South, where they've built a loyal following through word of mouth and consistent quality. This stretch of Birmingham reflects the city's working heart, where real restaurants feed real neighborhoods.
