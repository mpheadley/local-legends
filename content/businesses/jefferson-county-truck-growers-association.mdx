For decades, the Jefferson County Truck Growers Association has brought fresh produce straight from local farms to Birmingham shoppers. Located on Finley Avenue, this farmers market vendor connects neighbors with seasonal vegetables and fruits grown right here in Alabama. You'll find what's ripe now, picked at peak flavor, from farmers who know their soil. In a city hungry for real food and real community, this market keeps both thriving.

*Jefferson County Truck Growers Association is based in [Birmingham, Alabama](/places/birmingham). 344 Finley Ave W, Birmingham, AL 35204, USA · [alabamafarmersmarket.org](http://www.alabamafarmersmarket.org) · (205) 251-8737*
