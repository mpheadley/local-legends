For over four decades, Hoover Florist has been handling the flowers at Birmingham weddings, turning brides' visions into arrangements that actually last through the reception. They work with what grows well in Alabama's climate and listen to what you want rather than pushing trends. Located on Hoover Court, they're the kind of place where the florist remembers your name. Birmingham's south side has always been where people build their lives and celebrate the moments that matter.
