Live bands bring Birmingham's 55th Place North alive most nights at Jazz On 55th. You'll find local musicians playing the real thing, no pretense, just good jazz and the kind of crowd that knows what they're listening to. It's the sort of place where the music matters more than anything else. The neighborhood around this spot has been quietly building something worth paying attention to.
