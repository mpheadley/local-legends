Every Saturday morning, folks in Athens gather at the Limestone County Farmer's Market to pick up vegetables, meats, and goods straight from local growers. You'll find the market on West Green Street, where neighbors catch up while loading their trucks with whatever's in season. It's the kind of place where you know who grew your tomatoes. Athens has always been a town that values knowing where your food comes from.

*Limestone County Farmer’s Market is based in [Athens, Alabama](/places/athens). 409 W Green St, Athens, AL 35611, USA · [fma.alabama.gov/mapmarkets.aspx?County=LIMESTONE&OrgType=Market](http://fma.alabama.gov/mapmarkets.aspx?County=LIMESTONE&OrgType=Market) · (256) 233-6400*
