For twenty years, Main Street Florist has been handling Birmingham weddings with genuine care. Owner and designer work closely with couples to create arrangements that feel personal, not generic. They source quality blooms and pay attention to the details that matter on your big day. Located on Manning Place, they're part of a neighborhood where old-school service still means something.
