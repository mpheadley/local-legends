Avondale United Methodist Church has been part of Birmingham's fabric since 1902, serving its neighborhood with steady faith and open doors. Located on 40th Street South, the church welcomes folks of all backgrounds to worship, find community, and put their beliefs into action. They're rooted in the real work of helping neighbors and building connections that matter. In a city constantly changing, Avondale remains a gathering place where people find belonging.
