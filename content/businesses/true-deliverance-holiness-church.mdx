True Deliverance Holiness Church in Auburn fills their sanctuary with the sounds of Southern gospel, voices rising in harmony the way faith has been sung in these parts for generations. The group carries forward a musical tradition rooted deep in the region's spiritual soil, offering music that speaks to both heart and soul. You'll find them at 936 North Donahue Drive, sharing songs that matter to folks who understand the power of a good hymn. Auburn's streets have always welcomed those who gather to celebrate together.
