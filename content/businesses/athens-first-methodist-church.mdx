For over a century, Athens First Methodist has anchored downtown with honest faith and steady hands. The congregation gathers on Hobbs Street to worship, serve neighbors, and tend to the spiritual needs of this North Alabama community. They've weathered change while staying rooted in Methodist tradition. Athens itself remains a small town where churches like this one still shape the rhythms of daily life.
