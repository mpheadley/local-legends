Lindsay Lane Baptist Church has served Athens for generations, offering a place where neighbors gather for worship and community. Located on Lindsay Lane, the church welcomes folks seeking fellowship and spiritual growth through traditional services and fellowship programs. Their doors open to anyone looking for a faith community rooted in Southern Baptist tradition. Athens itself thrives as a welcoming city where churches like this one form the backbone of local life.
