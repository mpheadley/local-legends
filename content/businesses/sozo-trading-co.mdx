At Sozo Trading Co. in Birmingham, you'll find a market stall packed with goods sourced thoughtfully from around the world. The folks running it believe in connecting people to products with real stories behind them, from textiles to home goods. It's the kind of place where shopping means supporting craftspeople and small producers. The Fountain Heights neighborhood has long been a gathering spot for folks looking to do right by their dollars.
