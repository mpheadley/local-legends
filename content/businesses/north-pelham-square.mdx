Live music happens most nights at North Pelham Square on Helena Road, where folks gather to hear local bands play everything from country to rock. The venue's straightforward approach means good sound, cold drinks, and room to move around. You'll find regulars and newcomers rubbing shoulders in this corner of Birmingham. The spot fits right into the city's working neighborhoods, where people still know how to have a good time.
