Jacksonville Christian Outreach Center serves neighbors across Jacksonville with food assistance, community programs, and support for folks facing hard times. Run by volunteers and donations, the center meets practical needs while building connections among people who might otherwise feel isolated. It's the kind of place that matters most in small towns, where showing up for each other isn't optional. More at myjcoc.org.

*Jacksonville Christian Outreach Center is based in [Jacksonville, Alabama](/places/jacksonville). 206 Francis St W, Jacksonville, AL 36265, USA · (256) 435-1891 · [www.myjcoc.org](http://www.myjcoc.org)*
