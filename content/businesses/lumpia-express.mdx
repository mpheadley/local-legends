Lumpia Express is a food truck parked in Jacksonville that serves Filipino spring rolls and other Filipino dishes to folks hungry for something different. Owner prepares everything fresh, and the prices won't break you. It's become a steady spot for people craving authentic flavors that you won't find at the usual chain restaurants around town. Good food, good value, good reason to stop by.
