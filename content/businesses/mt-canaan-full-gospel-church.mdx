Mt Canaan Full Gospel Church has served Birmingham's neighborhoods since its founding, rooted in faith and community care. Located on 24th Street Southwest, the church welcomes folks through its doors for worship and fellowship. Their work reflects the spiritual traditions that have long anchored Black communities across Alabama. Birmingham's Southwest side carries the stories of generations who built their lives on faith and neighborly devotion.
