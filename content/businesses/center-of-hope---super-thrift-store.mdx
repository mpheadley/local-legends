Center of Hope - Super Thrift Store in Oxford offers affordable clothing, furniture, and household goods while supporting a local ministry. The pop-up shop helps folks stretch their dollars and keeps usable items out of landfills. Money raised goes back into community programs that address real needs. It's a straightforward way to shop with purpose and help neighbors at the same time.

*Center of Hope - Super Thrift Store is based in [Oxford, Alabama](/places/oxford). 1700 Hamric Dr E, Oxford, AL 36203, USA · (256) 831-7199 · [www.centerofhopeministry.org](http://www.centerofhopeministry.org)*
