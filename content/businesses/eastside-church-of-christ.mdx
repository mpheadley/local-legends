For over fifty years, Eastside Church of Christ has opened its doors to Athens believers seeking solid biblical teaching and genuine community. Located on French Way, the congregation welcomes visitors to worship services and midweek gatherings where neighbors become family. They focus on living out faith through service and study. Athens has always been a town where church life runs deep, anchoring the rhythms of daily life.
