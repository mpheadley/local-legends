For over a century, First Baptist Church of Alabaster has anchored the spiritual life of this Calhoun County community. Located on 3rd Avenue Northwest, the congregation gathers to worship, study Scripture, and serve their neighbors through faith-driven mission work. Their doors open to anyone seeking fellowship and a place to belong. Alabaster itself has grown from a small town into a thriving suburb that still values the deep roots churches like this one provide.
