If It Makes You is a pop-up shop in Auburn where local artisans craft jewelry by hand. Each piece tells a story, made with intention and care rather than mass production. Located at 1000 Main Street, they welcome folks to watch the creative process unfold. Auburn's Main Street keeps evolving as makers and creators stake their claim in this college town.
