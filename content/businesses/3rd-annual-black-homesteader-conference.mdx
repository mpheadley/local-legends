Every year, Black farmers and homesteaders gather in Anniston to share knowledge about growing food, raising animals, and building self-sufficient lives. The conference brings together experienced growers with folks just starting out, trading seeds, stories, and practical advice for Southern land. You'll find workshops on composting, poultry, and preserving, plus vendors selling heirloom varieties suited to Alabama's climate. Anniston sits in the foothills of eastern Alabama, where red clay and deep roots run through the community.
