Crossroads Assembly of God Church sits on Hurricane Road in Bay Minette, serving folks who gather to worship and build community together. The church welcomes neighbors looking for fellowship, prayer, and spiritual growth rooted in faith. Whether you're visiting or settling in, Crossroads offers a place to belong. Bay Minette itself is a quiet corner of Baldwin County where neighbors still know each other's names.
