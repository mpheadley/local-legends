On 3rd Avenue in Alabaster, First Baptist Church has been serving its community with straightforward faith and neighborly care for generations. They welcome folks through the doors at 903 3rd Ave NW for worship, fellowship, and the kind of spiritual grounding that matters when life gets complicated. You'll find them online at fbcalabaster.org or reach them at (205) 663-3531. Alabaster itself grew from mining limestone, a town built on solid ground that still values hard work and faith.
