Stop by Academy Sports + Outdoors on Gadsden Highway in Trussville for hunting gear, fishing tackle, and everything else you need to get outside. Their pop-up shop stocks what Birmingham folks have relied on for generations: quality equipment without the fuss. Whether you're gearing up for the season or just need new boots, the team knows their stuff and treats you right. Trussville's become a real hub for folks looking to do more than just talk about the outdoors.
