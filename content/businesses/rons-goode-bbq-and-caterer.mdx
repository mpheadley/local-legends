Out on Highway 144 in Alexandria, Ron's Goode BBQ serves up real smoked meat from a food truck that's become a gathering spot for folks who know their barbecue. The ribs and brisket come slow-cooked and honest, the kind of food that tastes like someone actually cared about getting it right. They'll cater your event too if you call ahead. Alexandria's the sort of place where a good food truck becomes part of the community fabric.

*Rons Goode BBQ and Caterer is based in [Alexandria, Alabama](/places/alexandria). 8314 AL-144, Alexandria, AL 36250, USA · [rons144.com](https://rons144.com) · (256) 952-5644*
