Bold Springs Presbyterian Church has served Birmingham's Cahaba Valley community since its founding, offering a steady presence for worship and fellowship along Cahaba Valley Road. The congregation gathers for Sunday services and welcomes visitors looking for a faith home. With roots running deep in this part of town, the church remains a familiar landmark for families navigating Birmingham's tree-lined neighborhoods and valley roads.
