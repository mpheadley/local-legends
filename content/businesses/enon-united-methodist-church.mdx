Enon United Methodist Church has anchored Birmingham's Fountain Heights neighborhood since 1902, offering worship and community to generations of Alabamians. Located on Fourth Avenue West, this congregation welcomes folks looking for spiritual fellowship and connection. The church remains a gathering place where neighbors become family and faith runs deep. In a city constantly reshaping itself, Enon stands as a quiet witness to Birmingham's resilience and soul.

*Enon United Methodist Church is based in [Birmingham, Alabama](/places/birmingham). 431 4th Ave W, Birmingham, AL 35204, USA · [umc.org](https://www.umc.org) · (205) 323-4893*
