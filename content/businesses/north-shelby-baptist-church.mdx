North Shelby Baptist Church has been serving the Belcher Drive community in Birmingham for generations. The congregation gathers for worship, fellowship, and service rooted in faith and Southern tradition. They welcome visitors through their doors every Sunday and offer programs that tie the community together. In North Shelby, you'll find a neighborhood where churches like this one still form the heartbeat of everyday life.
