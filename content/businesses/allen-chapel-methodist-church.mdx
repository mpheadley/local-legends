Allen Chapel Methodist Church has served Anniston's faithful since 1887, holding steady through generations of change on Caver Gilbert Drive. The congregation gathers to worship, support one another, and stay rooted in their community. Songs rise from the pews, and doors open to all seeking connection. In Anniston, churches like this one remain the heartbeat of neighborhood life.
