Alabaster Church of Christ opens its doors to folks seeking fellowship and spiritual grounding in this corner of central Alabama. The congregation gathers at their Fifth Avenue location to worship, study Scripture, and build community ties that run deep. You'll find straightforward preaching and genuine welcomes here, whether you're a longtime member or passing through town. Alabaster itself has grown from a quiet crossroads into a vibrant suburban community where neighbors still know each other's names.
