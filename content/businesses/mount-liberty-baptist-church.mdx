Mount Liberty Baptist Church has served Anniston's faithful for generations, offering a place where neighbors gather to worship and support one another through life's seasons. The congregation draws strength from Scripture and community, staying rooted in the values that have held families together in this corner of Alabama. Anniston itself sits at the heart of Calhoun County, a town shaped by its mills, mountains, and the people who've called it home.
