The Alcove Birmingham gives couples a gallery-like setting for their wedding day. Located in a restored building on Fourth Avenue North, the venue combines exposed brick and natural light with flexible spaces that adapt to how you want to celebrate. The team helps you design a ceremony and reception that feel genuinely yours. This stretch of Birmingham has become a gathering place where local businesses and neighbors are building something worth sticking around for.

*The Alcove Birmingham is based in [Birmingham, Alabama](/places/birmingham). 1713 4th Ave N, Birmingham, AL 35203, USA · [alcovebhm.com](http://alcovebhm.com) · (205) 471-7730*
