Rising Star Baptist Church has served Birmingham's westside community since its founding, offering worship and fellowship to generations of families. Located on Second Avenue, the church remains a spiritual anchor in the neighborhood, hosting Sunday services and community gatherings throughout the year. Members gather for Bible study, prayer, and mutual support. Birmingham's westside has long been home to strong churches that hold neighborhoods together.
