Every Saturday at Manning Place, neighbors gather to buy straight from the folks who grew it. Mt Laurel Farmers' Market keeps it simple: local produce, local people, local money staying put. You'll find vegetables in season, eggs from nearby farms, and conversations that matter. Birmingham's Mt Laurel neighborhood has always been the kind of place where people know their grower's name.

*Mt Laurel Farmers’ Market is based in [Birmingham, Alabama](/places/birmingham). 50 Manning Pl, Birmingham, AL 35242, USA · [facebook.com/groups/462890235576](https://www.facebook.com/groups/462890235576)*
