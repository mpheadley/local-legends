A-Train Express rolls up to feed Birmingham hungry. This food truck on Pearson Avenue serves straightforward meals that stick to your ribs, the kind of cooking that doesn't need fancy words. You can order ahead or grab what's hot when you swing by. The truck's become part of the neighborhood fabric, where folks know they'll get honest food and fair prices.
