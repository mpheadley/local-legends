Smith Metropolitan AME Church has been a spiritual anchor in Anniston since 1887, serving generations with worship, fellowship, and community service. Located on Pine Avenue, this historic congregation continues its Methodist tradition while remaining actively involved in local life. Visitors find a welcoming space rooted in faith and Southern hospitality. Anniston itself sits at the intersection of history and progress, where churches like Smith Metropolitan help shape the soul of this East Alabama town.
