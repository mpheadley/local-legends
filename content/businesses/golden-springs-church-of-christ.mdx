Nestled on Coleman Road in Anniston, Golden Springs Church of Christ has served its community with quiet faithfulness for generations. The congregation welcomes visitors through its doors to worship, study Scripture, and grow together in Christian fellowship. Whether you're new to the area or searching for a church home, they invite you to experience their warm hospitality. Anniston itself carries deep roots as an industrial city transformed by art, culture, and the enduring faith of its people.
