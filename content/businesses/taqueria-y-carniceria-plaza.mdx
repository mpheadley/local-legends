Taqueria Y Carniceria Plaza pulls up on South College Street in Auburn with fresh carnitas, chorizo, and hand-rolled tortillas that taste like somebody's grandmother knew what she was doing. The food truck keeps it simple: good meat, good technique, and prices that won't leave your wallet crying. They've been feeding Auburn folks for years, reliable as sunrise. South College has always been where Auburn's real food happens, away from the campus chains.

*Taqueria Y Carniceria Plaza is based in [Auburn, Alabama](/places/auburn). 1629 S College St, Auburn, AL 36832, USA · (334) 466-4975*
