Stowers Hill Baptist Church has served Attalla's community since its founding, offering worship and fellowship to generations of believers. Located on 9th Avenue, the church remains a steady presence in town, welcoming visitors through its doors at 407 9th Ave SW. Folks gather here for Sunday services and the kind of spiritual community that roots people to their hometown. Attalla itself sits in the foothills of northeastern Alabama, a place where church steeples still mark the landscape.
