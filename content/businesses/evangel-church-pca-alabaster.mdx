Evangel Church PCA opens its doors on Thompson Road in Alabaster, where folks gather for worship rooted in Reformed theology and biblical teaching. The congregation builds community through honest faith and straightforward preaching, welcoming neighbors who want to dig deeper into Scripture together. Whether you're exploring church for the first time or looking for solid doctrine, this congregation offers a place to belong.
