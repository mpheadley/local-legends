In Athens, a Bulgarian Pentecostal church serves its tight-knit congregation with faith rooted in old-country traditions. The church keeps alive cultural practices and spiritual teachings brought from Eastern Europe, offering a gathering place where Bulgarian families worship together. Services blend English and Bulgarian, honoring both heritage and home. Athens itself has long been a crossroads where different people build lives alongside each other.
