First Presbyterian Church has stood on Gadsden Highway since 1889, serving Birmingham's spiritual needs through worship, fellowship, and community outreach. The congregation gathers for Sunday services and welcomes visitors seeking a place of faith and connection. Their doors remain open to anyone looking for a welcoming church home. In this stretch of Birmingham, churches like this one form the backbone of neighborhood life and tradition.
