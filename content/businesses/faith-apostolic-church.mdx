Faith Apostolic Church has been serving Birmingham's southwest side for decades, offering worship rooted in Pentecostal tradition and community care. Located on Heron Johnson Drive, this congregation welcomes folks seeking genuine faith and fellowship without pretense. Pastor and members work together to help their neighbors through spiritual guidance and practical support. Birmingham's working neighborhoods hold stories of resilience and faith that run deep.
