Auburn United Methodist Church serves its neighbors through the Good News Center, where a food pantry helps families put meals on their tables. Located on Magnolia Avenue, the center opens its doors to anyone facing hunger, offering groceries and dignity without judgment. It's straightforward help rooted in faith and community. Auburn's diverse neighborhoods depend on places like this to keep folks fed and connected.
