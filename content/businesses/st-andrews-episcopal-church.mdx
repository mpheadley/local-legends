St Andrew's Episcopal Church has anchored Birmingham's Southside since 1906, offering a place where neighbors gather for worship and community service. The brick church building reflects the neighborhood's history while the congregation stays focused on practical faith: feeding the hungry, welcoming strangers, and standing with those pushed to the margins. Downtown Birmingham's tree-lined streets hold stories of ordinary people doing faithful work.
