For over a century, Roebuck Church of Christ has served its Birmingham congregation with straightforward faith and community care. Located on First Avenue North, this modest church welcomes folks seeking solid biblical teaching and genuine fellowship. Members gather regularly to study scripture, worship together, and support one another through life's seasons. The Roebuck neighborhood itself reflects Birmingham's working-class roots and enduring resilience.

*Roebuck Church of Christ is based in [Birmingham, Alabama](/places/birmingham). 5535 1st Ave N, Birmingham, AL 35212, USA*
