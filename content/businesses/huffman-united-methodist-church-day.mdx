On Gene Reed Road in Birmingham, Huffman United Methodist Church opens its doors to families seeking community and faith. The church welcomes children through its day program, offering a space where young ones learn Christian values alongside neighbors. Adults find fellowship and spiritual growth through regular services and gatherings. The congregation has rooted itself in this corner of Birmingham for generations, becoming part of the neighborhood's steady heartbeat.
