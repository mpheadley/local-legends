Altadena Valley Presbyterian Church has served Birmingham families for generations, offering worship and community rooted in faith and neighborly care. Located on Caldwell Mill Road, the congregation welcomes folks seeking spiritual guidance and genuine fellowship. Whether you're born and raised here or new to the area, you'll find a place among people who show up for each other. Birmingham's Caldwell Mill neighborhood thrives on these kinds of connections.
