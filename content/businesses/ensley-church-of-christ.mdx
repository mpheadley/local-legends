On Dugan Avenue in Birmingham's Ensley neighborhood, the Church of Christ has been a steady presence for generations. Members gather to worship, study scripture, and serve their community with quiet faithfulness. The congregation welcomes visitors through their doors and online at ensleychurchofchrist.org. Ensley itself carries the industrial heritage of Birmingham, a neighborhood where working people built lives and faith communities that still stand today.

*Ensley Church of Christ is based in [Birmingham, Alabama](/places/birmingham). 1001 Dugan Ave, Birmingham, AL 35214, USA · [ensleychurchofchrist.org](http://www.ensleychurchofchrist.org)*
