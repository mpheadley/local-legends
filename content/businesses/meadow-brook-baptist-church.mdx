Meadow Brook Baptist Church has been a gathering place for Birmingham families for generations, offering worship, fellowship, and community service along Meadow Brook Road. The congregation welcomes visitors of all backgrounds to join their Sunday services and midweek programs. Whether you're new to the area or looking for a church home, you'll find genuine Southern hospitality here. Birmingham's south side neighborhoods have always thrived on strong community institutions like this one.
