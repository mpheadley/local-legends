The spot doesn't pretend to be anything fancy, just a place where musicians and listeners meet on honest ground. You'll find everything from blues to rock to whatever else comes through the door. Birmingham's Avondale neighborhood has quietly become the city's creative heart, where working artists and neighborhood folks shape something genuine.
