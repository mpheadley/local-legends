On US-278 near Attalla, Mountain Top Flea Market brings together local farmers and vendors who know their craft. You'll find fresh produce, handmade goods, and the kind of treasures that make weekend hunting worthwhile. The folks running it understand that a good market is about community as much as commerce. Attalla itself sits in the foothills where old mining towns are finding new life through local enterprise and neighborly spirit.
