You'll find genuine Mexican food at Taqueria Ortega, a food truck parked on Industrial Road in Alabaster. The menu features tacos, tortas, and traditional plates made fresh daily. Owner-operated, it's the kind of spot where regulars know their order by heart. Alabaster's industrial corridor has quietly become a destination for folks seeking honest meals and real flavors.

*Taqueria Ortega is based in [Alabaster, Alabama](/places/alabaster). 151 Industrial Rd, Alabaster, AL 35007, USA*
