On 41st Street South in Birmingham, Avondale Brewing Company brings live music and cold beer to a restored industrial building with real character. The brewery pulls in local bands most nights, filling the space with the kind of sounds that make you want to stay longer than you planned. It's the sort of place where strangers become friends over a couple rounds. This neighborhood has been quietly rebuilding itself, one gathering spot at a time.

*Avondale Brewing Company is based in [Birmingham, Alabama](/places/birmingham). 201 41st St S, Birmingham, AL 35222, USA · [avondalebrewing.com](http://www.avondalebrewing.com)*
