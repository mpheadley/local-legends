Lauren Bailey captures weddings with a photographer's eye for real moments over posed perfection. Working out of Homewood, she builds relationships with couples and shows up ready to catch the laughter, the tears, the details that matter. Her work feels honest, grounded in the particular light and feeling of each day. Birmingham's tree-lined neighborhoods and historic venues provide the backdrop for love stories told the way they actually happen.

*Lauren Bailey Photography is based in [Birmingham, Alabama](/places/birmingham). Homewood, Birmingham, AL 35209, USA · [laurenbaileyphotography.com](https://www.laurenbaileyphotography.com)*
