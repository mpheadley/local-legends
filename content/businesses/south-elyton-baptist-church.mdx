For over a century, South Elyton Baptist Church has anchored its Birmingham neighborhood with steady faith and community service. Located on First Street South, this congregation welcomes folks looking for genuine fellowship and spiritual growth. Their doors stay open to anyone seeking connection in a place where faith still matters. In a city constantly reinventing itself, South Elyton represents the roots Birmingham keeps coming back to.
