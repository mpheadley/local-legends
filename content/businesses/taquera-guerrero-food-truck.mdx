On Birmingham's North Avenue, Taquería Guerrero serves up authentic Mexican food from a modest food truck that's earned a loyal following. The menu features handmade tacos, tamales, and traditional plates made fresh daily, drawing folks from across the city who know where to find real cooking. Owner-operated and family-focused, this spot represents the kind of neighborhood gathering place that keeps communities connected. North Birmingham's food scene keeps getting better because businesses like this one choose to put down roots here.
