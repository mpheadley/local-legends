On Crestwood Boulevard, the Crestwood Tavern brings live music to Birmingham nights, drawing folks who want a cold drink and genuine entertainment. The venue hosts local and touring bands, keeping the place buzzing with everything from rock to country. It's the kind of spot where you can count on hearing something worth staying for. In a neighborhood that's always got something happening, this tavern holds its own just fine.

*Crestwood Tavern is based in [Birmingham, Alabama](/places/birmingham). 5500 Crestwood Blvd, Birmingham, AL 35212, USA · [thecrestwoodtavern.com](http://thecrestwoodtavern.com) · (205) 510-0053*
