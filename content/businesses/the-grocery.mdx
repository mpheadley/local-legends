Live music and cold drinks come together at The Grocery on Central Avenue, where locals gather for the real thing. The band takes the stage while folks settle in at the bar, no pretense required. You'll find the kind of crowd that shows up for good music and good company, night after night. Birmingham's Fountain Heights neighborhood keeps its character one gathering place at a time.
