When a bride needs her dress adjusted just right, Sunny Alteration on Opelika Road is where Auburn women turn. The shop handles everything from hemming to restructuring, treating each gown like it matters, because it does. Owner and seamstress work closely with brides to make sure their wedding day outfit feels perfect. Auburn's bridal community knows good work when they see it, and they keep coming back.
