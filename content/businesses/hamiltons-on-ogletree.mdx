Hamilton's On Ogletree brings honest food to Auburn's west side from a food truck that knows what it's doing. The kitchen turns out straightforward meals that satisfy without fuss, drawing folks who want real cooking without the sit-down formality. Orders move quick, and the crew keeps things friendly even during the lunch rush. Auburn's Ogletree corridor keeps growing as a neighborhood where people actually want to gather and eat.
