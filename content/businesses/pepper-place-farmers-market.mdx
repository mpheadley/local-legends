Every Saturday morning, Birmingham folks head to Pepper Place Farmers Market on 2nd Avenue South to find what local growers pulled from the ground that week. You'll find vegetables, fruits, and goods from farmers who know their land. The market's been a gathering spot where neighbors catch up and families fill their bags with fresh food. It's the kind of place that keeps Birmingham's neighborhoods connected to the seasons and to each other.

*Pepper Place Farmers Market is based in [Birmingham, Alabama](/places/birmingham). 2829 2nd Ave S, Birmingham, AL 35233, USA · [pepperplacemarket.com](http://www.pepperplacemarket.com) · (205) 490-3176*
