For over a century, this United Methodist congregation on Christine Avenue has anchored spiritual life in Anniston. Members gather to worship, serve their neighbors, and support one another through life's seasons. The church runs community programs and maintains traditions that bind families together across generations. Anniston itself grew from iron foundries into a town known for its museums, tree-lined streets, and deep community roots.

*United Methodist Church is based in [Anniston, Alabama](/places/anniston). 1010 Christine Ave, Anniston, AL 36207, USA*
