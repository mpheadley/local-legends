West Anniston Methodist Church has served its community since the nineteenth century, offering worship and fellowship to generations of Anniston families. The congregation gathers to study Scripture, support one another through life's challenges, and live out their faith in practical ways. Their doors remain open to anyone seeking spiritual community and a place to belong. Anniston itself grew from iron and textiles into a city known for its museums and tree-lined streets.

*West Anniston Methodist Church is based in [Anniston, Alabama](/places/anniston). Anniston, AL 36201, USA*
