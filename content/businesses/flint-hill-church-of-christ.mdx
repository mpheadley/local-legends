Flint Hill Church of Christ has been a steady anchor for the Bessemer community for generations, offering worship and fellowship to folks seeking spiritual grounding. Located on Flint Hill Road, this congregation welcomes neighbors through its doors with the kind of genuine hospitality that defines small-town Alabama. The church remains a gathering place where people know one another and look out for each other. Bessemer itself carries the proud history of Alabama's industrial past and continues as a working community.
