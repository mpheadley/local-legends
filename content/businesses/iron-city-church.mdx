Iron City Church gathers folks on Highland Avenue who want faith without pretense. They worship together, serve their neighbors, and ask real questions about what it means to follow Jesus in modern times. The congregation ranges from longtime Birminghamians to newcomers finding their way. In a city with deep roots and ongoing transformation, Iron City represents how faith communities are adapting and staying relevant.

*Iron City Church is based in [Birmingham, Alabama](/places/birmingham). 2035 Highland Ave, Birmingham, AL 35205, USA · [ironcitychurch.org](http://ironcitychurch.org) · (205) 920-1968*
