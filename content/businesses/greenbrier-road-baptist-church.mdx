Greenbrier Road Baptist Church has served Anniston's community since its founding, offering worship, fellowship, and spiritual guidance to generations of families. Located on Greenbrier Dear Road, the congregation welcomes visitors through its doors each week for Sunday services and midweek programs. Their website and phone line keep folks connected to upcoming events and prayer needs. Anniston itself has long been a place where neighbors know neighbors and faith runs deep in the heart of East Alabama.
