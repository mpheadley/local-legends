Haven United Methodist Church has served Anniston's Fifteenth Street neighborhood since the early 1900s. The congregation gathers at their modest brick building to worship, fellowship, and support one another through life's seasons. They welcome visitors to Sunday services and community events throughout the year. Anniston itself carries deep roots in Alabama's industrial past, with tree-lined streets that reflect generations of faith and neighborly connection.
