# Wheeling Chapel

Since they started playing together, Wheeling Chapel has been leading worship at their Bessemer church on Avenue C. This band knows how to bring people together through music that speaks to faith and community. They play regularly for folks looking to connect with something deeper on Sunday mornings. Bessemer itself has roots running deep, a steel town where neighbors still know each other's names.
