On West Oxmoor Road in Birmingham, the Homewood Church of Christ welcomes folks looking for straightforward faith and honest community. Sunday services, Bible study, and genuine fellowship form the backbone of what they do, rooted in Scripture and shared values. You'll find a congregation that takes their beliefs seriously without pretense. The Homewood neighborhood itself reflects Birmingham's steady character, a place where longtime residents know their neighbors.

*Homewood Church of Christ is based in [Birmingham, Alabama](/places/birmingham). 265 W Oxmoor Rd, Birmingham, AL 35209, USA · [homewoodchurch.org](http://homewoodchurch.org) · (205) 942-5683*
