When Auburn brides need a wedding dress, they head to Behind the Glass on Magnolia Avenue. The shop carries gowns from established designers alongside pieces you won't find elsewhere, and the staff takes time getting to know each bride's vision. They understand that finding the right dress matters deeply. In a college town that values tradition, this bridal shop has become a quiet landmark for life's biggest moments.

*Behind the Glass is based in [Auburn, Alabama](/places/auburn). 168 E Magnolia Ave, Auburn, AL 36830, USA · [btgauburn.com](http://www.btgauburn.com) · (334) 826-1133*
