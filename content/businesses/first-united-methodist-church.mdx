For over a century, First United Methodist Church has anchored downtown Birmingham's spiritual life on 19th Street North. The congregation welcomes folks seeking meaningful worship, community service, and fellowship rooted in Methodist tradition. Whether you're a longtime member or new to the city, you'll find genuine hospitality here. Downtown Birmingham's historic neighborhoods reflect the resilience and faith that built this city.
