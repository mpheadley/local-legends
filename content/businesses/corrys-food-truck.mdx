Corry's Food Truck rolls out of Bessemer serving up meals that stick to your ribs and taste like somebody who knows what they're doing is behind the wheel. You'll find them parked at 2730 Allison-Bonnett Memorial Drive, ready to feed hungry folks looking for real food, not fuss. Give them a call at (205) 370-4038 or check their Facebook for what's cooking that day. Bessemer's the kind of place where a good food truck becomes part of the neighborhood rhythm.
