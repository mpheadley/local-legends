For nearly a century, Alex Kontos Fruit Co has stood on Finley Avenue selling the freshest produce Birmingham can find. What started as a family operation still runs that way, with folks stopping by for fruit, vegetables, and the kind of service you don't find everywhere anymore. The market stall is where neighbors meet neighbors. In this corner of Birmingham, community still means something real.

*Alex Kontos Fruit Co Inc. is based in [Birmingham, Alabama](/places/birmingham). 444 Finley Ave W, Birmingham, AL 35204, USA · [kontosfruit.com](https://kontosfruit.com) · (205) 322-3459*
