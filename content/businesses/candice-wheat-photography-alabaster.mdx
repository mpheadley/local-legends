Candice Wheat captures weddings with a photographer's eye for the moments that matter most. Working out of Alabaster, she builds relationships with couples before the big day, which shows in her work. Her style favors natural light and honest emotion over staged perfection. Alabaster's historic Railroad District, where her studio sits, has quietly become a hub for small creative businesses.

*Candice Wheat Photography is based in [Alabaster, Alabama](/places/alabaster). 1024 Railroad St suite a, Alabaster, AL 35007, USA · [candicewheatphotography.com](http://candicewheatphotography.com)*
