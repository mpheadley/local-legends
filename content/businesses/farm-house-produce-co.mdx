Every Saturday morning at the farmers market, Farm House Produce Co brings fresh vegetables and fruits straight from local growers to Birmingham tables. You'll find tomatoes that actually taste like tomatoes, squash still warm from the field, and folks who know exactly where everything came from. Located at 414 Finley Ave W, they're part of what keeps Birmingham's West Side connected to real food and real people.

*Farm House Produce Co is based in [Birmingham, Alabama](/places/birmingham). 414 Finley Ave W, Birmingham, AL 35204, USA · (205) 251-6530*
