Hot sandwiches served from a food truck on Auburn's main drag have become a go-to stop for hungry folks looking for honest food. Hoagie Express keeps things simple: quality ingredients piled onto fresh bread, made to order while you wait. You'll find them parked up on Veterans Boulevard, where students and townspeople alike line up for lunch. Auburn's got that college-town energy where a good sandwich spot becomes part of everybody's routine.

*Hoagie Express - Food Truck is based in [Auburn, Alabama](/places/auburn). 131 E Veterans Blvd, Auburn, AL 36832, USA · [facebook.com/hoagieexpress](https://www.facebook.com/hoagieexpress) · (334) 559-2947*
