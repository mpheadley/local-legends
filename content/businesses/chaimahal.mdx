Chaimahal parks its food truck on 11th Avenue South, serving up Indian street food that draws folks from across Birmingham. Owner-run and straightforward, they do what they do well: fragrant curries, fresh naan, and chai that'll warm you right through. You'll find them where the city's neighborhoods blend together, in a pocket where hungry people from all walks come looking for something real.

*Chaimahal is based in [Birmingham, Alabama](/places/birmingham). 1413 11th Ave S, Birmingham, AL 35205, USA · (205) 810-1237*
