Guiding Light Church welcomes folks seeking spiritual community on Birmingham's west side. Located on John Rogers Drive, this congregation has built a reputation for genuine fellowship and straightforward preaching that speaks to everyday life. They open their doors to anyone looking for connection and purpose through faith. In a neighborhood where churches anchor neighborhoods, Guiding Light stands as a gathering place for those seeking to worship together.
