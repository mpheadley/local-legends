Recovered By Grace Church in Athens opens its doors to folks seeking healing and faith together. The congregation gathers on Bain Road with a simple mission: to walk alongside people through their struggles and toward restoration. They believe grace isn't earned but given freely, and that belief shapes how they treat everyone who walks through. Athens remains a place where community ties run deep and neighbors still look out for one another.
