Fresh Gulf shrimp meets a food cart on Auburn's College Street, where Shrimp Basket has been slinging fried baskets and po'boys to hungry students and locals for years. The menu keeps things simple: quality shrimp, good seasoning, and sides that stick with you. Order at the window, grab your basket, and find a spot nearby. Auburn's College Street stays lively with folks coming and going, always looking for something good to eat between classes and errands.
