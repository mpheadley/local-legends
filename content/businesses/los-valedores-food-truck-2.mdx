Los Valedores Food Truck #2 serves up authentic Mexican fare from a humble spot on 14th Street South in Birmingham. The kitchen turns out fresh tacos, tamales, and other traditional dishes that taste like home cooking. You'll find real flavors here, not shortcuts. The neighborhood around this food truck reflects Birmingham's growing Latino community and its appetite for genuine regional cooking.
