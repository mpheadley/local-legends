On any given night, Acapulco Bar & Grill fills with folks looking for good food, cold drinks, and live music that gets people moving. Located on Green Springs Highway, this Birmingham spot knows how to bring a crowd together around a stage. The band plays covers that folks actually want to hear, the kind that get you dancing whether you planned to or not. Green Springs has always been where Birmingham comes to unwind.
