Alabaster Church of Christ has been serving its community since 1957, offering a place where folks gather to worship and grow in faith. Located on 5th Avenue, the church welcomes newcomers with genuine Southern hospitality and straightforward Bible teaching. Whether you're looking for a Sunday service or a midweek study group, you'll find a congregation that takes their faith seriously. Alabaster itself has grown from a small town into a thriving suburb while keeping its small-town heart intact.
