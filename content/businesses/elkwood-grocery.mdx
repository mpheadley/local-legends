Out on Old Railroad Bed Road in Ardmore, Elkwood Grocery pops up to bring neighbors what they need. The shop operates as a flexible community gathering spot, selling groceries and goods to folks who might otherwise have to drive far for basics. It's the kind of place that keeps a small town connected. Ardmore sits in the rolling country of northeastern Alabama, where neighbors still know each other's business.
