Sixth Avenue Baptist Church has stood on Birmingham's west side since 1873, rooted in the heart of a community that's weathered hard times and celebrated victories together. The church remains a gathering place where neighbors worship, support one another, and keep faith alive. Sunday services draw folks from across the city who come for the preaching, the singing, and the fellowship. Along Martin Luther King Jr. Drive, the church anchors a neighborhood still finding its way forward.
