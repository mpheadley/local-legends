Grace Tabernacle Apostolic Church has served Birmingham's Red Lane community for decades, offering worship rooted in Apostolic faith and practice. The congregation gathers to pray, study scripture, and support one another through life's seasons. Their doors stay open to anyone seeking spiritual fellowship and a place to belong. In a neighborhood where church remains central to how folks build community, Grace Tabernacle keeps that tradition alive.
