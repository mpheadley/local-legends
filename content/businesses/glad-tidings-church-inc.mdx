Glad Tidings Church has been a spiritual home for Birmingham's faithful since 1914. Located on Brisbane Avenue, this congregation welcomes neighbors seeking genuine fellowship and biblical teaching. Their doors open for worship, prayer, and community service rooted in Christian values. You'll find real people here, not performances. In a city known for its civil rights legacy, Birmingham's churches remain gathering places where faith still matters.
