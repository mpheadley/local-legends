For over a century, Carpenter United Methodist has anchored East Anniston with steady faith and neighborly care. This modest brick sanctuary on F Street has weathered good times and hard times alike, hosting weddings, funerals, and Sunday morning gatherings that bind the community together. The congregation still opens its doors to anyone seeking a place to worship and belong. Anniston's historic neighborhoods hold stories like these in nearly every block.
