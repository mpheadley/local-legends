Stop by 106 N College Street in Auburn and you'll find the Popcorn Company doing one thing right: fresh, flavored popcorn made in small batches. They've got the classics and creative combinations that'll surprise you. It's a pop-up spot, so call ahead at (334) 329-7700 or check auburnpopcorn.com to catch them when they're open. Auburn's downtown keeps evolving with local makers like these who care about quality over flash.
