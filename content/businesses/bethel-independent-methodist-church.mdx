Bethel Independent Methodist Church has stood on Balsam Avenue since the early days of Birmingham, serving its Southwest neighborhood with quiet faithfulness. The congregation gathers for worship and fellowship, rooted in Methodist tradition and community connection. You'll find them at 1716 Balsam Ave SW, where their phone line stays open at (205) 923-0907. This part of Birmingham carries the stories of working families and generations of Southern faith.
