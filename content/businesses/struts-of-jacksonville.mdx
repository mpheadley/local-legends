Struts of Jacksonville serves hot chicken and wings from a food truck parked in town, offering folks a quick meal that tastes like someone cared about making it right. The owners know their neighbors by name and remember what they order. In a place where chain restaurants dominate, Struts provides something made fresh and local, the kind of spot that gives people a reason to gather and eat together.
