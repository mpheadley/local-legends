In Birmingham's historic Southside, Avenue D Events opens its doors to couples ready to say "I do" their way. This warehouse venue on Fourth Avenue gives you blank canvas rooms, exposed brick, and natural light to build the wedding you've actually want. The team knows how to work with your vision, not against it. The neighborhood itself is full of good restaurants and shops, making it easy for guests to spend the whole day around your celebration.
