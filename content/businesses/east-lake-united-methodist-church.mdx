East Lake United Methodist Church has served Birmingham's South Side since 1902, anchoring a neighborhood with steady faith and community presence. The congregation welcomes folks through its doors on First Avenue, offering worship, fellowship, and the kind of steadfast commitment that's carried families through generations. You'll find plain-spoken kindness here, rooted in Methodist tradition. Birmingham's South Side neighborhoods remain places where churches like this one keep community bonds strong.
