Copeland Presbyterian Church has anchored the Athens community since its founding, welcoming folks through its doors on Copeland Road for generations. The congregation gathers for worship, fellowship, and service rooted in their faith tradition. Whether you're local or passing through, you'll find a place among neighbors who know the value of showing up for one another. Athens remains a town where these kinds of gathering places still matter.
