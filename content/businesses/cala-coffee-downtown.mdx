Pull up to Cala Coffee Downtown in Birmingham and you'll find craft espresso drinks and pastries from folks who actually care about their work. Located on Third Avenue North, they're doing mobile coffee the right way, building relationships one cup at a time. It's the kind of spot that reminds you Birmingham's neighborhoods are full of people making something real happen, one morning at a time.

*Cala Coffee Downtown is based in [Birmingham, Alabama](/places/birmingham). 1826 3rd Ave N #101, Birmingham, AL 35203, USA · [calacoffeeco.com](http://calacoffeeco.com) · (205) 632-1040*
