Hoover First United Methodist Church has anchored its Birmingham community since 1934, when it opened its doors on Patton Chapel Road. The congregation gathers for worship, fellowship, and service work that reaches beyond the church walls into neighborhoods that need it most. Whether you're seeking spiritual roots or community connection, you'll find neighbors who show up for each other. In this part of Birmingham, faith and neighbor-helping still run deep.

*Hoover First United Methodist Church is based in [Birmingham, Alabama](/places/birmingham). 1934 Patton Chapel Rd, Birmingham, AL 35226, USA · [hooverfirstumc.org](http://www.hooverfirstumc.org) · (205) 822-8099*
