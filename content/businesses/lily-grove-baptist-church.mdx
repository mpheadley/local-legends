Lily Grove Baptist Church has been a spiritual home for Birmingham's North End since 1909. Located on 20th Street, this congregation gathers for worship and community service, grounded in faith and neighborhood ties. Members work together to support one another and strengthen their surroundings. In a city known for its civil rights history, churches like Lily Grove remain vital gathering places where folks come together on Sundays and beyond.
