For over seventy years, Flowers Baking Company has been crafting wedding cakes that taste as good as they look. Located in Bessemer on Highway 150, they handle everything from custom designs to delivery, working with couples to make their big day sweeter. You'll find their cakes at celebrations across the region. Bessemer's been a working-class heart of Alabama for generations, and Flowers Baking fits right into that reliable, dependable spirit.
