Fresh Value Oxford brings grocery shopping to neighborhoods that need it most. Operating as a mobile market, they stock fresh produce, meats, and pantry staples, then drive the store right to Oxford residents who might otherwise struggle to reach a traditional supermarket. It's a practical solution to a real problem, making healthy food accessible without the hassle of transportation. They're doing necessary work in the community, one stop at a time.

*Fresh Value Oxford is based in [Oxford, Alabama](/places/oxford). 601 Main St, Oxford, AL 36203, USA · (256) 831-5510 · [oxford.fresh-value.com](https://oxford.fresh-value.com)*
