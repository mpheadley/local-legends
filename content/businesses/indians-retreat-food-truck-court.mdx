Indians Retreat in Athens brings together food trucks in one spot, letting folks grab what they're hungry for without hunting all over town. Whether you want barbecue, tacos, or something else entirely, you'll find it parked right there on East Limestone Road. It's a simple idea that works: pick your truck, pick your meal, grab a seat. Athens keeps finding ways to bring neighbors together around good food.
