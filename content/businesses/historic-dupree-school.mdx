In the red clay country around Ashford, a bluegrass band called Historic Dupree School carries forward the sound of old-time string music. These players know their roots, picking their instruments with the kind of feeling that comes from growing up around these melodies. You'll find them playing at festivals and gatherings across the region, keeping alive a tradition that matters to folks who value where they come from. Ashford sits in the heart of Houston County, where rural Alabama still moves at its own pace.
