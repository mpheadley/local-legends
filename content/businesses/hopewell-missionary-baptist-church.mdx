Hopewell Missionary Baptist Church has anchored Birmingham's North Avenue neighborhood since its founding, serving as a spiritual home and gathering place for generations of worshippers. The congregation continues its mission of faith, community support, and fellowship, welcoming visitors through its doors each Sunday. Located at 2315 26th Avenue North, Hopewell remains a cornerstone of this historic part of Birmingham, where deep roots run through the very streets themselves.
