You can grab a burger and a cold drink from Milo's mobile stand near Jordan-Hare Stadium, where Auburn folks have been stopping by for years. They keep things simple: quality burgers cooked fresh and their famous sweet tea that hits different on a Saturday afternoon. It's the kind of spot where you know what you're getting and that's exactly the point. Auburn's got that college-town energy where a good burger spot becomes part of the rhythm.
