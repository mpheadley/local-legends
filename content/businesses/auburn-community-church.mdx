Auburn Community Church welcomes folks of all backgrounds through its doors on Airport Road. The congregation gathers for worship, study, and service rooted in Scripture and neighborly care. They focus on building genuine connections and helping Auburn's people navigate life's big questions together. In a college town that's always moving, this church offers a steady place where folks can belong.

*Auburn Community Church is based in [Auburn, Alabama](/places/auburn). 357 Airport Rd, Auburn, AL 36830, USA · [auburncommunitychurch.com](https://www.auburncommunitychurch.com)*
