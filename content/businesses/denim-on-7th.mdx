You can catch live bands seven nights a week at Denim on 7th in Birmingham's Southside. The intimate venue on 7th Avenue draws musicians and folks looking for real music in a comfortable space. The setup keeps things straightforward: good performers, cold drinks, and room to move around. It's the kind of place where Birmingham's music scene still has room for bands to play without pretense or fuss.

*Denim on 7th is based in [Birmingham, Alabama](/places/birmingham). 2808 7th Ave S #105, Birmingham, AL 35233, USA · [denimon7th.com](http://denimon7th.com) · (205) 203-2409*
