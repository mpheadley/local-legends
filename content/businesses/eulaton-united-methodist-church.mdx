For over a century, Eulaton United Methodist Church has opened its doors to Anniston's faithful on Westover Road. The congregation gathers for worship, fellowship, and service rooted in Methodist tradition. Whether you're a lifelong member or visiting town, you'll find a welcoming community committed to living out their faith. Anniston itself carries deep history as a planned industrial city with surprising cultural riches tucked along its tree-lined streets.
