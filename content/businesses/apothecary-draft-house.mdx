Apothecary Draft House in Jacksonville books live bands several nights a week, giving local and touring musicians a stage and giving neighbors a reason to gather. The place serves beer and food in a casual setting where the focus stays on the music. In a small town, a venue like this matters because it keeps live performance alive and gives the community somewhere to belong on a Friday night.
