Pull up to Sabor Catracho Food Truck on Green Springs Highway for honest Honduran cooking. Owner serves up pupusas, baleadas, and fresh seafood that tastes like home, made right there in the window. The prices won't hurt your wallet, and the folks running it treat you like family. Birmingham's Green Springs neighborhood keeps growing, drawing folks hungry for real food and real stories.

*Sabor Catracho Food Truck is based in [Birmingham, Alabama](/places/birmingham). 2398 Green Springs Hwy, Birmingham, AL 35205, USA · (205) 872-5850*
