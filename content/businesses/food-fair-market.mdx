Food Fair Market brings honest groceries to Birmingham's north side, stocking what folks need for their tables. You'll find fresh produce, meats, and staples at prices that make sense. The market's been a neighborhood fixture, serving the community with straightforward service and reliable quality. On 18th Street North, it's woven into the everyday life of a hardworking part of town.

*Food Fair Market is based in [Birmingham, Alabama](/places/birmingham). 1500 18th St N, Birmingham, AL 35234, USA · (205) 447-7300*
