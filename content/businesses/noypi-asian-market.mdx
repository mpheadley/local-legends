You can find authentic Filipino and Asian cuisine rolling through Anniston from Noypi Asian Market's mobile kitchen. Owner serves up favorites like lumpia, adobo, and fresh noodle dishes that bring real flavors to your neighborhood. The food comes straight from family recipes and traditional techniques passed down through generations. Anniston's diverse community has embraced this mobile spot as a gathering place for good eating and good company.
