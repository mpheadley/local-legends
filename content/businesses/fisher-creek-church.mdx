Nestled on Cox Chapel Road, Fisher Creek Church has served the Attalla community for generations, offering a quiet place for worship and fellowship. The church anchors this corner of Calhoun County with Sunday services and gatherings that bring neighbors together. Whether you're passing through or putting down roots, you'll find the kind of faith-centered gathering that's woven into the fabric of small-town Alabama life. Attalla itself sits in the rolling foothills where industry and countryside meet.

*Fisher Creek Church is based in [Attalla, Alabama](/places/attalla). 88 Cox Chapel Rd, Attalla, AL 35954, USA*
