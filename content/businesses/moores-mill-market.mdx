Moores Mill Market brings fresh goods and honest food to Auburn shoppers looking for quality ingredients and prepared meals. The market stall at 1899 Ogletree Road stocks what locals need, from produce to ready-to-eat options, without any fuss or pretense. It's the kind of place where folks know they're getting real value and real food. Auburn's growing east side keeps finding new reasons to gather around spots like this.
