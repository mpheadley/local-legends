Ramona J's food truck serves up honest cooking that keeps folks coming back to 1212 US-31 in Athens. The menu changes with the seasons, but you'll always find fresh, made-from-scratch meals that taste like somebody cared about every bite. Stop by and you'll see why regulars consider this spot their go-to for a real meal. Athens has always been a place where good food brings the community together.
