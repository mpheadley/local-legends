# Courtyard 280

Out on US-280, Courtyard 280 pulls folks in for live music most nights of the week. The venue keeps things unpretentious, the kind of place where you might catch anything from country to rock to soul. It's the sort of spot where neighbors become friends over a cold drink and honest entertainment. Birmingham's 280 corridor keeps growing, drawing folks looking for good food, good drinks, and good times.
