On a quiet stretch of 29th Street South, Innisfree Pub brings live music to Birmingham's evenings. The Irish pub draws folks looking for good beer, honest food, and the kind of entertainment that happens when a band takes the stage. Whether you're catching local musicians or joining regulars at the bar, there's a welcoming feel that keeps people coming back. This neighborhood has quietly become a destination for those seeking something real.
