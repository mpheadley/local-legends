Yume Japanese & Asian Cuisine brings fresh ramen, sushi, and other Japanese fare to Oxford from a food truck and market space. Owner-operated and focused on quality ingredients, it fills a genuine gap in the area's dining scene. Whether you're after a quick bowl of noodles or ingredients to cook at home, Yume offers something most Northeast Alabama restaurants don't: authentic Asian food made with care.
