Presbyterian Community Ministry on East Thach Avenue has been serving Auburn's students and neighbors for decades, offering meals, housing assistance, and fellowship to anyone who walks through their doors. They work quietly, showing up for folks when life gets hard. You'll find church, sure, but mostly you'll find people who genuinely want to help. Auburn's a college town that moves fast, and this place keeps its feet planted where it matters most.
