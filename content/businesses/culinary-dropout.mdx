Located on Summit Boulevard, this spot brings together good music and honest food without pretension. The kitchen takes its work seriously, but the atmosphere stays loose and welcoming. Birmingham's dining scene keeps finding new ways to surprise, and this place proves the city's creative spirit runs deep.
