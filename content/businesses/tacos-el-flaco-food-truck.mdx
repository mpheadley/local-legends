Rolling up on Green Springs Avenue, Tacos El Flaco serves the kind of food that sticks with you: slow-cooked meats wrapped in fresh tortillas, simple and honest. The truck draws a steady crowd of folks who know what real Mexican street food tastes like. Owner and crew work with the kind of care that shows in every plate. Birmingham's neighborhoods have found their spot for a genuine meal that won't break the bank.
