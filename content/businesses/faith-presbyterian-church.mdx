Faith Presbyterian Church on Valleydale Road has been serving Birmingham families for decades with worship, Bible study, and community fellowship. They welcome visitors looking for a traditional Reformed faith experience in a warm, genuine atmosphere. Their doors open for Sunday services and throughout the week for those seeking spiritual community. In the Valleydale neighborhood, this church remains a steady gathering place for folks who take their faith seriously.

*Faith Presbyterian Church is based in [Birmingham, Alabama](/places/birmingham). 4601 Valleydale Rd, Birmingham, AL 35242, USA · [faith-pca.org](https://www.faith-pca.org) · (205) 991-5430*
