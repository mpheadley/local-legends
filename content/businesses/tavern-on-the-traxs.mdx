Live music keeps this College Street spot humming most nights, drawing Auburn folks looking for a cold drink and good company. The bands rotate regular, so you might catch anything from country to rock depending when you wander in. It's straightforward tavern fare: nothing fancy, just a place where people gather to hear real musicians play. Auburn's downtown still knows how to have a proper night out.
