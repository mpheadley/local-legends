Greater New Height Baptist Church has been a steady presence on Birmingham's northside since 1947, offering worship and community support to families in the 34th Avenue neighborhood. The congregation gathers at their home on 34th Avenue North to pray, sing, and help one another through life's challenges. In a city shaped by faith and perseverance, neighborhoods like this one hold the stories that make Birmingham real.
