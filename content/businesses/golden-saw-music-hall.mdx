Golden Saw Music Hall in Jacksonville keeps live country music alive in a corner of Northeast Alabama where that matters. Local and traveling bands pick and grin most weekends, giving folks a place to hear real music played by real musicians. It's the kind of venue that remembers why honky-tonks matter—community, good songs, and people who show up for each other.

*Golden Saw Music Hall is based in [Jacksonville, Alabama](/places/jacksonville). 5196 Pleasant Valley Rd, Jacksonville, AL 36265, USA · [m.facebook.com/PickingNGrining](https://m.facebook.com/PickingNGrining)*
