From a mobile kitchen on Adamsville's Main Street, Woodbury's Food for the Soul serves up honest cooking that sticks to your ribs. The menu changes with what's good and what feels right, built on recipes and instincts passed down through family. You'll find folks from three counties over pulling up for a plate. Adamsville's the kind of place where everybody knows what matters: good food and good people showing up for each other.

*Woodbury's Food for the Soul is based in [Adamsville, Alabama](/places/adamsville). 4814 Main St, Adamsville, AL 35005, USA · (404) 457-3478*
