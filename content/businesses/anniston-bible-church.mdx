Anniston Bible Church on Coleman Road has been serving the community with straightforward preaching and a focus on Scripture for decades. Members gather Sunday mornings to study the Bible together, finding strength in fellowship and faith. The church welcomes newcomers looking for a steady, no-frills place to worship. Anniston itself has deep roots in Alabama's industrial past, with churches like this one anchoring neighborhoods across town.
