Greater Shiloh Missionary Baptist Church has served Birmingham's Smithfield neighborhood since 1902, anchoring community life through worship, fellowship, and support for those in need. Located on Jefferson Avenue, the church opens its doors to anyone seeking spiritual growth and Christian community. Members gather weekly to pray, sing, and strengthen bonds forged over generations. Smithfield has always been a place where neighbors know each other's names and look out for one another.

*Greater Shiloh Missionary Baptist Church is based in [Birmingham, Alabama](/places/birmingham). 2135 Jefferson Ave SW, Birmingham, AL 35211, USA · [greatershiloh.org](http://greatershiloh.org) · (205) 925-9750*
