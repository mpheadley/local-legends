Red Mountain Crawfish brings Louisiana straight to Birmingham out of a food truck parked on Vulcan Road. They steam crawfish with corn, potatoes, and spices, then pile it all on butcher paper for eating the way folks down in the bayou know how. You grab your mallets and dig in, getting your hands messy and your taste buds happy. It's the kind of casual eating that fits right into Birmingham's growing food scene.

*Red Mountain Crawfish is based in [Birmingham, Alabama](/places/birmingham). 195 Vulcan Rd, Birmingham, AL 35209, USA · [redmountaincrawfish.com](http://www.redmountaincrawfish.com) · (205) 590-6950*
