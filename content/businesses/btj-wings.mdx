Roll up to BtJ Wings in Anniston and you'll find a food truck serious about its craft. They smoke their wings low and slow, then finish them hot, letting the meat speak louder than any sauce. The flavors land different here, layered and genuine, the kind of food that keeps folks coming back. Quintard Avenue has always been where Anniston's working folks grab a real meal.

*BtJ Wings is based in [Anniston, Alabama](/places/anniston). 2129 Quintard Ave, Anniston, AL 36201, USA · [btjwingsanniston.fronteats.com](http://btjwingsanniston.fronteats.com) · (256) 624-6888*
