Shades Mountain Baptist Church has anchored Birmingham's Southside for generations, welcoming folks through its doors on Columbiana Road. The congregation gathers for worship, fellowship, and service rooted in faith and community commitment. Whether you're a longtime member or visiting Birmingham, you'll find a church grounded in Southern Baptist tradition. The Southside neighborhood itself reflects the kind of steady, neighborly character that defines so much of what Birmingham has always been.

*Shades Mountain Baptist Church is based in [Birmingham, Alabama](/places/birmingham). 2017 Columbiana Rd, Birmingham, AL 35216, USA · [shades.org](https://www.shades.org) · (205) 822-1670*
