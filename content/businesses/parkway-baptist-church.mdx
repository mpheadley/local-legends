Parkway Baptist Church has been a cornerstone of Auburn's East University Drive for generations, welcoming students and families through its doors with genuine warmth. The congregation gathers for worship, fellowship, and service rooted in their Baptist faith and commitment to the community. Whether you're new to town or returning home, you'll find a place at their table. Auburn's tree-lined streets and college-town spirit draw people seeking connection and belonging.
