Fusion Tacos rolls up to Town Center Drive in Anniston with something different: tacos that blend flavors from across the map instead of sticking to tradition. The food truck keeps things honest, mixing fresh ingredients into combinations that work. You'll find folks lining up during lunch and dinner, drawn by the creativity and quality. Anniston's Town Center has become a gathering spot where locals discover new tastes without leaving home.

*Fusion Tacos is based in [Anniston, Alabama](/places/anniston). 171 Town Center Dr, Anniston, AL 36205, USA*
