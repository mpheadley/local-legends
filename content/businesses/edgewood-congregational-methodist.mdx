Edgewood Congregational Methodist has been a steady spiritual home for Anniston folks since way back. They gather on Brighton Avenue to worship, sing, and support one another through life's ups and downs. The church welcomes newcomers and longtime members alike, staying true to their Methodist roots while meeting the community's needs. In a town where churches anchor neighborhoods, Edgewood remains a familiar landmark where people still believe in showing up for each other.
