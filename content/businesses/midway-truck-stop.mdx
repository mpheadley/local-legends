Out on US-43 in Axis, you'll find Midway Truck Stop slinging honest food to folks passing through. It's the kind of place where hungry travelers know they can pull in and get something real to eat, made straightforward and served without fuss. The menu sticks to what works, feeding hungry stomachs on the road. Axis sits along the corridor between larger towns, a crossroads community where the truck stop serves as a natural gathering spot.
