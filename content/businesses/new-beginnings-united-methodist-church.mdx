Tucked along 32nd Avenue North in Birmingham, New Beginnings United Methodist Church opens its doors to folks looking for community and faith. The congregation gathers for worship and service, grounded in Methodist tradition while staying connected to neighbors who need a hand. Their website and phone line keep people informed about services and ways to get involved. Birmingham's Norwood neighborhood has long been a place where churches like this one anchor the heart of their communities.
