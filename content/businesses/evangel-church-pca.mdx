Evangel Church PCA in Alabaster brings folks together around Reformed theology and faithful teaching. Located on Thompson Road, this Presbyterian congregation emphasizes Scripture and community worship without the pretense you might expect. They've built something straightforward: a place where believers gather to study God's Word and grow in their faith. Alabaster itself has become a growing bedroom community south of Birmingham, where families put down roots and build lives together.

*Evangel Church PCA is based in [Alabaster, Alabama](/places/alabaster). 423 Thompson Rd, Alabaster, AL 35007, USA · [evangelchurchpca.org](https://www.evangelchurchpca.org) · (205) 664-0889*
