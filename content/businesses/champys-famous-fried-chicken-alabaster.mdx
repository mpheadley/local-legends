On Highway 119 in Alabaster, Champy's Famous Fried Chicken serves up crispy birds alongside live music that'll keep you dancing past dinner. The chicken comes out golden and hot, the kind of food that tastes like somebody's grandmother knew what she was doing. Grab a seat, order a plate, and stick around for the band. Alabaster's grown into quite the spot for folks looking for good eating and honest entertainment.
