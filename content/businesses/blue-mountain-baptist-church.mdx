For over a century, Blue Mountain Baptist Church has anchored the Anniston community with faithful worship and service. Located on Blue Mountain Road West, the congregation gathers to study scripture, support neighbors, and live out their faith together. Whether you're visiting or calling Anniston home, you'll find genuine welcome here. The church reflects the kind of steady spiritual presence that built communities across the rural South.
