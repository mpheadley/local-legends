Roses Express brings discount shopping to Bessemer Road, where folks can find name-brand goods at prices that don't break the bank. This pop-up shop stocks everything from clothing to household items, keeping things fresh and affordable for the community. You'll never know exactly what you'll find when you walk through the door, which is part of the fun. Birmingham's Bessemer corridor has long been a neighborhood where people know how to stretch a dollar.

*Roses Express is based in [Birmingham, Alabama](/places/birmingham). 2217-A Bessemer Rd, Birmingham, AL 35208, USA · [rosesdiscountstores.com](http://www.rosesdiscountstores.com) · (205) 781-2996*
