Life In Abundance Fellowship Church in Birmingham offers folks a place where faith meets everyday living. Located on Park Lawn Avenue, the congregation focuses on practical spirituality and community connection rather than just Sunday service. They gather to study Scripture, support one another, and work outward into their neighborhood. Birmingham's southwest side has long been a place where churches serve as anchors for their communities.
