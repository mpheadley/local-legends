For over a century, West Highland Baptist Church has anchored a corner of Athens with steady faith and open doors. The congregation gathers at their Fifth Avenue home to worship, serve their neighbors, and build community roots that run deep. You'll find them welcoming visitors and living out their beliefs through local mission work. Athens itself grew up around churches like this one, places where people still gather to matter to each other.
