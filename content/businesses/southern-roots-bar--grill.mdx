Southern Roots Bar & Grill brings live music back to Anniston's nightlife. Located downtown, they host local and regional bands most weekends, giving musicians a steady stage and giving folks a reason to get out and hear real music played by real people. It's the kind of place where neighbors run into neighbors, and the sound of a guitar means something.
