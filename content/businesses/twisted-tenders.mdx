If you're driving through Athens and craving something fried and finger-licking good, Twisted Tenders pulls up with chicken tenders that'll make you forget every chain restaurant you've ever known. Operating from their mobile setup on Athens-Limestone Boulevard, they nail the simple stuff: quality bird, proper seasoning, and a no-fuss approach to feeding hungry folks. You can find them ready to go or call ahead at 256-216-1880. Athens has always been the kind of place where good food finds you on the road.
