Camp Branch United Methodist Church has been serving Alabaster families for generations, offering Sunday worship, Bible study, and a welcoming community for folks seeking spiritual connection. Located on quiet Camp Branch Road, the church opens its doors to neighbors looking for fellowship and faith. You'll find genuine people here, rooted in Methodist tradition and committed to their community. Alabaster itself has grown considerably while maintaining the small-town character that draws people to its tree-lined neighborhoods.
