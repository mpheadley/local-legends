Live bands fill the room most nights at Fairway Social, a Birmingham spot where you can catch good music without the fuss. They keep the vibe relaxed, the drinks flowing, and the stage active with local and touring acts. The 13th Street South location sits in the heart of a neighborhood that's been rediscovering itself one live show, one good meal, and one craft cocktail at a time.

*Fairway Social Birmingham is based in [Birmingham, Alabama](/places/birmingham). 114 13th St S, Birmingham, AL 35233, USA · [fairway-social-birmingham.com](http://www.fairway-social-birmingham.com) · (205) 286-6893*
