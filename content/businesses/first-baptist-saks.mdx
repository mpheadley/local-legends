First Baptist Saks has served the Anniston community since 1901, offering a place where neighbors gather for worship and fellowship. The church welcomes families of all backgrounds to its Sunday services and community programs along Saks Road. Their focus remains simple: sharing faith and supporting one another through life's seasons. Anniston's rural character and strong sense of community make it a place where traditions like this one still matter.
