Rosa Lee brings bridal dreams to life in downtown Anniston. The shop carries wedding dresses, bridesmaid gowns, and formal wear for the big day and beyond. Owner Rosa Lee knows her customers by name and takes time getting the fit right, understanding that a wedding dress carries weight beyond fabric and thread. For generations of Calhoun County brides, Rosa Lee has been the place where those moments begin.
