Every spring in Anniston, the Weaver FFA chapter gathers to honor its members and celebrate what they've accomplished in agriculture and leadership. The banquet brings together students, families, and mentors for an evening of good food, recognition, and real talk about what it takes to build something from the ground up. It's the kind of event where you see the next generation of farmers and rural leaders taking shape. Anniston's been rooted in community gatherings like this for generations.
