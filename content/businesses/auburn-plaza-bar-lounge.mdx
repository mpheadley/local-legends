Live music fills the nights at Auburn Plaza Bar & Lounge on Main Street, where local and touring bands take the stage while folks settle in with drinks and good company. The place has that comfortable, worn-in feel where regulars know the bartenders and strangers become friends by the end of the night. Auburn's downtown has always been a gathering spot where students and townspeople mix, and this venue sits right at the heart of it all.
