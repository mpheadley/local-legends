Edwards Lake Church of Christ has been a steady presence in Birmingham since 1952, serving its community with straightforward faith and practical care. Located on Medical Park Drive, the congregation welcomes folks looking for genuine connection and biblical teaching without pretension. They gather weekly for worship, study, and fellowship, staying rooted in their commitment to Scripture and service. Medical Park sits in a neighborhood where families have built lives for generations, and this church remains part of that fabric.

*Edwards Lake Church of Christ is based in [Birmingham, Alabama](/places/birmingham). 800 Medical Park Dr, Birmingham, AL 35235, USA · [edwardslakechurch.org](http://edwardslakechurch.org)*
