Ono Grill rolls through Anniston serving sushi, hibachi, and Chinese food from a mobile kitchen on McClellan Boulevard. The menu mixes Japanese precision with Asian comfort food, offering something different in a town where strip mall dining dominates. You'll find fresh rolls alongside stir-fries and hibachi plates cooked to order. Anniston's been reinventing itself for years, and spots like this show how the city keeps surprising folks willing to look beyond the obvious.
