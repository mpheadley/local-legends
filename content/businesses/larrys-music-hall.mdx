Larry's Music Hall in Anniston keeps the live band tradition alive on AL-202, where folks gather for honest entertainment and good times. The venue books regional acts that get people on their feet, the kind of place where neighbors catch up between songs and the music runs real. You'll find everything from country to rock to whatever else the band feels like playing that night. Anniston's music scene has always been about community first.
