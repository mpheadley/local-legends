At The Picker's Choice in Arab, you'll find honest goods gathered from estate sales and auctions, the kind of treasures folks have held onto for generations. The selection shifts weekly, so regulars know to stop by often for vintage finds, antiques, and curiosities that tell stories. You never quite know what you'll discover on those shelves. Arab sits in Marshall County's rolling foothills, a quiet town where picking through the past feels like second nature.
