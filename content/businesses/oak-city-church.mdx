Oak City Church opens its doors to Birmingham folks looking for honest faith and real community. Located on Greendale Road, this congregation welcomes newcomers with a straightforward approach to worship and fellowship. They're built on the belief that church should feel like home, not a formal performance. Birmingham's neighborhoods thrive when places like this bring people together around something that matters.

*Oak City Church is based in [Birmingham, Alabama](/places/birmingham). 3251 Greendale Rd, Birmingham, AL 35243, USA · [oakcitybirmingham.com](http://www.oakcitybirmingham.com)*
