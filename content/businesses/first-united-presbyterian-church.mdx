First United Presbyterian Church has anchored the Tomahawk Road community in Birmingham since its founding, offering worship and fellowship to generations of families. The congregation gathers each week for traditional services rooted in Presbyterian faith and values. Members work together on local outreach, strengthening bonds between neighbors and serving those in need. Birmingham's neighborhoods thrive when institutions like this one remain committed to their people.
