The Church at Brook Hills gathers folks in Birmingham who want worship that feels genuine and grounded. Pastor Chip Henderson leads the community through teaching that connects Scripture to everyday life, drawing families from across the city. Located in the Brook Highland neighborhood, the church emphasizes belonging over perfection, creating space where real people work out their faith together. Birmingham's south side has grown into a vibrant area where churches like this one anchor their communities.
