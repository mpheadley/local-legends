Send Noodz LLC operates a food truck in Oxford serving noodle dishes that hit different from the usual county fare. The owners focus on quality ingredients and flavors that reflect their genuine interests, not what focus groups say sells. They've built a small following of folks who appreciate showing up to a parking lot and finding something made with care. That matters in a place where chain restaurants dominate.
