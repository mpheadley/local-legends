# Voice Sample — Adlene Email
*Date: 2026-05-19 | Context: Personal reconnection after 4 years of silence*
*Use: Founder Note register, personal address tone, vulnerable disclosure*

---

Hi Adlene,

It's Matt. I know it's been a long time. I pray you and your family are doing well!

I was in touch with you and TK until about 2021. I was going through a difficult time while at Anniston First. I didn't know it, but I was beginning to suffer from clinical depression, and I fell out of touch with y'all. I have regretted that over these years. Heather and I have thought about you all many times. You have always been so kind to us and our children.

It's been a hard few years. I got diagnosed bipolar. I've left the job at Anniston and got healthy and stable again. We've moved back to Jacksonville. I'm in a different place now, and I'm building again.

Two projects I've been working on that might be a resource to you and your circles. The first is a set of pastoral resources for couples seeking a marriage blessing or reaffirmation of vows — you know pastors are often asked about this and struggle to find a clear place to point people. I've built those at theaisle.app/marriage-blessing. The second is a sermon prep tool built with United Methodist pastors in mind. Free to check it out at sermoncoach.app.

I'm planning to be at Annual Conference. I've wondered if there might be an opportunity to be a vendor there — if it's too late, I totally understand, but I wanted to put the idea out there.

I'd love to reconnect with you there.

Sending love and gratitude to you and your family.

Matt

---

## Why This Is a Voice Reference

- Plain sentences. No performance.
- Disclosure without explanation: "I got diagnosed bipolar."
  Not "I struggled with mental health challenges." The noun, not the euphemism.
- Regret named directly: "I have regretted that over these years."
- Transition that earns the pivot: "I'm in a different place now, and I'm building again."
  The personal context makes the product mention land differently.
- Ends warm without being saccharine.
- No kill-list words anywhere.
- This is the Founder Note register at its best.
