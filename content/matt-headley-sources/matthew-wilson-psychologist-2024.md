## ADHD diagnosis

This is the first documented mention of an ADHD diagnosis in the archive. Matt was diagnosed ADHD by Matthew Wilson, a psychologist. The timing — during the 2024 depression — and the subsequent mania raise clinical questions about the diagnosis and the treatment path that followed. Bipolar disorder and ADHD are common comorbidities; misdiagnosis in one direction is documented in psychiatric literature. Matt's psychiatrist's strong reaction to the antidepressant advice suggests she has views about what Matthew Wilson was doing in that office.

**For the essay:** The ADHD diagnosis adds to the diagnostic complexity — Matt is a person with bipolar disorder and ADHD who was told by a psychologist not to take antidepressants and then received mushrooms from that same psychologist.
