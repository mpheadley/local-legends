### Case Study: Hiring Process & Structural Challenges

"During my hiring process for the Associate Pastor of Worship role, I was brought into a face-to-face meeting with Pastor Tammy while April was present only via phone. In that meeting, I was informed that:
- I would be hired as a staff person rather than an appointed pastor.
- I would not receive conference benefits.
- My salary would be lower than anticipated, and I had only three days to accept the offer before it would be extended to another candidate.

This created a sense of pressure, and in hindsight, I wonder if the church was intentionally discouraging me from accepting the role due to my qualifications. The structure of my employment placed me in a unique liminal position: I was a pastor with congregational influence, yet I lacked access to the SPRC and was not connected to the broader conference resources available to other clergy."

### Power Dynamics & Personality Differences

- As an Enneagram One, his inclination to be loyal and deferential to leadership led to a dynamic where his willingness to serve was taken for granted.
- Pastor Tammy, as an Enneagram Three, was in a stressful leadership position and often prioritized institutional success over the well-being of staff.
- No proactive conversations about cost-of-living salary increases. When he requested a salary discussion, told it was not possible despite the church's financial gains from the sale of The Bridge and other improvements.
- "I felt that my personal and professional needs were deprioritized in favor of stabilizing the church's financial standing, which left me feeling unsupported."

### Lessons Learned & Path to Growth

**What I Have Learned About Myself:**
- It is essential for me to **stand firm in my decisions** and to advocate for my own well-being as I would for others.
- I must **speak my truth with confidence**, trusting that honesty leads to healthier relationships and stronger leadership.
- I can no longer allow others to make my decisions for me; instead, I must **take ownership of my choices without second-guessing.**
- I have learned the importance of **setting and holding healthy boundaries**—not just for my own protection but for the benefit of everyone involved.
- **When I stop downplaying my needs and priorities, everyone benefits.** Living authentically allows others to do the same.

### Compassion for Pastor Tammy & Shared Responsibility

"I have compassion for Pastor Tammy because I recognize how her actions have, at times, harmed herself and the church. I also acknowledge that my own actions—or my failure to act—have contributed to challenges within the church."

- Pastor Tammy has harmed herself and the church by **valuing her image more than genuine relationships within the congregation.**
- I have harmed myself and the church by **prioritizing correctness over honesty**, suppressing thoughts and emotions that needed to be voiced.
- I have hidden away parts of myself that I believed were "bad," when in reality, those honest thoughts and feelings needed to be expressed for the benefit of everyone.

### Final Thoughts & Hopes for AFUMC

"My hope for AFUMC moving forward is that it will continue to grow in its mission, not just in numbers but in depth of **community, authenticity, and leadership integrity**. I hope that the church will foster an environment where **open dialogue is encouraged**, where both staff and congregants feel **heard, valued, and empowered** to contribute to its vision.

I believe that **transparent leadership, mutual accountability, and a shared commitment to personal and spiritual growth** will allow AFUMC to thrive. My prayer is that this transition, though difficult, will ultimately be a catalyst for the church's ongoing renewal and transformation.

I remain deeply grateful for the congregation, my pastoral experiences, and the relationships formed during my tenure. While my transition has been complex, I hold hope for the church's future and its continued growth in ministry and leadership."

## Notes for the AI psychosis piece

**What this document cluster shows about March 14, 2025:**
