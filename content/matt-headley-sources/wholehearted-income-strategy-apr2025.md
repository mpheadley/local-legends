## Context and annotation

**The timing.** Created April 7, 2025 — 25 days after the resignation (March 13–14), two days after the $87K debt / $25K asset balance sheet. The farm had approximately 7 weeks left of active operation at this point.

**The "Wholehearted" branding.** Every document in this cluster is labeled "Wholehearted" — the same word in the AI Council documents (`chatgpt-memory-backup-feb26-2025.md`). The Wholehearted Collaboration, Wholehearted Finance Hub, Wholehearted Income Strategy. The brand name that NotebookLM was organizing Matt's life under was also the label on the financial survival documents.

**Lawn care at the top.** The highest-ROI near-term idea listed is lawn care / brush clearing — physical labor, using the truck, targeting the truck loan. Matt took construction/physical labor work that fall to survive. This document prefigures it.

**"Preston Pay"** — money owed to Preston appears as a specific debt line item. By fall 2025, Matt was doing construction work for Preston after the farm failed. The relationship inverted: from equity partner to creditor to employer.

**Digital sales as passive.** Near the bottom: e-books, workshops. "Long-term passive" — listed last, targeting the mortgage. The fantasy column, left for last.

**What didn't happen.** None of these strategies saved the farm. The GoFundMe was also active. The farm was sold approximately September 2025.
