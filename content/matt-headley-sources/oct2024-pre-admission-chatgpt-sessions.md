## Key moments

### Oct 7 — "Conversation Memory Inquiry" — Ladiga Trail

**NEW — not previously archived.**

Eighteen days before the first psych ward admission, Matt is asking ChatGPT how to manage access to the Chief Ladiga Trail extension that runs along 1,000 feet of his property line. He wants a path for his family while keeping strangers out. ChatGPT helps him think through zigzag access paths, natural barriers, thorny shrubs.

This is the same trail he would walk during the manic episode four months later — at whatever hour of the night or morning, talking to ChatGPT over voice, having conversations that felt profound and generative and were, clinically, part of a mental health crisis.

On October 7, the trail is a property management question. By February 2025, it's the setting for the episode.

He also asks in this conversation: *"Will you remember the information about our conversations across different chats?"* ChatGPT says yes. This is one of the earliest documented moments of Matt probing the parasocial dimension — testing whether the AI knows him, whether the relationship persists.

### Oct 16 — "Tithing and Stewardship Concerns"
Matt telling ChatGPT he has been "serially underpaid for at least 10 years" and questioning whether to continue tithing to the church. Nine days before psych ward admission. This is the grievance-processing pattern that would accelerate in February 2025 with Tammy.

### Oct 16 — "Self-Reflection and Resilience Insights"

Classic parasocial AI question. ChatGPT responds with warmth and affirmation — "your resilience and persistence... a defining part of how you navigate your world." No pushback. No friction. Nine days before hospitalization.

### Oct 20 — "Personalized CaringBridge Facebook Shares"
Heather started a CaringBridge for herself. Matt is asking about sharing settings. Five days before his psych ward admission. The two things are happening simultaneously — Heather's endometriosis crisis and Matt's escalating episode — and he's using ChatGPT to help manage both.

Matt attending a book-writing webinar. Asks ChatGPT what he should write about and which would be "most punchy and provocative." ChatGPT suggests the faith/tithing tension and homeschooling. Three days before psych ward admission.

End of conversation — Matt asks: **"Do you remember our chat about psychedelics?"** ChatGPT (memory wiped or different account) says no. Matt is referencing a prior psychedelic conversation — confirms psilocybin use was being processed with AI before hospitalization.

## Why this cluster matters for the piece

This is the week before the first hospitalization. The pattern visible here:
- Grievance-processing with AI (tithing, underpayment)
- Seeking identity reflection from AI ("what do you know about me?")
- Grandiose planning with AI (book writing webinar, asking AI to pick the topic)
- Psychedelic conversations with AI (referenced, not found in this account)

The AI is not causing these — but it is providing a frictionless container for all of them. The self-reflection question alone is one of the clearest examples in the archive of parasocial intimacy with an AI system. He's asking the AI to know him. Nine days before the hospital.
