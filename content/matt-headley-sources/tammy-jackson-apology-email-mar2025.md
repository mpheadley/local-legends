## Email (verbatim)

Dear Matt,

I want to apologize for any hurt or frustration my message to the congregation may have caused you. My intention was to honor your ministry at AFUMC, express gratitude for your time with us, and communicate your resignation in a way that reflected the terms you expressed. I deeply regret that my wording—particularly the phrase "effective immediately"—came across as dismissive or as if I were pushing you out. That was never my intent.

In hindsight, I wonder if we may have misunderstood each other at some points in this process. I understood your request to mean that you wished to step away from regular staff responsibilities as soon as possible, and I meant only to acknowledge that the transition was beginning immediately rather than at a later, undetermined date. It was never my intention to imply that a smooth transition would not take place—only that it was now underway. Even the desire to celebrate your family and you this week stemmed from my understanding that you wished to be released from worship responsibilities. I am genuinely sorry that my wording suggested anything different.

Please know that in every conversation we have had, my goal has been to more fully integrate you into our staff and not for you to leave it. Though we have not always agreed, I have always valued your presence, your leadership, and the work you have done to serve this congregation. I regret that my message did not fully convey my respect and appreciation for you.

Again, I am truly sorry for any offense my words caused. It would be my hope that we could move forward in grace. Regardless, I will remain grateful for the ministry we have shared.

Peace,
Tammy
