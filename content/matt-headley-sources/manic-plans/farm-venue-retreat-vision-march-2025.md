**Executive Overview**

The Headley Farm, Venue & Retreat Center is designed to be a multi-purpose agritourism destination integrating permaculture, event hosting, floral arranging, weddings, music performances, spiritual retreats, and sustainable living. Positioned near the Ladiga Trail, Walmart, and Tractor Supply, this property presents unique accessibility for community engagement, tourism, and economic growth.

**Key Takeaways & Immediate Next Actions**

- Grand Opening Event – Coordinate with Calhoun County Chamber of Commerce and City of Anniston to align the farm's launch with the Ladiga Trail Southern Extension opening.
- Funding & Grant Strategy – Work with Justin Marsh (wealth strategy) and Kimberly Westbrooks (grant research) to secure financing for home and farm improvements.
- Team & Delegation Strategy – Begin incremental outreach to Justin Headley (AI & automation), Taylor (career transition), Karrie (branding & marketing), Preston (carpentry & project oversight), and Emily Whitney (event coordination).
- Property Acquisition & Growth Strategy – Explore purchasing adjacent properties and a part-time home for Justin & Adriana to strengthen family collaboration and business expansion.
- Permaculture Design Workshops & Thought Leadership Partnerships – Reach out to Noah Sanders, Paul from Homesteading Family, and explore a farm visit from Justin Rhodes or similar permaculture influencers.
- Consulting with Shannon Jenkins – Given his extensive experience in managing and coaching staff, he could provide valuable mentorship and insight into team leadership and scaling this vision.
- Exploring the Farm as a Healing & Wellness Retreat Center – Integrating mind-body-spirit healing practices, with potential partnerships with wellness practitioners, addiction recovery programs, and spiritual retreats.
- Outsourcing Housework to Free Up Children's Creativity & Contribution – Evaluating whether delegating household tasks could allow the children to pursue skills that align with their strengths and contribute to the farm's success.
- Trail-Facing Kiosk & Farm Shop (10x12 Building) – Serve as a welcoming node for Ladiga Trail users and a gateway to the broader farm vision.

**Core Vision & Purpose**

Key pillars: Event Hosting (weddings, floral workshops, music performances, retreats). Agritourism & Permaculture (regenerative farming, farm stays, eco-tourism). Spiritual & Community Engagement (Fresh Expressions gatherings, contemplative spirituality, faith-based retreats). Healing & Wellness Integration (holistic wellness retreats, chronic pain healing, community-centered recovery). Technology & AI Integration (farm automation, digital storytelling, business scalability).

**The Trail Porch (Kiosk/Farm Shop)**

The kiosk and 10x12 shop serve as a literal and symbolic front porch to the larger vision. Roles: Welcome Center, Mini Market, Spiritual Touchpoint ("a place for pause, prayer, meditation, or a journaling prompt"), Kids' Engagement ("managed in part by the children as a real-world business incubator"), Soft Launch Tool.
