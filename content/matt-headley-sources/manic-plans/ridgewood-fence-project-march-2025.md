# Ridgewood Farm Fence Installation Project Plan
*Source artifact — Notion, late March 2025*
*Cross-reference: `manic-episode.md`, `../../journal-drafts/gold-star-draft-notes.md`*

---

A 2,900-foot, 4-strand electric perimeter fence. Spec'd to the T-post. Materials list, installation schedule across three days, energizer model (Gallagher M800), voltage targets, YouTube content plan for the installation. The episode in project-management mode.

Saved here as evidence of scale and detail, not for direct use in writing.

**Key specs:** 2,900 ft perimeter, 116 T-posts, 4 gates, 11,500 ft of high-tensile wire, 464+ insulators, 3-day installation window (March 31–April 2). Goal voltage: 5,000V+.

**Content plan embedded in the project plan:** Long-form YouTube video ("Why We're Installing an Electric Perimeter Fence"), Reels/TikToks ("How We Keep Deer OUT of Our Crops," "The Truth About Trespassing Near the LaDaga Trail"). The fence installation was also a content opportunity.
