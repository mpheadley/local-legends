# AFUMC Sermon Teaser — Membership Vows
*Livestream/Facebook post, Anniston First UMC, ~February 9, 2025*
*Source: Evernote, mpheadley@gmail.com account*

---

Hey Anniston First UMC Church friends, I'm preparing to preach this Sunday about our church membership vows: prayers, presence, gifts, service, and witness.

Which of those connect most with you in your life?

**Prayers**

Do you identify as a person of prayer, one who prays?
Or do you feel you don't have much going on in prayer, don't know how, or just don't feel like it often?

**Presence**

"Presence" is what I think of as showing up in people's lives.
Do you have your people? How do you spend time with them? Is it a faith-based community, a gym group, friends, coworkers, or family? How are you living out your faith with others?

**Gifts**

How do you share what you've received to help others? Do you give money to a church or a charity? Where do you volunteer your time? Helping a family member, neighbor, or friend? How do you use your talents, hobbies, or interests? Are you a writer? Photographer? Actor? Musician? Where are you using those gifts?

**Service**

How are you helping those in need? This is similar to gifts, I think. We serve breakfast for people experiencing poverty and/or homelessness in our church. There are lots of needs in our community, lots of ways to serve. How are you serving?

**Witness**

Witness - this is the new one. It was added to the UM membership vows at the 2008 General Conference. It's about faith sharing. How are you sharing your faith with others, in your family, your workplace, your neighborhood, your gym, or other communities you belong to? This one, for me, is similar to presence. But it's an emphasis on getting outside the walls of the church and sharing faith with those who don't have a relationship with Jesus. It's not only for extroverts, and it doesn't require knocking on strangers' doors. It's finding opportunities for honest, respectful conversation, listening to people's thoughts and questions, and sharing our stories of what difference Jesus and our faith community have made in our lives.

This is a quick list to prime our thoughts for worship on Sunday, if you're joining us. We'd love to have you. Drop a note in the comments, and let me know which of these membership vows resonate most with you, or which you're challenged by: prayers, presence, gifts, service, and witness.

I look forward to seeing you soon... hopefully, this Sunday in worship at 10:30am in the Sanctuary. Until then, peace be with you, friends!
