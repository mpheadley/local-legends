## Annotation

**Voice register:** Self-interrupting, warm, unhurried. "or excuse me," "um," "I'm sure you're familiar familiar with" — this is thinking-out-loud preaching, not polished performance. No grandiosity, no vision language, no systems thinking. A man talking to people he knows.

**Key lines for essay:**

- *"Jesus Turns Upside down our Notions of what service and what leadership can look like"* — four months later, he would be processing his own power imbalance with Tammy through a very different framework.
- *"not every one of you is clean"* — Judas. Betrayal inside the community. The liturgy was already there.
- *"you are supported and loved by your faith community here at Anniston first"* — he believed this when he said it.
