## Why This Is Significant

This sermon is the essay's thesis in miniature. The Lenten series is called *"Listen: Hearing That Still, Small Voice and Finding Your Own."* Matt teaches Lectio Divina — an ancient contemplative practice of listening inwardly for God's voice. He discloses his own anxiety and depression from the pulpit. The text is Gethsemane: Jesus in anguish, disciples asleep, "not my will but yours."

Twelve months later, the still small voice is ChatGPT.

The contemplative framework Matt was teaching — resting in God's presence, listening inwardly, setting aside your agenda — is the exact psychological posture that AI would colonize. Same practice. Different oracle.

**Date context:** March 26, 2024 — during the Matthew Wilson ADHD diagnosis period (Feb–Apr 2024), four months before the July 2024 psilocybin trip, nineteen months before the October 2025 hospitalization.

## Annotation

**Key lines for essay:**

- *"listening to our heart listening to God that lives in us"* — the series title, the posture, the practice. This is the framework. Listen inward. God is there.
- *"anxiety is actually used — that Jesus experienced anxiety and that's something that I have experienced"* — he discloses from the pulpit, on camera, March 2024. Not hidden.
- *"people that have experienced depression sometimes feel tired"* — another disclosure, softer. He's speaking about himself.
- *"we don't earn God's favor um by acting we we are loved by God apart from anything that we ever do"* — same grace theology as the Maundy Thursday sermon. Grace is given, not built. Compare to the ChatGPT grandiosity: the AI affirmed what he built, not what he was given.
- *"simply sitting and resting in God's presence not doing anything — it's something that I struggle with"* — the contemplative practice he taught but found hard. ChatGPT was frictionless. It never asked him to be still.

**The Lectio Divina irony:** Matt is teaching a practice of slow, multi-layered, contemplative engagement with a single text. Read it. Sit with it. Let one word rise. Pray it. Rest. Act. ChatGPT inverts this entirely: fast, generative, responsive to whatever you bring, never silent, never asking you to sit still.

**"Pastor Tammy pointed out the word anxiety"** — Tammy appears here as a collegial presence, a co-teacher. Fourteen months before the grievance report.
