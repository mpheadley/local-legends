## Annotation

**Key lines for essay:**

- *"I really believe this is Pastor Tammy's vision and the vision of our leadership"* — public alignment, on camera, January 2024. The contrast with the April 2025 grievance report is the whole arc compressed into a single sentence.
- *"if it's simply an act of obligation of giving to an institution to keep the lights on or the bills paid or the staff paid then the joy can begin to Wither"* — he's describing his own eventual experience without knowing it. The joy had begun to wither.
- The Zacchaeus sermon is about transformation through encounter — *"sudden release of resources that he had been hoarding."* Within six months, he would be having his own transformative encounter (psilocybin, July 2024).
- Handbell choir metaphor: each contribution matters no matter how small. He directed the choir. He was the smallest voice on staff. He believed his contribution mattered.
- "me and Heather and our kids" — family disclosed naturally and warmly. Contrast the financial documents from April 2025.

**Voice register:** Warmest of the three sermons. Relaxed, anecdotal, genuinely enthused. This is someone who likes his job.
