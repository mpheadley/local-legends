## Notes

- **"After a successful initial campaign"** — there was an earlier GoFundMe. This is an update/expansion. The initial campaign was likely from the depression/recovery period (fall 2024 or earlier). The updated campaign was being drafted March 19, the day after the gold star visit.

- **March 19 context:** This was drafted in the same ChatGPT thread as the Facebook announcement and the exit interview response. Healer (gold star), victim (SPRC letter), entrepreneur (GoFundMe) — all three tracks running simultaneously.
- **"Wholesome Family Homestead"** does not appear here — by this point he was using "Headley Flower Farm" branding for the campaign.
