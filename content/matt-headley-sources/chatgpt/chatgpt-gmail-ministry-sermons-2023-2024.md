## The Roman Coin Sermon — Found

**October 14, 2023** — "Sermon: Two Sides of the Coin"

This is the sermon Matt remembered. Text: **Matthew 22:15-22** (Caesar's coin / render unto Caesar).

**Focus:** As parents and disciples, Jesus invites us to see beyond our evil motives (fear and insecurity) and to recognize within ourselves and others God's divine image.

**Function:** To raise consciousness of our motives and our identity as "eikons" (image bearers of God).

**Format:** Lowry Loop (homiletical plot)

**Central image:** A coin with two sides — one side = our fear and insecurity; the other = the image of God (eikon) within us. The Pharisees were so focused on trapping Jesus they couldn't see the divine truth. We become so obsessed with our fears we can't see the divine image in ourselves or others.

**Personal story woven in:** Matt's experience as a parent navigating children's screen time — authentic, specific, humorous.

**Humor angle developed:** "They say the average kid spends 7 hours on screens daily. I thought, 'Great! Only 7 hours? Mine are way ahead of the curve!'"

**Dialogue developed:** Inner voice wrestling between "I'm failing as a parent" and "Remember the divine image in them. Maybe they're exploring, learning, connecting in ways we didn't have."

Matt also asked about incorporating active congregation response, then requested "a prompt for a new GPT conversation that would help me construct a new sermon with a new topic and new scripture in a similar format" — indicating this was part of a regular sermon-prep workflow with ChatGPT.

**Location in archive:** No audio/transcript found on Samsung T5. This sermon was preached at Anniston First (Oct 2023 date) but no recording appears to have been uploaded to the YouTube channel or archived.
