## Notes for the AI psychosis piece

The AI kept saying "here's how to enhance your plan" and generating another version. This is the architecture of mania: iterative, perfectionist, sleepless, generative.

**"Church Responsibilities: Engaging in open discussions with Tammy about scaling back church hours."** The March 7 plan already includes scaling back from AFUMC as a near-term action — six days before the resignation. ChatGPT was modeling the resignation trajectory while framing it as collaborative. "Scaling back" became "effective immediately" within a week.

**The custom instructions evolution.** From March 7 to March 11, the custom instructions shifted:
- March 7: "Strategic & Proactive," "Monetization-Driven," multiple businesses (Remodel City Media, Wedding & Events Initiative, AI & Spirituality Integration)
- March 11: Added "Mindful & Sustainable Success — Remind me that slower, intentional work is often more effective" — which suggests Matt was beginning to notice the pace and trying to build in a corrective. But the instruction was given to ChatGPT, not to himself.

**The talking points for the staff meeting.** ChatGPT pre-scripted Matt's lines for Tuesday's staff meeting — including how to handle being interrupted, what to say when Tammy "hijacks" the conversation, and specific pivot lines. This is significant: Matt was no longer improvising human interactions. He was entering rooms with AI-generated scripts for how to navigate his relationships.

**"Remodel City Media" and the wedding LLC.** The March 7 custom instructions mention business entities — Remodel City Media (digital storytelling), a "Wedding & Events Initiative" (exploring an LLC with Katie & Emmalie from AFUMC staff). These are manic-era businesses that did not materialize. Catalogued here because they show the scope of parallel planning happening at 2 AM.

**"Supporting Heather's recovery."** Both March 7 documents reference Heather's recovery. This context — whatever Heather was recovering from at that time — shaped the household dynamic during the episode. The essay should sit with that reference rather than explain it away.

**The memory backup loop.** Both custom instruction documents include: "Memory Backup Plan: If I reset memory, remind me to paste my saved Evernote summary to restore context."
