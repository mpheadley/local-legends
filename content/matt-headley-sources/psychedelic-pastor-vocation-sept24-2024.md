## Notes for the AI psychosis piece

**September 24, 2024. This is where the book project begins.** Not February 12, 2025 ("The AI-Guided Christian Mystic"). Here. Same 6–7 AM hour, same brainstorm-to-title arc, same structure: a transformative experience → a book and a platform → "there is a VACUUM to fill." Five months before the AI Mystic titles, Matt was generating the Psychedelic Pastor titles. The medium changed; the pattern didn't.

**"Could my brokenness be my new vocation?"** The question that runs through the entire archive. It's the question underneath the "Transmogrified by the Enemy" essay (January 2025). It's the question underneath the AI psychosis piece itself. It's the question Matt was already asking in September 2024, before the manic episode, before the second hospitalization. The essay is the attempt to answer it honestly rather than prematurely.

**"Thomas Merton, Richard Rohr, Donald Trump all were present for my mushroom trip."** This line needs to be in the essay. It's specific, strange, funny, and true — the kind of detail that can only be written about honestly from enough distance. Three men whose images apparently came up during a psychedelic experience, two of whom are his primary theological influences, one of whom is the 45th president. The combination is the piece.

**"GPT - steps to write a book."** The earliest documented use of ChatGPT in the primary source archive. September 24, 2024 — five months before the AI Council, eight months before the manic episode's acceleration. ChatGPT was present from the beginning of the vocation discernment, not just during the mania. This is significant for the essay's timeline.

**"get conservative & liberal legal counsel: Brian Howell, Will Clay, Will Broom"** He already knew this was a legal and professional risk. September 24, 2024. He was thinking about what it would cost — legal exposure as a pastor in Alabama publicly discussing a Schedule I substance. "tell Tammy about what I can tell her" — already managing the Tammy relationship around this material, nine months before the March 14 resignation.

**The ChatGPT link.** `https://chatgpt.com/share/66f2a4d3-3b68-8010-9802-2d76599ed1c5` — a shared ChatGPT conversation from September 24, 2024. This may be retrievable. If it is, it would be the earliest documented ChatGPT session in the archive — predating even the February 2025 material significantly. **Retrieve if possible before writing the piece.**

**The platform brainstorm.** TikTok, Facebook livestream, blog, book, newspaper article, Ligare — the same proliferation of platforms and outlets documented on February 12, 2025 with the AI Mystic titles. Same energy, same structure: one idea, nine distribution channels. The manic episode didn't create this pattern; it amplified it.
