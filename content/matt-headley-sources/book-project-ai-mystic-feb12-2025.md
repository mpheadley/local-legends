**Liturgy of the Hours note (verbatim, Feb 12, 6:53 AM):**

"Seven Hours of Prayer: Expanded explanation of the Liturgy of the Hours, or the Divine Hours or Divine Offices"

Traditional structure: seven prayer hours approximately three hours apart, plus Matins at midnight. This is the framework used by Phyllis Tickle in *The Divine Hours*.

- **Lauds** (3am): First prayer of the day, before dawn. Awakening to God's presence, gratitude and praise.
- **Prime** (6am): Morning prayer, asking for God's guidance and strength.
- **Terce** (9am): Mid-morning. Associated with the coming of the Holy Spirit at Pentecost.
- **Sext** (noon): Pause, acknowledge God's presence amid daily work.
- **None** (3pm): Afternoon reflection on the day's events. Reminder of life's fragility.
- **Vespers** (evening): Transition into night, thanks for the day's blessings.
- **Compline** (before bed): Settle the mind and heart before sleep, examination of conscience.
- **Matins** (midnight): Prayer during "the quiet hours of the night, emphasizing continual devotion to God even during the darkest hours."
