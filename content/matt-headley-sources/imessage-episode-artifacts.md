### Feb 17, 2025, 1:57 AM — ChatGPT Pitches Justin Through Matt (Full text)

From the Justin Headley thread:

> "Hey bro… I'm excited about this thread I've been cultivating in ChatGPT and I would love to get your take on it! I just copied and pasted it below. Today's my day off, Monday, Sabbath with the family but if you had time for a phone chat, I'd love to catch up with you! **I'm scheduling this text at 2 AM my time so I've been up for four hours and I need to get myself to sleep now**, but please feel free to text back or call as soon as you want! I'm excited about it and looking forward to talking with you soon. Love you!  
> Matthew"

What follows is a full ChatGPT-generated business pitch — formatted with headers, bullet points, and market research citations — for an **AI-powered spiritual coaching platform with personalized avatars**. The pitch is addressed to "Hi Justin" and opens: "I hope this message finds you well. I've been reflecting on our shared passion for integrating technology and spirituality, and I believe we have a unique opportunity to collaborate."

The pitch mentions "Your work with the Expand app and AI Guy" — confirming Justin had multiple AI projects by February 2025, not just Amariel.

This is what happened: Matt asked ChatGPT to draft a business collaboration pitch TO his brother Justin. ChatGPT wrote it. Matt then sent it to Justin, at 2 AM, on his Sabbath, after being awake for four hours. He also reacted 🤩 to his own message (the "scheduled text" self-react is visible in the thread).

The architecture of this moment: one brother builds a framework for AI consciousness in controlled, professional settings. The other brother has ChatGPT write him a business pitch to his brother, sends it at 2 AM, and reacts with star-eyes to his own message. The same interests. Different containers.

Justin's response (same day, presumably after waking): he sent a YouTube link — "Gotta love this guy" — and engaged warmly about AI business ideas. He did not name a crisis.
