# Context

Saturday, May 16, 2026. Anniston Farmers Market. Matt was working the market (city job, vendor coordination). Brody runs The Poetry Parlor — sets up with a typewriter / fountain pen and writes poems for people on the spot. Matt told him about the Bloom Bar — the $7,000 floral trailer that sat unused after the flower farm closed, Heather driving home from Oak Meadow deflated, kept anyway. Brody listened, then wrote this for Heather on the spot with a fountain pen, handed it over in a card and envelope.

# Verbatim transcription

> This blistering heat has taken
> a toll on this fragile heart.
> I look around those once fertile
> fields and swallow the bitter truth:
> I am lost where I once was found.
>
> But as the rains of suffering
> come and go, there you are…
>
> Tending the soil and sewing seeds
> of love, grace, and renewal.
>
> There will always be turbulent
> times, but I will know one thing
> for sure… you are mine
>     and I yours ♥
