# Called Coffee — Jerod Snider Interview Prep

## Hybrid List (recommended for first sit-down)
1. **The History:** How did Called Coffee get started?
2. **The Mission:** What was the "gap" in town you wanted to fill?
3. **The Misconception:** What do people usually misunderstand about what you do here?
4. **The Grit:** What's been harder about running this than you expected?
5. **The Heart:** You checked on me when things were falling apart — where does that instinct to care for the neighborhood come from?
6. **The Invitation:** What should people try first when they visit?

## Alternative (StoryBrand-flavored)
1. "Jerod, coffee is the product, but connection seems to be the mission. When you were dreaming up Called Coffee, was there a specific moment you realized our town needed a place like this?"
2. "I've personally felt how much you care about the people who walk through these doors. Where does that instinct to check on the neighbors come from in your own life?"
3. "What's a side of small-business ownership in Calhoun County that people don't see — the part that requires the most grit?"
4. "When people leave Called Coffee, what's the one feeling or thought you hope they carry out the door with them?"
