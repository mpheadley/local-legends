## Fall Season (September – December)

### Oxford Arts Alliance Fall Market
**Date:** October (confirm with [Oxford Arts Alliance](https://oxfordartsalliance.org))  
**Location:** Oxford, Calhoun County  
**Audience:** Community, families, arts buyers  
The Oxford Arts Alliance puts on one of the more curated vendor events in the area. Juried for quality — expect the buyer to be selective, not a flea market crowd.  
**Booth:** Apply early. Selective juried process. Worth it for handmade/art vendors.

---

### The Aisle Bridal Expo — Anniston
**Date:** October 18, 2026  
**Location:** Anniston Museum of Natural History, Anniston  
**Audience:** Engaged couples + wedding vendors  
**Website:** [theaisle.app](https://theaisle.app)  
A curated bridal expo at the Anniston Museum — the only one of its kind in Calhoun County. Not a convention center floor show. 13 AMAG board members comped, 350 guests capacity. Vendor booths available.  
**Booth:** Vendor application at theaisle.app. Relevant for: florists, photographers, stationers, cake artists, caterers, jewelry.

---

### Anniston Art & Jazz Festival
**Date:** Typically October (confirm with City of Anniston)  
**Location:** Downtown Anniston  
**Audience:** Community, arts buyers, families  
Annual outdoor arts and music festival in downtown Anniston. Vendor booths in the mix — street fair format. Good exposure for local makers.  
**Booth:** Check with Anniston city events or Anniston Arts.

---

### Noble Street Festival
**Date:** Fall (confirm with City of Anniston events calendar)  
**Location:** Noble Street, Anniston  
**Audience:** Community, cyclists (if Gran Fondo timing aligns), families  
Noble Street has been repositioning as a destination corridor. The festival draws foot traffic and community energy.  
**Booth:** Contact city events.

---

### Gadsden Christmas City Craft Fair
**Date:** November – December  
**Location:** Gadsden, Etowah County  
**Audience:** Holiday shoppers, families  
The Gadsden area runs holiday craft fairs through November and December. One of the higher-volume markets in the region for holiday-season vendors.  
**Booth:** Multiple events — check Gadsden Downtown Development Authority and local Facebook event groups for dates and vendor applications.

---

### Calhoun County Farmers Market — Fall Markets
**Date:** Saturdays through fall season  
**Location:** Anniston/Oxford area (confirm current location)  
**Audience:** Local shoppers, regulars, community  
The farmers market runs through the growing season. Some markets include craft/maker vendor slots alongside produce and prepared foods.  
**Booth:** Contact market organizers directly for vendor availability.

---

## Spring Season (March – May)

### Chief Ladiga Trail Days
**Date:** Spring (coordinate with trail events — check chiefladigatrail.org)  
**Location:** Along Chief Ladiga Trail corridor (Cleburne County to Oxford)  
**Audience:** Trail users, cyclists, families  
Community events along the trail corridor. Good for vendors with outdoor, trail, or heritage products.

---

### Oxford Spring Arts Crawl
**Date:** April – May (confirm with Oxford Arts Alliance)  
**Location:** Oxford, Calhoun County  
**Audience:** Arts buyers, community  
Spring counterpart to the fall market. Smaller event, more intimate venue.

---

### Anniston Museum of Natural History Events
**Date:** Various — check [annistonmuseum.org](https://www.annistonmuseum.org)  
**Location:** Anniston  
**Audience:** Families, members, community  
The museum runs events throughout the year that sometimes include vendor space — particularly for heritage, art, and nature-themed products. Worth checking their events calendar.

---

## How to Find More

**Facebook is the real calendar for NE Alabama craft fairs.** Most events are promoted in local group pages: "Anniston/Calhoun County Events," "Gadsden Area Events," Northeast Alabama Makers groups. Event organizers post there first.

**FestivalNet and TheCraftMap** list Alabama craft fairs with booth costs and application deadlines. The average Alabama booth fee runs around $207/event.

**Average NE Alabama craft fair attendance:** 200–800 depending on the event and season. The Aisle (Oct 18) and Coldwater Trail Race (Oct 30-Nov 1) are the two highest-draw events in Calhoun County this fall season.
