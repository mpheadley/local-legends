# The Best Church in Calhoun County

## How we made this list (the honest part)
Most "best of" lists are one person's opinion dressed up as fact. This one isn't. We started with **291,691 Alabama businesses** from the Foursquare Open Source Places dataset, filtered to **church** in **Calhoun County**, and kept the 15 that are actually established — real address, real phone, a working web presence where we could confirm one. No pay-to-play, no ranking anybody who bought an ad. If a spot is missing, it's because the data didn't have it yet — tell us and we'll add it.
