## Trail Running

### Woodstock 5K
**Date:** August 2, 2026  
**Location:** Ohatchee, Calhoun County  
**Distance:** 5K  
**Category:** Road/trail 5K  
A community race in the Ohatchee area. Good for local runners warming up for fall race season.  
**Vendor opportunity:** No booth. Community turnout, local audience.

---

### Coldwater Trail Race — Life Time
**Date:** October 30 – November 1, 2026  
**Location:** Coldwater Mountain, Anniston  
**Distances:** 25 miles (MTB + trail run) · 50 miles (MTB + trail run)  
**Website:** [coldwatertrailrace.org](https://coldwatertrailrace.org)  
The anchor race of NE Alabama's trail scene. The 2025 inaugural drew 600+ athletes from 34 states and Canada. Life Time acquired it, which means real marketing reach and national registration. One of the few Life Time events in the Southeast. Expect it to grow every year.  
**Vendor opportunity:** Yes — this is the booth. CLT and Coldwater Mountain designs. Highest-income athletic audience in the Anniston area. Coordinate with event organizers for vendor placement.

---

### Pinhoti 100
**Date:** First weekend of November (typically Nov 1–2, 2026)  
**Location:** Lineville/Heflin, Cleburne County (start near Coleman Lake)  
**Distance:** 100 miles on the Pinhoti Trail  
**Website:** [pinhoti100.com](https://pinhoti100.com)  
One of the premier ultramarathons in the Southeast. The Pinhoti National Recreation Trail runs 335 miles through the Talladega National Forest; the 100-mile race follows its southern Alabama section. A serious event with serious runners — and a volunteer culture that keeps people coming back.  
**Vendor opportunity:** Limited — remote location, aid-station culture. Worth a presence if you have Pinhoti-specific designs.

---

### Chief Ladiga Trail Races
**Date:** Various — check [chiefladigatrail.org](https://chiefladigatrail.org)  
**Location:** Cleburne County to Oxford (trail runs the Chief Ladiga Trail)  
**Distances:** 5K, 10K, half marathon  
The Chief Ladiga Trail connects Cleburne County through Jacksonville to Oxford — 33 miles of paved rail-trail through the Calhoun/Cleburne County line. Race events on the trail draw regional participants.  
**Vendor opportunity:** Yes — CLT shirt designs. Family-friendly audience. Check the event calendar for dates.

---

### JSU Homecoming 5K
**Date:** November 7, 2026  
**Location:** Jacksonville State University, Jacksonville  
**Distance:** 5K  
**Category:** Campus fun run  
The JSU Homecoming weekend race. Campus crowd, alumni, families. Friendly, community-oriented event.  
**Vendor opportunity:** Minimal. Campus event, vendor space limited.

---

### Oxford Kiwanis Turkey Trot
**Date:** Thanksgiving morning (typically Nov 27, 2026)  
**Location:** Oxford, Calhoun County  
**Distance:** 5K  
Annual community Turkey Trot. Local families, first-timers, repeat regulars. One of the most consistent community race events in Calhoun County.  
**Vendor opportunity:** Small — booth presence possible if you know the organizers. Local audience, not athletic specialty.

## Mountain Biking

### NEABA Coldwater Mountain Races
**Date:** Various throughout the season — check [neaba.org](https://www.neaba.org)  
**Location:** Coldwater Mountain, Anniston  
The Northeast Alabama Bicycle Association organizes trail days, races, and events at Coldwater Mountain through the year. The trail system at Coldwater is one of the best in Alabama — IMBA Ride Center level trail infrastructure.  
**Vendor opportunity:** Yes — committed MTB audience, Coldwater Mountain designs.

## Road Cycling

### Noble Street Gran Fondo
**Date:** TBD 2026 (confirm at annistonal.gov/events or Noble Street Festival)  
**Location:** Anniston, starting at Noble Street  
**Category:** Gran Fondo — road cycling, Grand Fondo global qualifier series  
If you're into road cycling, this is the one to know about. Noble Street is a Grand Fondo global qualifier — meaning finishers earn qualification for the Gran Fondo World Championships. That brings serious cyclists from outside the region. High income, passionate audience, and the kind of people who buy gear.  
**Vendor opportunity:** Yes — road cycling audience, CLT and Anniston heritage designs. Confirm booth availability through the city events office.

---

### Cheaha Challenge
**Date:** Spring (typically March, confirm at [cheahachallenge.com](https://cheahachallenge.com))  
**Location:** Cheaha State Park, Clay County (Anniston area)  
**Category:** Mountain road cycling  
One of the most challenging road cycling events in the Southeast — climbs into Cheaha State Park, which sits at the highest point in Alabama. Serious cyclists, beautiful terrain, national draw.  
**Vendor opportunity:** Yes — serious athletic crowd, regional draw. Cheaha and Alabama heritage designs.

## How to Use This List

This list is updated quarterly. If you know of an event we missed, [let us know](mailto:matt@southernlegends.blog).

**For vendors:** The Coldwater Trail Race (October) and Noble Street Gran Fondo are the highest-yield events for outdoor/adventure merchandise. Both draw athletes from outside the region who are shopping. The Pinhoti 100 has a devoted community but requires advance coordination.

**For participants:** The Coldwater Trail Race inaugural showed what's possible here. Life Time doesn't pick ordinary venues. NE Alabama's trail infrastructure — Coldwater, Pinhoti, Chief Ladiga — is genuinely world-class.
