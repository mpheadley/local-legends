Andy Curtis handed me his pulpit on a Sunday when I was not sure I had any business standing in one.

That is either reckless or faithful. I have not decided which. I think Andy has, and he landed on faithful.

I preached on John 12:24 that morning. The grain of wheat that falls into the earth and dies. I held up a handful of actual wheat berries before I said a word. The text I had been living for months and only recently could name.

Andy heard that sermon and kept calling me.

That is the thing about Andy Curtis. He is not afraid of the complicated ones.

---

Jacksonville First United Methodist Church is not the largest church in Jacksonville, Alabama. What it is, at least in my experience of it, is a room that opens when it could close. A pulpit that goes to people Andy trusts, even when trust is the harder bet.

Tuesday mornings, we run morning prayer there. Six-thirty. The room is small. The coffee is real. Jason Wright comes. So do others who are still sorting out what they believe or whether the church is worth their time.

Andy keeps the lights on for that too.
