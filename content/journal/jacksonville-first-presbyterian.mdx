Jacksonville First Presbyterian Church sits on its corner in Jacksonville, Alabama, and has been there longer than most of the people who know it.

It is a Presbyterian congregation in a town built around a Methodist university — an interesting position to occupy. They have occupied it with steadiness.

---

The music at Jax FPC is real. Blair Callaway and Susie Dempsey share the organ bench, alternating weeks, each one covering when the other can't. Between them they have held that room through a long season of Sunday mornings. That kind of continuity is not accidental. It is a decision, renewed on every rotation, by two people who care about the work.

Thrive worship meets there Wednesday evenings. It is a different kind of service — less formal, room for something else to happen. Matt Headley plays guitar there now on Wednesday nights. The 9am traditional service at Jacksonville First UMC is another commitment, another room, another morning.

Tuesday morning prayer has been at Jacksonville First United Methodist Church — Jason Wright comes, Andy Curtis holds the light on. Wednesday evening Thrive worship is here, at FPC.

The buildings are a few blocks apart. The community that moves between them is smaller than the distance suggests.
