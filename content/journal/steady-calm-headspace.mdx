Calm has forty million users and a billion-dollar valuation.

Headspace has evidence. Real clinical trials. Real research partnerships. It is one of the more honest wellness companies in the space because it actually studies whether its product does what it says.

I use neither of them.

---

Let me tell you why, and then let me tell you what they are actually good for, because this is not a takedown of two companies that have helped a lot of people. It is a description of what they are not built for, and what I built Steady for instead.

Both apps are designed for the middle. The person who is functional, managing something difficult, looking for daily support that helps them stay regulated. They are subscriptions, which means they work best for people who have already decided they have a practice and want to maintain it. Calm is mostly sleep and relaxation content. Headspace is meditation. Both are designed on the assumption that if you are in crisis, you will call someone else.

Neither of them knows what county you live in.

---

This is not a trivial point.

When I was looking for mental health resources in Calhoun County after coming home from UAB, I was not looking for a sleep meditation. I was looking for a therapist who was accepting new patients, took Medicaid, and could actually see someone who lived forty-five minutes outside of Birmingham.

Calm does not know whether there is a therapist in Oxford, Alabama who has an opening on Thursday.

Headspace does not know that the peer support group that meets on Tuesday nights in Anniston exists, or where it meets, or whether it still meets.

That is not their job. That is my job. That is what I built Steady for.

---

Here is what Calm and Headspace are for.

After. When the crisis is managed, when you have found the therapist, when you are trying to build a daily practice that helps you stay level. When you want something to do with the 6am before the world starts asking things of you. When you can afford seventy dollars a year and you want to use it.

They are good at that. I am not trying to take that from them.

But the person at two in the morning who does not know what to do next is not who they are building for. That person needs a phone number that works. That person needs to know who will answer in Calhoun County.

---

Calhoun County has more than you think. It has less than it needs.

The list is at [gatherstudio.app/steady](https://gatherstudio.app/steady).

If you want something to do with your 6am after you have found the support you need, Calm and Headspace are real options. I am not telling you not to use them. I am telling you that the directory I built solves a different problem, and I want to be honest about what that problem is.
