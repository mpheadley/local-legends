## Honest read: don't pitch Del Marsh

Two reasons, both worth saying out loud:

1. **Off-ICP.** GS sells to local small businesses, weddings, churches, creatives, nonprofits. Marsh is a wealthy retired power-broker who sold his company to a national conglomerate. Nothing in the GS product ladder fits him.
2. **Optics.** Publishing an SL profile that features a bribery *lawsuit*, then cold-pitching the subject GS services, reads as leverage. That is the exact opposite of the Limping Guide positioning. Profiling-then-selling a litigious public figure is a bad trade — small maybe-revenue against real reputational risk to SL's editorial credibility.

**Recommendation: no sales pitch to Marsh. The profile stands on its own as editorial.**
