Kyle Bryan preached a sermon about going to the hospital. He called it a vacation with grippy socks.

[MATT: What year? What church? What was it about — depression, mania, something physical? How long was he in? What did the congregation do with it?]

The phrase did something. It is the kind of reframe that makes a thing survivable before you have to survive it. Not a euphemism. Not a denial. Just a different angle on the same room: the place they send you when you are out of options, and you have to wear the socks with the rubber on the bottom because they don't trust you with the other kind.

I heard that sermon from the outside. Then I needed it from the inside.
