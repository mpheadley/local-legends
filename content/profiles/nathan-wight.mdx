He is from Kermit, Texas. He is a baritone and a rancher.

That is a specific combination of identities, and it is not accidental that he leads with both. The voice and the land are not separate things for Nathan Wight. They are the same formation.

He got his bachelor's degrees in both Voice and Biology from Abilene Christian University — the science and the singing, kept together. His master's came from Rice University. His Doctor of Musical Arts in Performance came from Arizona State. Then he came to Jacksonville, Alabama in 2003 to run the vocal program at Jacksonville State University.

He has been there ever since.

---

## What He Built

When Dr. Nathan Wight arrived at JSU, there was no Jacksonville Opera Theatre.

He started it. He built it into a program that presents two to three major productions per year at the Oxford Performing Arts Center. Over 31 shows since 2004. *Little Women* in 2022. *Cinderella* in 2023. *Peter Pan* in 2018. He has staged Sondheim and Sondheim's predecessors, standard operatic repertoire, and the kind of musical theater that a regional university program puts before a community that might otherwise never see it live.

He is the Director of Vocal Activities. He runs opera for a campus in northeast Alabama, far from any major metropolitan opera company, building the case from scratch every season that the voice is worth the room you put it in.

In 2023, JSU recognized him for Outstanding Service — not the first time. The university recognized him for the same thing in 2013.

## The Anniston First UMC Concert

In November 2023, Dr. Wight performed in a concert at Anniston First United Methodist Church alongside JSU colleagues — a program of classical and popular music, open to the community.

He has performed in this county as well as taught in it. The building where [Martha Vandervoort](/profiles/martha-vandervoort) was memorialized, where [Dale Clem](/profiles/dale-clem) preached his last pastorate, where [Kyle Bryan](/profiles/kyle-bryan) runs the daily operations — that room has also held Nathan Wight's voice.

---

*Dr. Nathan Wight is Professor of Music, Director of Vocal Activities, and Director of the Jacksonville Opera Theatre at Jacksonville State University. He joined the faculty in 2003. He is a native of Kermit, Texas.*
