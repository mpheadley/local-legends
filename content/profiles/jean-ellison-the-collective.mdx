Jean Ellison co-founded The Music Box music school on Noble Street in Anniston and runs Mom-To-Go catering out of the same energy that drives everything else she does. She is a classically trained soprano, a jazz vocalist, a cookbook author, and a caterer who makes jerk chicken people request by name. The Collective is what happens when you gather all of that into one room. Jean is building what she calls an empire, and she means it without apology. She has earned the right to use the word. She came through a period of real difficulty — the ground gave out under her, more than once — and what she built on the other side is something that holds. The Music Box has been teaching voice, drums, guitar, and piano in Anniston for years. Mom-To-Go has been feeding people who want dinner to feel like love.
