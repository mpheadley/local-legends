Heather Headley started with flowers and kept going. Heather Florals and the Bloom Bar are her operations — floral design and a bar-style workshop concept that brings people in to make their own arrangements. She works weddings, events, and custom orders, and her style leans toward the organic: arrangements that look gathered from a garden rather than constructed from a cooler. The Bloom Bar model is worth noting. It's a format that makes floristry accessible — guests come in, pick their stems, and build something with their hands. It's become a popular option for bridal showers, girls' nights, and team events, and it fits the community-first ethic that Heather runs by. She is not trying to be the biggest shop in the county.
