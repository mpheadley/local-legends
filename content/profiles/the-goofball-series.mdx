Kathryn Parker writes the Goofball Series out of Jacksonville, Alabama. The books follow characters who are funny in the way real kids are funny — impulsive, a little chaotic, not always doing what they're supposed to. Parents who buy them tend to read them twice: once to the kid, and once to themselves, recognizing something. Parker is a local author doing the slow work of building a readership one book and one reading at a time. Children's books live and die on word of mouth, and the Goofball Series has found its audience in East Central Alabama families who want stories that don't talk down to kids.
