# Everyone Has Opinions About Your Wedding
### A Children's Book for People Who Are Getting Married and Are Very Tired

*by Matt Headley*

---

The flowers are chosen. The venue is booked.
The caterer's hired. The menu is cooked.
The table is set with the napkins just right.
Everyone, please, just let it be night.

---

Your mother has thoughts on the centerpiece height.
She saw something better on Pinterest last night.
She's not trying to meddle. She's trying to help.
She'll send you the link. She's already sent twelve.

---

The florist is lovely. She's doing her best.
She just needs to know — dusty rose or the rest?
Sage green? Champagne? Blush? A deeper blush?
Just pick one, my love. I'm begging. Please. Hush.

---

The caterer called. There's a change to the fish.
He wanted to offer an alternative dish.
Also, the chicken. Also, the bread.
Also, he's checking — are any guests dead?

*(He means dietary restrictions. He worded it wrong.
He's called back four times. This has gone on too long.)*

---

Your uncle has RSVPed. He said maybe.
He said probably yes. He said his wife's having a baby.
Wait — no. He said no. He said yes with a plus one.
He left a voicemail. He said it was done.

---

The photographer needs just one more candid shot.
Of you by the window. And you by the pot
of hydrangeas. And you looking off to the right.
Can you look like you love him? Can you look like the light?

---

The DJ is asking about your first dance.
He needs a backup. He needs an advance.
He just wants to know — is it *Unchained Melody*?
Because that one's been taken. He's booked through the century.

---

The guest list is final. Except for the Tates.
Your grandmother added them. Nobody debates
your grandmother. No one has ever said no.
There are now two more Tates. They don't know where to go.

---

It's midnight. The seating chart covers the bed.
You've moved the Hendersons eleven times in your head.
They cannot sit near the Bakers. You know why.
Nobody will say it. Everybody knows why.

---

The dress is exactly the dress that you wanted.
Your best friend said "stunning." Your mother said "haunted —
not haunted like sad, like *ethereal*, like wow."
She means it. She loves it. She's crying right now.

---

The day will be beautiful. All of them say it.
And they are not wrong. You will probably believe it
somewhere around 4pm, in the golden hour light,
when everyone's dancing and everything's right.

---

But right now it is 1am.
The napkins are wrong.
The florist is texting.
Your mother is calling.

Everyone has opinions about your wedding.

Everyone.

So many opinions.

**Would you all just please shut up.**

---

*The end.*

*(They will not shut up. The napkins are still wrong.
Congratulations. You're getting married.)*
