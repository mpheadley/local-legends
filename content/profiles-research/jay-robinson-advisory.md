## Who He Is

Jay Robinson has been Director of the JSU Wesley Foundation in Jacksonville, Alabama for more than a decade. Campus minister, United Methodist, trained in relational ministry. He runs Campfire — a reCreation fellowship built around adventure, renewal, and the outdoors. His tagline: **"Friendships are forged, not found."**

He is the reason Matt is an ordained United Methodist pastor. In 2014, after Matt returned to Alabama, Jay introduced him to District Superintendent Sherill Clontz. Matt was appointed to Weaver First UMC that June. Without that lunch, without Jay, the whole nineteen-year pastoral arc looks different. Maybe it doesn't happen.

## The Profile Angle

**This is a profile about a man who decided to stop counting.**

Jay Robinson spent decades in an events-based ministry model that measures attendance, recruits for programs, and runs on the anxiety of institutional survival. At some point he stopped. He built Campfire. He moved his metric from *how many came* to *how many friendships were forged*. He adopted a tagline — "Friendships are forged, not found" — that is almost anti-institutional, and he lives it inside an institution.

That tension is the profile. Jay is not a rebel who left. He stayed. He works inside the United Methodist system, inside a university campus ministry, inside a church-adjacent structure that mostly still runs on the consumer model. He just decided to measure something different. That is a stranger, harder, more interesting choice than leaving.

**The Matt connection is not incidental — it's structural.**

Jay is the reason Matt got ordained. Jay was there in December 2013 when Matt was figuring out whether to go back. Jay introduced him to Sherill Clontz in 2014. Jay coached Matt's church council in 2018. Jay had lunch with Matt in May 2026 — eleven weeks before the bipolar diagnosis upended everything — and said something that Matt is still applying to the expo, the software company, the marriage curriculum. That's twelve years. That's the whole arc. Jay is in the before, the middle, and the still-going.

The profile would need to hold both: Jay's story (the Campfire philosophy, the shift from counting to forging) and the Matt thread (what it means to be the person who shows up at the right moment, over and over, without knowing it matters).
