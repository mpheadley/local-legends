## Summary

1. **Content Split**
   - Personal journal essays (mental health, grief, faith) → Anniston Star + Calhoun Journal (both)

   - **Week 1:** Publish on Southern Legends (own platform first)

   ```

   He writes local business profiles and personal essays about mental health and faith 
   at Southern Legends. He runs Headley Web & SEO in Jacksonville, Alabama.
   ```

   - **Clickable link:** Southern Legends only
   - No second link; business name mentioned as text for SEO + credibility

4. **Distribution Plan**
   - Personal email to trusted circle (not just Facebook)
   - Facebook teasers + links (not full essays)
   - Email list (direct to interested readers)

5. **SEO & Authority Building**
   - Essays on Southern Legends → backlinks from Star + Journal → domain authority boost
   - Byline with Southern Legends link drives traffic back to hub
   - Business name mention (non-linked) aids local SEO + brand recognition
   - Cold outreach warmer later (prospects already know Matt's story)
