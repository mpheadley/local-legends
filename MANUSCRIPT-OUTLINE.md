## The 6 Connective Tissue Passages (Bridges)

These are what Matt asked for: the passages that hold the chapters together when they're assembled into a book. Each brief tells Matt what the bridge needs to do; Matt drafts the prose.

### B1 · The Unfinished Bow
**Sits between:** Ch 3 (Hope in the Wilderness) and Ch 4 (The Gold Star).
**Bridges:** The gap between "I made it through" and "I was already coming back into it." The 2024 essay ties a bow; the gold star scene undoes it. The reader needs to know, in the book's voice (April 2026 looking back), that the bow held for a year and then didn't.
**Must do:** Signal that the first essay was honest at the time *and* wrong. Don't retract it; re-contextualize it.
**Length:** ~300–500 words.
**Voice note:** Spare. Later-Matt looking at earlier-Matt without contempt.

### B2 · From Prophecy to Action
**Sits between:** Ch 6 (Defying Gravity) and Ch 7 (Ash Wednesday).
**Bridges:** Goosebumps in the living room → resigning from 19 years of ministry. How does receiving a song as prophecy escalate into leaving the pulpit?
**Must do:** Show the escalation without inventing scenes. Name that it didn't feel like escalation at the time. The aspirational kept feeling more achievable until action seemed like obedience.
**Length:** ~300–400 words.
**Voice note:** Close to present. Not apologizing. Not theorizing. Reporting.

### B3 · When the Aspirational Stopped Feeling Achievable
**Sits between:** Ch 8 (Scaling) and Ch 9 (Atlanta).
**Bridges:** The pivot. Thesis reversal in a single passage. Mania felt like recovery — until it didn't. The first moment the engine coughed.
**Must do:** Locate the moment, or acknowledge the moment is unlocatable. Either is honest.
**Length:** ~200–400 words.
**Voice note:** Do not use the word "crash." Find a truer verb.

### B4 · The Descent to the Floor
**Sits between:** Ch 10 (The Farm Sale) and Ch 11 (UAB).
**Bridges:** From frozen at a yard sale to checking into a psychiatric hospital. Weeks or days? Who called whom? Who made the call to go in? The reader cannot skip from selling plates at a yard sale to a locked ward without the interval.
**Must do:** Fill the interval. Even if it was blurry from inside, name that it was blurry.
**Length:** ~400–600 words.
**Voice note:** This is the hardest one. Write it last.

### B5 · Living Without the Roles
**Sits between:** Ch 11 (UAB) and Ch 12 (Ministry Grief).
**Bridges:** Discharged but not home. Camper at parents'. Heather at the museum, kids at her parents'. The identity vacuum — not pastor, not farmer, not husband-as-provider, not yet anything.
**Must do:** Hold the void without narrating a lesson from it. Description, not meaning.
**Length:** ~300–500 words.
**Voice note:** Quiet. Almost boring. Days that were days.

### B6 · Walking Into the Room
**Sits between:** Ch 14 (Attempt 73) and Ch 15 (I'm Not Going to Disappear).
**Bridges:** The gap between "I started to find it within me" (internal) and walking into Called Coffee (external). What got him to text the 50 friends. What got him in the car that morning.
**Must do:** Show the specific first external action. Not the decision — the action. One shoe, then the other.
**Length:** ~200–400 words.
**Voice note:** Concrete. No momentum language ("momentum," "coming back," "turning point" — kill on sight).
